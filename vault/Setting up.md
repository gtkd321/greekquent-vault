# Setting up

Two minutes, once. Back to [[Today]].

> [!tip]- 1. Turn on the Greekquent tools
> The first time you open the vault, Obsidian asks whether you trust it. Tap **Trust author and enable plugins**. If you missed it: Settings, Community plugins, **Turn on community plugins**.

> [!tip]- 2. The four tool buttons (Today, Look up, Ask, More)
> These are small icons, not the colored buttons on word notes. On a computer they sit in the thin strip of icons along the far left edge of the window. On a phone, tap the menu button (three lines) at the bottom right of the screen, when you are not typing in a note.
> - **Today** (house): today's story line, your words for today, comebacks, this week's lesson, and words to review. Kids: tap **Kid track** at the bottom of Today, or use the **Greekquent Kids** vault.
> - **Look up** (magnifying glass): any word in English or Greek. Words outside the 1,500 are translated right in the box. Tick Guess first to try before you see the answer.
> - **Ask** (two people, Intermediate and Advanced): a question about the note you are on, ready to share in the class GroupMe. You can also ask on video from there.
> - **More** (three dots): Word graph (Intermediate and up), Send homework, Share feedback, What's new, Update vault.

> [!tip]- Layouts: Simple, Intermediate, Advanced
> The small switch at the top of every page changes how much you see. **Simple** (where everyone starts) keeps Today and this week's lesson bright. **Intermediate** adds games, the word graph, and your colors. **Advanced** opens everything. Grayed items still open when you tap them.

> [!tip]- 3. Color your words
> Every word note has a row of buttons just under the meaning, lined up with the text: **Hear it**, **Word graph** (Intermediate and up), and three colors: **Don't have it** (red), **Kind of** (yellow), **Have it** (green). Tap one after you try to say the word from memory. Tap it again to clear it. Red words come back on the Today page.

> [!tip]- 4. Your own notes
> Type in the **My Work** folder: Homework, My Notes, and anything you add. Updates never change My Work. Other notes open read-only so they can't change by accident.

> [!tip]- 5. Weekly updates
> When Obsidian says an update is ready, tap the message. Or tap More, then Update vault. Only changed files download.
