# Kid variant · Lesson 4: Telescope spy

Play version of [[L04 Postcards and the cafe-teacher|Lesson 4: Postcards and the cafe]] · [[K04 Telescope spy-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** The kid spies on the room through a paper telescope and reports what he sees, practicing το / τον / τη.

**You'll need:** a paper-towel tube, tape, markers; a few toys placed around the room; a Greek postcard or photo

## How to play

1. Kid decorates the tube, then looks through it.
2. Teacher asks what he sees. He names one thing at a time, using the right article.
3. Finish with the postcard: what do you see in Greece?

## Lines

- T: **Τι βλέπεις;** — What do you see?
- K: **Βλέπω το τραπέζι.** — I see the table.
- K: **Βλέπω τον Spider-Man!** — I see Spider-Man!
- K: **Βλέπω τη γάτα.** — I see the cat.
- K: **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα και ένα σπίτι.** — I see the sea and a house.
- T: **Βλέπεις τον ήλιο;** — Do you see the sun?

## Make it

The telescope itself: stripes, stars, his name in Greek letters he knows so far.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Use real photos of Greek villages and describe them. A café card (Τι θέλετε; / Ένα νερό, παρακαλώ) can sit next to each photo.
