## Today's phrases

Make the gesture every time you say the phrase. It helps the phrase stick.

| Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| **Δεν έχω…** | I don't have… | Turn out empty pockets or open empty hands. |
| **Πού είναι το κινητό μου;** | Where is my phone? | Pat your pockets, looking around. |
| **Δεν βλέπω…** | I can't see… / I don't see… | Hand over your eyes, head turning. |
| **Εδώ είναι!** | Here it is! | Point down hard at the spot and smile. |
| **Γιατί δεν μπορείς;** | Why can't you? | Both palms up, head tilted to the side. |
| **Έχω δουλειά.** | I have work. / I'm busy. | Mime typing fast. |
| **Μπορώ να δω;** | May I see? | Point at your eye, then at the object, eyebrows up. |
| **Πόσο κάνει;** | How much is it? | Rub thumb and fingers together. |
| **Δύο ευρώ.** | Two euros. | Tap two fingers on the counter, one coin at a time. |
| **Δεν ξέρω.** | I don't know. | Shrug with palms up. |

## Main scene

**Where's my phone? and the corner shop**: Part 1: a lost phone. Part 2: shopping at a small shop.

**New words:** μισό, το λεπτό, αυτός, πέντε, καλός, η μέρα

- A: **Ωχ! Πού είναι το κινητό μου;** — Oh no! Where's my phone?
- B: **Δεν ξέρω. Δεν βλέπω το κινητό σου.** — I don't know. I don't see your phone.
- A: **Δεν έχω το κινητό μου! Και τα κλειδιά μου;** — I don't have my phone! And my keys?
- B: **Τα κλειδιά σου είναι εδώ!** — Your keys are here!
- A: **Ευχαριστώ! Αλλά το κινητό μου;** — Thanks! But my phone?
- B: **Είναι στην τσάντα σου;** — Is it in your bag?
- A: **Όχι, δεν είναι… Μισό λεπτό… Εδώ είναι!** — No, it isn't… Wait a second… Here it is!
- B: **Τέλεια! Μπορείς τώρα; Θέλω καφέ.** — Great! Can you come now? I want a coffee.
- A: **Όχι, δεν μπορώ. Έχω δουλειά.** — No, I can't. I'm busy with work.
- B: **Γιατί δεν μπορείς;** — Why can't you?
- A: **Γιατί δεν έχω ώρα!** — Because I don't have time!
- A: **Καλημέρα! Μπορώ να δω;** — Good morning! May I look?
- Seller: **Ναι, βέβαια.** — Yes, of course.
- A: **Πόσο κάνει;** — How much is it?
- Seller: **Δύο ευρώ.** — Two euros.
- A: **Και αυτό;** — And this one?
- Seller: **Πέντε ευρώ.** — Five euros.
- A: **Δεν θέλω αυτό. Θέλω το βιβλίο. Ορίστε δύο ευρώ.** — I don't want this one. I want the book. Here are two euros.
- Seller: **Ευχαριστώ! Καλή μέρα!** — Thank you! Have a good day!

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Lost item: το κινητό μου · τα κλειδιά μου · η τσάντα μου · το βιβλίο μου
> - Where: εδώ · στην τσάντα · στο τραπέζι · κάτω από το τραπέζι
> - Price: ένα ευρώ · δύο ευρώ · πέντε ευρώ
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα.**
>
> **Twists to try with a partner**
>
> - The shopkeeper says Δεν έχω… to everything, and the customer has to keep asking for something else.
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). When you're listening, just listen.

> [!example]- Build it with tiles
> Cut out your Lesson 5 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
>
> 1. Build it from the English, then read it aloud.
> 2. Turn it into a question: swap the . for ; and raise your voice at the end.
> 3. Make it negative: put δεν right before the verb.
> 4. Swap one tile for another word from the extra tiles.
> 5. Write your last version by hand in your notebook.
>
> - **A:** Your keys are here!
> - **B:** Is it in your bag?
> - **C:** Why can't you?
>
