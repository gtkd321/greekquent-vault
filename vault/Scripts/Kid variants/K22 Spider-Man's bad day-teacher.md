# Kid variant · Lesson 22: Spider-Man's bad day

Play version of [[L22 Yesterday I wanted-teacher|Lesson 22: Yesterday I wanted]] · [[K22 Spider-Man's bad day-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** A toy acts out yesterday's story in a three-panel comic, giving lots of practice with the anchor verbs in the past.

**You'll need:** the favorite toy again, paper folded into 3 panels

## How to play

1. Teacher tells a short story with the toy: it wanted something but couldn't.
2. Kid draws the three panels.
3. Kid retells it from the comic, then tells his own yesterday.

## Lines

- T: **Χθες ο Spider-Man ήθελε να φάει…** — Yesterday Spider-Man wanted to eat…
- T: **…αλλά δεν είχε ψωμί!** — …but he didn't have any bread!
- T: **Ήθελε να παίξει, αλλά δεν μπορούσε.** — He wanted to play, but he couldn't.
- K: **Χθες ήθελα να πάω στο πάρκο, αλλά δεν μπορούσα.** — Yesterday I wanted to go to the park, but I couldn't.
- K: **Έβλεπα τηλεόραση.** — I was watching TV.

## Make it

The three-panel comic.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Tell what you wanted to do yesterday and couldn't, using the four anchors in the past.
