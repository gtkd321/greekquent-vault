**Week of** Aug 30 to Sep 5 · **No class this week** · **Running total:** 1290 of 1,500

## This week's story

One line a day, same people, one scene. Read it from the top each day, then add today's line. On Sunday, read the whole thing aloud.

> [!quote]- The week in six lines
> 1. Είμαι περήφανη για σένα! — Σοφία, η βαλίτσα σου; Πού πας; (I'm proud of you! — Sofia, your suitcase? Where are you going?)

> 6. Χίλια μίλια απόσταση... — Αλλά πάντα με υποστήριξη! (A thousand miles of distance... — But always with support!)

## Group meet prompt

**News**

- **Τι νέα;** (What's new?)
- **Τι διάβασες στις ειδήσεις;** (What did you read in the news?)

**Bridge:** Everyone brings one Greek headline and explains it in Greek.
