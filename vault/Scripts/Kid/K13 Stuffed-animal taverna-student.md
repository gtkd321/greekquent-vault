# Stuffed-animal taverna

**The game:** The kid is the waiter with a notepad, taking orders from the toys and bringing play food.

## Say it

- You: **Τι θέλετε;** — What would you like?
- Grown-up (as toy): **Θα ήθελα μια σαλάτα, παρακαλώ.** — I'd like a salad, please.
- You: **Τι πίνετε;** — What are you drinking?
- Grown-up: **Νερό. Τι τρως;** — Water. What are you eating?
- You: **Τρώω ψάρι!** — I'm eating fish!
- Grown-up: **Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ!** — The bill, please!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι θέλετε;** — What would you like?
- You (teacher): **Τι πίνετε;** — What are you drinking?
- You (teacher): **Νερό. Τι τρως;** — Water. What are you eating?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

Make a menu with drawings of each dish, and play-dough food to go with it.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Θα ήθελα…**: Hand on chest, then a polite palm-up "may I".
- **Έχετε ψάρι σήμερα;**: Swim one flat hand like a fish.
- **Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ.**: Mime signing in the air toward the waiter.
- **Πολύ νόστιμο!**: Kiss your fingertips, then pat your stomach.
- **Καλή όρεξη!**: Both hands sweep over the table, offering the food.
