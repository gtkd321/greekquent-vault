# Shoebox house and color hunt

**The game:** Draw or build a house, describe it with colors and sizes, then race to find colors around the room.

## Say it

- You: **Έχω ένα μικρό σπίτι.** — I have a small house.
- You: **Η κουζίνα είναι κίτρινη.** — The kitchen is yellow.
- Grown-up: **Βρες κάτι κόκκινο!** — Find something red!
- You: **Ένα κόκκινο αυτοκίνητο!** — A red car!
- You: **Μια κόκκινη μπάλα!** — A red ball!
- You: **Βλέπω ένα μεγάλο δωμάτιο.** — I see a big room.

## Make it

The shoebox house, painted and colored, with a room for every color.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Έχω ένα μικρό σπίτι.**: Make a small roof with your hands.
- **ένα μεγάλο δωμάτιο**: Spread your arms wide.
- **Η κουζίνα είναι μικρή.**: Stir a pot inside a tiny box drawn with your hands.
- **Βλέπω ένα κόκκινο…**: Point at something red in the room.
- **καινούργιο ή παλιό;**: Shiny sparkle hands, then bend over like an old person.
