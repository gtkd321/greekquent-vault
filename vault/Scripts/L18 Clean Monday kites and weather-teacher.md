## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 7 new words on their printed list: μεγάλος (99) · πειράζει (242) · ο αέρας (490) · ψηλά (581) · το κρύο (859) · η ζέστη (1243) · βρέχει (1384)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'What's the weather like?'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Τι καιρό κάνει;**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Τι καιρό κάνει;** | What's the weather like? | Look up at the sky, palm up. |
> > | **Κάνει κρύο.** | It's cold. | Hug yourself and shiver. |
> > | **Κάνει ζέστη.** | It's hot. | Fan your face. |
> > | **Βρέχει.** | It's raining. | Wiggle your fingers downward like rain. |
> > | **Έχει αέρα.** | It's windy. | Sway like a tree and blow. |
> > | **Βλέπω τον χαρταετό!** | I see the kite! | Shade your eyes and tug a kite string. |
>
> **Step 1: the weather**  
> T: (cards) Κάνει κρύο. Κάνει ζέστη. Βρέχει. Έχει ήλιο. Έχει αέρα.  
> **Step 2: today**  
> T: (points out the window) Τι καιρό κάνει σήμερα; — S1: Σήμερα κάνει κρύο. / Έχει ήλιο.  
> **Step 3: Καθαρά Δευτέρα**  
> T: (holds up the kite) Ο χαρταετός! Βλέπω τον χαρταετό! Ο χαρταετός είναι ψηλά.  
> **Step 4: anchor recycling**  
> T: Θέλω λαγάνα. Θέλεις ελιές; Μπορείς να δεις τον χαρταετό μου;  
> S1: Ναι, βλέπω τον χαρταετό σου!  
> **Warm-up dialogue**  
> A: Καλή Σαρακοστή! Τι καιρό κάνει;  
> B: Κάνει λίγο κρύο, αλλά έχει αέρα.  
> A: Τέλεια! Πάμε για χαρταετό!  
> B: Βλέπω τον χαρταετό σου! Είναι πολύ ψηλά!  
> A: Θέλεις λαγάνα;  
> B: Ναι, θέλω! Καλή Σαρακοστή!  
> **Exit ticket:** Say today's weather in one sentence.

## 3 · Main scene

**Clean Monday kites**: Friends check the weather and fly kites on Καθαρά Δευτέρα.

**Familiarity:** 93% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** μεγάλος, λόφο, χαλβά, πειράζει

- A: **Καλή Σαρακοστή!** — Have a good Lent!
- B: **Καλή Σαρακοστή! Τι καιρό κάνει σήμερα;** — Have a good Lent! What's the weather like today?
- A: **Κάνει λίγο κρύο, αλλά έχει ήλιο.** — It's a little cold, but it's sunny.
- B: **Έχει αέρα;** — Is it windy?
- A: **Ναι, έχει πολύ αέρα! Είναι καλή μέρα για χαρταετό.** — Yes, it's very windy! It's a good day for a kite.
- B: **Έχεις χαρταετό;** — Do you have a kite?
- A: **Ναι, έχω έναν μεγάλο χαρταετό. Πάμε στον λόφο!** — Yes, I have a big kite. Let's go to the hill!
- B: **Πάμε! Θέλω λαγάνα και ελιές.** — Let's go! I want lagana and olives.
- A: **Έχω λαγάνα, ελιές και χαλβά.** — I have lagana, olives, and halva.
- B (on the hill): **Βλέπω τον χαρταετό σου! Είναι πολύ ψηλά!** — I see your kite! It's really high!
- A: **Ωχ, βρέχει!** — Oh no, it's raining!
- B: **Δεν πειράζει. Πάμε στο σπίτι. Κάνει κρύο τώρα.** — Never mind. Let's go home. It's cold now.

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Weather: Κάνει κρύο. · Κάνει ζέστη. · Βρέχει. · Έχει ήλιο. · Έχει αέρα.
> - Food: λαγάνα · ελιές · χαλβάς · ταραμοσαλάτα
> - Place: στον λόφο · στο πάρκο · στη θάλασσα
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Weather changes every 30 seconds (the teacher holds up a card), and the scene has to react each time.
> - TV weather report: one student presents the weather for three Greek cities from a map.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Time shift: as tomorrow, with θα.
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "It's a little cold, but it's sunny." · B: "I have lagana, olives, and halva." · C: "What's the weather like?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Say today's weather in one sentence. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Every morning, give a one-line weather report in Greek.
> - Look up the weather in Athens, Thessaloniki, and Heraklion and say it aloud.
>
> **Out in the world:** Fly a kite, or make a paper one, and say the weather in Greek before you go out each day this week: Σήμερα κάνει…
>
> **Watch-list:** βρέχει is VRE-hee. χαρταετός is khar-ta-e-TOS. Students say "είναι κρύο"; it's Κάνει κρύο.
