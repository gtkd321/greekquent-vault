> [!tip]- Word web: chunks for σύμφωνα με and more (2026-11-24a)
> σύμφωνα με is one entry in the 1,500 because σύμφωνα almost never comes without με. Its web now has chunks when you hold a word: σύμφωνα με την αστυνομία, σύμφωνα με τα στοιχεία, and more. δικός μου, παρόλο που and με συγχωρείτε got chunks too. When two words have no chunk written yet, holding one now shows a short example line that uses both, if there is one.

> [!tip]- Clearer help pages and one More button (2026-11-24a)
> The button that opens the side panel now says **More ›** on lessons too, the same as on word cards. In the ⋯ menu it is called **Side panel**. Setting up and the other help pages now match the buttons you see.

> [!tip]- Kids: hello song and Now you are the teacher (2026-11-24a)
> The hello song plays from the very first tap now. In Now you are the teacher, every line says who says it, You or Grown-up, and the grown-up's turn to answer (and get one wrong on purpose) is a step in the list.

> [!tip]- Tap a word, get its card. Star favorites. Resources everywhere (2026-11-23b)
> In Words search and Look up, a tap anywhere on a word now opens its word card: the Greek, the English or the number. Close words open the card too. Word web is still there as its own button. On any word card, tap ☆ Star to keep it as a favorite. Your starred words show a ★ in the list, and a ★ Starred button on Words shows just those. Stars are saved in My Work, so updates never erase them. Verb tables and the other reference pages are now one tap away from any screen: in the app, tap Resources at the top right. In Obsidian, it is in the ⋯ menu.

> [!tip]- Your class days plan your week (2026-11-23a)

On class day: a quick rally warm-up. Language Transfer is now on for everyone. Tap "I listened" for 1 φόρα a track and "I read today" for 2. Change your days or book any time in Options (Class, then Settings and help).

> [!tip]- Search finds any part of a word (2026-11-20u)
> Words search now finds letters anywhere in a word: μένω also brings up περιμένω and παραμένω. Spell it how it sounds and it still finds the word (σκεψι finds σκέψη). Missing letters? Type * for the gap, like *ξη or _μεν_. Not in the 1,500? The translation shows right there under the search, and if you misspelled it, it shows the closest spelling (κατάψηξη becomes κατάψυξη, freezing).

> [!tip]- New to Greek? Start by ear (2026-11-20q)

> [!tip]- Clearer English, and word families (2026-11-20p)
> A few words shared one English meaning without being the same, so they now say how they differ: πότε is "when? (asking)" and όταν is "when, whenever"; νομίζω is "I think (that)" and σκέφτομαι is "I think about." In the Word web, many words now have a Family button. It shows the words built on the same root, like μένω, περιμένω and παραμένω. Hold one to see how it is built: περι- (around) + μένω. Tap it again for Goes with.

> [!tip]- Words that mean the same thing both count (2026-11-20o)
> Some of the 1,500 share one English meaning: ότι and πως both mean "that," and αμάξι and αυτοκίνητο both mean "car." When a quiz asks you to say the Greek, either one counts. If you already know the other word, it shows after your answer, for example "also πως."

> [!tip]- Go deeper after a quiz (2026-11-20n)
> After Quiz me, Go deeper runs one longer round: your 10 words plus up to 20 more from all three colors, mixed. It asks you to make the Greek, by saying it or building it from its letters. A word you miss comes back a few questions later, up to three tries. It stops at 30 words or 15 minutes, because coming back on another day helps more than another round now. Close spelling still counts. Also fixed: on some phones the first word a quiz played by itself was silent until Hear it again. Now the voice wakes on your first tap.
