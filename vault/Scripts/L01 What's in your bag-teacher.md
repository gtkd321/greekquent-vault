## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 24 new words on their printed list: και (2) · εγώ (5) · σε (8) · ένας, μία, ένα (10) · εσύ (15) · σου (16) · έχω (17) · τι (18) · όχι (22) · ναι (23) · λέω (38) · σας (55) · ευχαριστώ (56) · πώς (66) · παρακαλώ (83) · γεια (96) · ορίστε (249) · το νερό (270) · το βιβλίο (394) · το κλειδί (632) · η τσάντα (645) · το κινητό (664) · το τετράδιο (1472) · το στυλό (1474)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** say **Γεια σας** with a big wave to the room. Students guess what it means before you explain.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Γεια σας** | Hello (to a group, or polite) | Big wave to the whole room, fingers together and slightly bent. |
> > | **Γεια σου** | Hi (to one friend) | Small wave to one person, fingers together. |
> > | **Πώς σε λένε;** | What's your name? | Palm-up hand toward the partner, eyebrows up. |
> > | **Με λένε…** | My name is… | Hand flat on your own chest. |
> > | **Έχω ένα…** | I have a… | Hold the object up close to your body. |
> > | **Έχεις ένα…;** | Do you have a…? | Point at the partner's bag, eyebrows up. |
> > | **Ναι, έχω.** | Yes, I do. | Small Greek nod, head tipping down and to the side, and pat the object. |
> > | **Τι έχεις;** | What do you have? | Both palms up, shoulders slightly raised. |
>
> **Welcome**  
> T: Γεια σας! — Ss: Γεια σας!  
> T: Γεια σου.  
> T: Με λένε [name]. Πώς σε λένε;  
> S1: Με λένε ___.  
> T: Γεια σου, ___!  
> T: Ναι. / Όχι.  
> **Anchor verb: έχω**  
> T: Έχω ένα βιβλίο. Έχω ένα στυλό. Έχω ένα κινητό. Έχω ένα κλειδί. Έχω νερό. Έχω ένα τετράδιο.  
> T: Έχεις ένα κλειδί; — S1: Ναι, έχω.  
> T: Έχεις ένα κινητό; — S2: Όχι.  
> **Scenario: "Τι έχεις;" bag guessing game**  
> S1: Έχεις ένα κινητό;  
> S2: Ναι, έχω! / Όχι.  
> T: Τι έχεις;

## 3 · Main scene

**First day: what's in your bag?**: Two classmates meet before class and compare what they brought.

**Familiarity:** 84% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** σε, η τσάντα, και, ορίστε, ευχαριστώ, παρακαλώ

- A: **Γεια σου!** — Hi!
- B: **Γεια σου! Πώς σε λένε;** — Hi! What's your name?
- A: **Με λένε Μαρία. Εσένα;** — My name is Maria. And you?
- B: **Με λένε Νίκο.** — My name is Nikos.
- A: **Τι έχεις στην τσάντα;** — What do you have in your bag?
- B: **Έχω ένα βιβλίο και ένα στυλό. Εσύ;** — I have a book and a pen. You?
- A: **Έχω ένα κινητό, ένα κλειδί και νερό.** — I have a phone, a key, and water.
- B: **Έχεις ένα τετράδιο;** — Do you have a notebook?
- A: **Όχι. Έχεις ένα στυλό;** — No. Do you have a pen?
- B: **Ναι, έχω. Ορίστε!** — Yes, I do. Here you go!
- A: **Ευχαριστώ!** — Thank you!
- B: **Παρακαλώ.** — You're welcome.
- A: **Γεια σου, Νίκο!** — Bye, Nikos!
- B: **Γεια!** — Bye!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Names: your own name · a Greek name card from a hat
> - Objects: βιβλίο · στυλό · κινητό · κλειδί · τετράδιο · νερό
> - Greeting: Γεια σου (one friend) · Γεια σας (a group, or polite)
>
> **Twists**
>
> - B answers Όχι to everything until the last item, so A has to keep asking.
> - A third student walks in halfway, and both have to greet them and start again with Γεια σας.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "My name is Nikos." · B: "What do you have in your bag?" · C: "Do you have a notebook?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** papers away. Each student writes two of today's phrases by hand on an index card, then says them with the gesture, plus one true sentence about themselves.
