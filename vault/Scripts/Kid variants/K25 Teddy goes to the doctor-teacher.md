# Kid variant · Lesson 25: Teddy goes to the doctor

Play version of [[L25 Christ is risen and the pharmacy-teacher|Lesson 25: Christ is risen and the pharmacy]] · [[K25 Teddy goes to the doctor-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** A toy doctor's visit: point to the body part that hurts, then buy pretend pills at the pharmacy. Ends with the Easter greeting and egg tapping.

**You'll need:** a toy doctor kit or a spoon, band-aids or stickers, a stuffed animal, red eggs

## How to play

1. Touch-the-body-part game: teacher says a body part, and the kid touches it.
2. Kid is the doctor and asks what's wrong. Teacher points and says what hurts.
3. Pharmacy: ask for something, get a sticker "band-aid".
4. Tap red eggs and say the Easter greeting.

## Lines

- K: **Τι έχεις;** — What's wrong?
- T: **Με πονάει το κεφάλι!** — My head hurts!
- T: **Με πονάει το χέρι.** — My hand hurts.
- T: **Με πονάει η κοιλιά.** — My tummy hurts.
- K: **Μπορείτε να μου δώσετε κάτι για το κεφάλι;** — Can you give me something for my head?
- T: **Χριστός Ανέστη!** — Christ is risen!
- K: **Αληθώς Ανέστη!** — Truly He is risen!

## Make it

A paper outline of a body where the kid puts sticker band-aids on the part that hurts.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** A real pharmacy role-play: ask for something for a headache or sore throat.
