## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 15 new words on their printed list: να (3) · είμαι (4) · αυτός (9) · μου (12) · εδώ (28) · ξέρω (33) · καλός (47) · πού (87) · η δουλειά (94) · η μέρα (103) · δύο (104) · το λεπτό (140) · πέντε (323) · μισό (1398) · το ευρώ (1496)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'I don't have…'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Δεν έχω…**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Δεν έχω…** | I don't have… | Turn out empty pockets or open empty hands. |
> > | **Πού είναι το κινητό μου;** | Where is my phone? | Pat your pockets, looking around. |
> > | **Δεν βλέπω…** | I can't see… / I don't see… | Hand over your eyes, head turning. |
> > | **Εδώ είναι!** | Here it is! | Point down hard at the spot and smile. |
> > | **Γιατί δεν μπορείς;** | Why can't you? | Both palms up, head tilted to the side. |
> > | **Έχω δουλειά.** | I have work. / I'm busy. | Mime typing fast. |
> > | **Μπορώ να δω;** | May I see? | Point at your eye, then at the object, eyebrows up. |
> > | **Πόσο κάνει;** | How much is it? | Rub thumb and fingers together. |
> > | **Δύο ευρώ.** | Two euros. | Tap two fingers on the counter, one coin at a time. |
> > | **Δεν ξέρω.** | I don't know. | Shrug with palms up. |
>
> **Negation:** Έχω νερό. → Δεν έχω νερό. Θέλω κρασί. → Δεν θέλω κρασί.  
> **Keystone, now readable:** Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.  
> **Possession:** Το κινητό μου. Το κινητό σου. Η τσάντα μου. Τα κλειδιά σου.  
> T: Γιατί δεν μπορείς; — S1: Γιατί… έχω δουλειά.  
> **Chunk:** Μπορώ να δω;  
> **Scenario: "I can't find my phone"**  
> S1: Πού είναι το κινητό μου; Δεν βλέπω το κινητό μου!  
> S2: Δεν ξέρω… Εδώ είναι!  
> S1: Ευχαριστώ!  
> **Mini-scene: in a shop**  
> S1: Καλημέρα! Μπορώ να δω;  
> S2: Ναι, βέβαια.  
> S1: Πόσο κάνει;  
> S2: Δύο ευρώ.  
>
> Full teacher transcript: [[Greekquent Year One Curriculum-teacher|Greekquent Year One Curriculum]], Section 3.
>
> **Recall 1 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three of today's phrases. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

## 3 · Main scene

**Where's my phone? and the corner shop**: Part 1: a lost phone. Part 2: shopping at a small shop.

**Familiarity:** 92% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** μισό, το λεπτό, αυτός, πέντε, καλός, η μέρα

- A: **Ωχ! Πού είναι το κινητό μου;** — Oh no! Where's my phone?
- B: **Δεν ξέρω. Δεν βλέπω το κινητό σου.** — I don't know. I don't see your phone.
- A: **Δεν έχω το κινητό μου! Και τα κλειδιά μου;** — I don't have my phone! And my keys?
- B: **Τα κλειδιά σου είναι εδώ!** — Your keys are here!
- A: **Ευχαριστώ! Αλλά το κινητό μου;** — Thanks! But my phone?
- B: **Είναι στην τσάντα σου;** — Is it in your bag?
- A: **Όχι, δεν είναι… Μισό λεπτό… Εδώ είναι!** — No, it isn't… Wait a second… Here it is!
- B: **Τέλεια! Μπορείς τώρα; Θέλω καφέ.** — Great! Can you come now? I want a coffee.
- A: **Όχι, δεν μπορώ. Έχω δουλειά.** — No, I can't. I'm busy with work.
- B: **Γιατί δεν μπορείς;** — Why can't you?
- A: **Γιατί δεν έχω ώρα!** — Because I don't have time!
- A: **Καλημέρα! Μπορώ να δω;** — Good morning! May I look?
- Seller: **Ναι, βέβαια.** — Yes, of course.
- A: **Πόσο κάνει;** — How much is it?
- Seller: **Δύο ευρώ.** — Two euros.
- A: **Και αυτό;** — And this one?
- Seller: **Πέντε ευρώ.** — Five euros.
- A: **Δεν θέλω αυτό. Θέλω το βιβλίο. Ορίστε δύο ευρώ.** — I don't want this one. I want the book. Here are two euros.
- Seller: **Ευχαριστώ! Καλή μέρα!** — Thank you! Have a good day!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Lost item: το κινητό μου · τα κλειδιά μου · η τσάντα μου · το βιβλίο μου
> - Where: εδώ · στην τσάντα · στο τραπέζι · κάτω από το τραπέζι
> - Price: ένα ευρώ · δύο ευρώ · πέντε ευρώ
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα.**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Actually hide a student's phone or keys in the room. The class can only use Greek to find it.
> - The shopkeeper says Δεν έχω… to everything, and the customer has to keep asking for something else.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "Your keys are here!" · B: "Is it in your bag?" · C: "Why can't you?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** papers away. Each student writes two of today's phrases by hand on an index card, then says them with the gesture, plus one true sentence about themselves.
