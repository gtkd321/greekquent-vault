# Teddy goes to the doctor

**The game:** A toy doctor's visit: point to the body part that hurts, then the kid gives the teddy a pretend pill. Ends with the Easter greeting and egg tapping.

## Say it

- You: **Τι έχεις;** — What's wrong?
- Grown-up: **Με πονάει το κεφάλι!** — My head hurts!
- Grown-up: **Με πονάει το χέρι.** — My hand hurts.
- Grown-up: **Με πονάει η κοιλιά.** — My tummy hurts.
- You: **Σου δίνω κάτι για το κεφάλι. Ορίστε!** — I'm giving you something for your head. Here you go!
- Grown-up: **Χριστός Ανέστη!** — Christ is risen!
- You: **Αληθώς Ανέστη!** — Truly He is risen!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι έχεις;** — What's wrong?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

A paper outline of a body where the kid puts sticker band-aids on the part that hurts.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Τι έχεις;**: Doctor face: a hand on the teddy's forehead.
- **το κεφάλι**: Both hands on your head: ouch.
- **το χέρι**: Hold your hand and shake it.
- **η κοιλιά**: Hands on your tummy, and groan.
- **Σου δίνω**: Hand over a pretend pill.
- **Χριστός Ανέστη!**: Tap your red egg on your grown-up's egg.
