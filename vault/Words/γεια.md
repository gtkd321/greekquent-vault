# γεια

**hi, bye** · say it: ya · rank **#96** of 1,500 · first in [[L01 What's in your bag-student|Lesson 1]] · English cognates: hygiene

> Γεια σου, φίλε!

> [!tip]- When to say it
> Γεια σου (a friend, family) or Γεια σας (someone older, a stranger, a group) works any time, coming or going.
> [Play Τι λέμε;](obsidian://greekquent?open=hello) to practice hello and goodbye through the day.

- [[όλος]] — all, whole
