## 3 · Main scene

**What did you do last weekend?**: Friends swap weekend news with the common "did once" past forms: πήγα, έκανα, είδα, είπα, έφαγα, ήπια.

**Familiarity:** 93% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** ποιον, έφαγε, παγωτό, έφαγες, ότι

- A: **Τι έκανες το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What did you do last weekend?
- B: **Το Σάββατο πήγα στη θάλασσα.** — On Saturday I went to the beach.
- A: **Ωραία! Με ποιον;** — Nice! Who with?
- B: **Με τη Μαρία. Είδα και τον Νίκο εκεί!** — With Maria. I saw Nikos there too!
- A: **Αλήθεια; Τι είπε;** — Really? What did he say?
- B: **Είπε «Γεια σας!» και μετά έφαγε ένα παγωτό.** — He said "Hi!" and then he ate an ice cream.
- A: **Χα! Κι εσύ; Τι έφαγες;** — Ha! And you? What did you eat?
- B: **Έφαγα ψάρι και ήπια μια μπίρα. Εσύ τι έκανες;** — I ate fish and had a beer. What did you do?
- A: **Είχα πολλή δουλειά. Την Κυριακή πήγα στην εκκλησία και μετά ήπια έναν καφέ με τη γιαγιά μου.** — I had a lot of work. On Sunday I went to church and then had a coffee with my grandmother.
- B: **Πώς είναι η γιαγιά σου;** — How's your grandmother?
- A: **Καλά! Είπε ότι θέλει να σε δει.** — Good! She said she wants to see you.
- B: **Κι εγώ!** — Me too!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Where: πήγα στη θάλασσα · πήγα στην εκκλησία · πήγα στη λαϊκή
> - What: είδα… · έφαγα… · ήπια… · έκανα… · είπα…
> - Who: με τη Μαρία · με τον Νίκο · με την οικογένεια
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Χθες ήθελα να πάω, αλλά δεν μπορούσα.**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Two truths and a lie: each student says three past-tense sentences, and the class guesses the lie.
> - Chain story: each student adds one sentence with a new past verb.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Time shift: as yesterday (ήθελα, πήγα…) or as tomorrow (θα…).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "What did you do last weekend?" · B: "On Saturday I went to the beach." · C: "How's your grandmother?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Say three things you did last weekend. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Every Monday, write a 5-sentence diary of your weekend.
> - Tell someone at home what you did today in 3 "did once" sentences.
>
> **Out in the world:** Write a short diary entry by hand about last weekend, in Greek, five sentences. Read it to someone, even if they don't understand it.
>
> **Watch-list:** είδα is EE-dha. ήπια is EE-pya. Point out that Είχα πολλή δουλειά is correct as is: "had" usually stays in last week's form.
