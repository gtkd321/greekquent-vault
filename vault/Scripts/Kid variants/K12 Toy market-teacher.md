# Kid variant · Lesson 12: Toy market

Play version of [[L12 Laiki street market-teacher|Lesson 12: Laiki street market]] · [[K12 Toy market-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** A play shop with price tags and play money, just like the λαϊκή.

**You'll need:** toy food or real fruit and vegetables, play money (or real coins), sticky notes for prices

## How to play

1. Kid is the seller first: he puts prices on everything.
2. Teacher buys, asks prices, and pays. Count the money together.
3. Swap roles.

## Lines

- T: **Καλημέρα! Έχετε ντομάτες;** — Good morning! Do you have tomatoes?
- K: **Ναι, έχουμε!** — Yes, we do!
- T: **Πόσο κάνουν;** — How much are they?
- K: **Δύο ευρώ το κιλό.** — Two euros a kilo.
- T: **Θέλω ένα κιλό, παρακαλώ.** — I'd like one kilo, please.
- K: **Ορίστε!** — Here you go!

## Make it

Price tags with drawings of the food. The kid can write the numbers in Greek words once he knows them.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Do it with real produce at home, and push the numbers to 100.
