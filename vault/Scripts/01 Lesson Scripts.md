# 01 Lesson Scripts

The 26 lessons of Greekquent Year One (late October to mid May). All 26 lessons have a full warm-up script and a longer main scene. The complete teacher transcripts, with timings, English explanations, and homework, are in [[Greekquent Year One Curriculum-teacher|Greekquent Year One Curriculum]]. Every lesson also has a **kid variant**: the same language taught through toys, movement, and making things. Adults enjoy them too. They live in the Kid variants folder.

Every lesson note now follows a 60-minute flow: spaced review, then the warm-up script, then a longer main scene, then fork rounds. The **Intermediate** column links to a longer scene on the same theme, so beginners and intermediate students can share one vault. How the review and forking work: [[03 Class Flow and Spaced Repetition]]. Why each step is there: [[04 How Memory Works]]. **Familiar** is the share of main-scene words students already know after the warm-up (the target is about 95%; Lessons 1–2 are lower because students start from zero). Each lesson has two files: **-teacher** is your run sheet (timings, prompts, recall points, watch-list), and **-student** is the page students use (phrases with gestures, review prompts with hidden answers, boards, the scene, practice, homework). Student pages are in the Student folder, and all 26 are also collected in Handouts as one workbook. Each teacher note keeps the main scene open and folds the rest into boxes: click a box title to open or close it.
