# Watch list

Free Greek videos for beginners. On some days a Watch this card on Today picks one that fits your focus for the week and the lesson you are on. Each one plays on YouTube; nothing here is copied.

> [!tip]- How the levels work
> - **ears**: easy listening. Just let the sound wash over you.
> - **a1**: first words, letters and short scenes.
> - **a2**: longer, a bit faster.
> After a video, tap Too easy, Just right or Too hard, and the next one adjusts.
