## Today's phrases

Make the gesture every time you say the phrase. It helps the phrase stick.

| Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| **Έχουμε δύο παιδιά.** | We have two children. | Circle hand between yourself and a partner ("we"), then show two short heights. |
| **Έχετε παιδιά;** | Do you have children? | Sweep a hand across the group, then show a child's height. |
| **η γιαγιά μου** | my grandmother | Bend over a pretend cane, hand on your chest. |
| **ο παππούς μου** | my grandfather | Twirl a pretend moustache, or play with κομπολόι worry beads. |
| **Θέλουν…** | They want… | Point at two other people, then reach out eagerly. |

## Main scene

**Family photos**: Two friends show each other family photos. This is the first time all six present-tense forms appear together.

**New words:** γλυκός

- A: **Αυτή είναι η οικογένειά μου.** — This is my family.
- B: **Ωραία! Αυτή είναι η μητέρα σου;** — Nice! Is this your mother?
- A: **Ναι, και αυτός είναι ο πατέρας μου.** — Yes, and this is my father.
- B: **Έχεις αδελφό ή αδελφή;** — Do you have a brother or a sister?
- A: **Έχω έναν αδελφό και μια αδελφή.** — I have a brother and a sister.
- B: **Έχουν παιδιά;** — Do they have kids?
- A: **Ναι, έχουν τρία παιδιά. Εσείς; Έχετε παιδιά;** — Yes, they have three kids. And you? Do you have kids?
- B: **Ναι, έχουμε δύο παιδιά. Θέλουν γλυκά!** — Yes, we have two kids. They want sweets!
- A: **Και η γιαγιά σου;** — And your grandmother?
- B: **Η γιαγιά μου και ο παππούς μου είναι στην Ελλάδα.** — My grandmother and grandfather are in Greece.
- A: **Βλέπετε τη γιαγιά;** — Do you see your grandmother?
- B: **Όχι, δεν μπορούμε. Αλλά έχουμε φωτογραφίες!** — No, we can't. But we have photos!
- Teacher: **Εμείς έχουμε… εσείς έχετε… αυτοί έχουν…** — We have… you all have… they have…

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Who: η μητέρα · ο πατέρας · ο αδελφός · η αδελφή · η γιαγιά · ο παππούς
> - How many: ένα παιδί · δύο παιδιά · τρία παιδιά
> - Person switch: έχω → έχουμε · θέλεις → θέλετε · μπορεί → μπορούν · βλέπει → βλέπουν
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;**
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Tell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (90, 60, then 45 seconds). When you're listening, just listen.

> [!example]- Build it with tiles
> Cut out your Lesson 9 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
>
> 1. Build it from the English, then read it aloud.
> 2. Turn it into a question: swap the . for ; and raise your voice at the end.
> 3. Make it negative: put δεν right before the verb.
> 4. Swap one tile for another word from the extra tiles.
> 5. Write your last version by hand in your notebook.
>
> - **A:** This is my family.
> - **B:** Do you have a brother or a sister?
> - **C:** And your grandmother?
>
> Extra tiles: δεν · ; · . · η μητέρα · ο πατέρας · ο αδελφός · η αδελφή
