## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 15 new words on their printed list: μπορώ (24) · εμείς (30) · γιατί (34) · καλά (39) · εντάξει (40) · πολύς (77) · η ώρα (101) · σήμερα (137) · πόσο (145) · λυπάμαι (155) · το βράδυ (200) · αύριο (204) · το πρωί (256) · τέλεια (279) · βέβαια (334)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'May I?'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Μπορώ;**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Μπορώ;** | May I? | Reach toward an object and pause, eyebrows up. |
> > | **Ναι, βέβαια.** | Yes, of course. | Big open-handed "go ahead" sweep. |
> > | **Μπορείς;** | Can you? | Point at the partner and tap your wrist. |
> > | **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.** | I want to, but I can't. | Reach out eagerly, then pull back and shake your head. |
> > | **Μπορείς σήμερα το βράδυ;** | Can you make it tonight? | Point down at the floor ("today"), then lay your head on your hands ("evening"). |
> > | **Λυπάμαι, δεν μπορώ.** | Sorry, I can't. | Hand on chest with a sad face, then a small head shake. |
> > | **Αύριο το πρωί μπορώ.** | Tomorrow morning I can. | Roll a hand forward ("tomorrow"), then stretch both arms up as if waking. |
> > | **Έχεις ώρα;** | Do you have time? | Tap your wrist. |
>
> **Anchor verb: μπορώ**  
> T: Μπορώ; — Ss: Ναι, βέβαια.  
> T: Μπορείς; — (S1 opens the jar) T: Μπορεί!  
> **Keystone sentence:** Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.  
> T: Μπορείς σήμερα; — S1: Ναι, μπορώ. / Λυπάμαι, δεν μπορώ.  
> **Scenario: making plans**  
> S1: Μπορείς σήμερα το βράδυ;  
> S2: Λυπάμαι, δεν μπορώ. Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.  
> S1: Αύριο το πρωί;  
> S2: Ναι, αύριο το πρωί μπορώ!  
> **Reading card:** Μπορώ; – Ναι. / Μαρία, μπορείς; / Πόσο θέλεις; – Πολλά! / Έχεις ώρα; – Όχι.  
>

>

## 3 · Main scene

**Can you make it?**: Two friends ask permission and try to find a time to meet.

**Familiarity:** 91% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** γιατί, τέλεια, καλά, εμείς, εντάξει

- A: **Γεια σου, Νίκο! Μπορώ; (points to the empty chair)** — Hi, Nikos! May I?
- B: **Ναι, βέβαια!** — Yes, of course!
- A: **Έχεις ώρα σήμερα;** — Do you have time today?
- B: **Όχι σήμερα. Γιατί;** — Not today. Why?
- A: **Θέλω καφέ. Μπορείς σήμερα το βράδυ;** — I want a coffee. Can you make it tonight?
- B: **Λυπάμαι, δεν μπορώ. Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.** — Sorry, I can't. I want to, but I can't.
- A: **Αύριο το πρωί;** — Tomorrow morning?
- B: **Αύριο το πρωί… ναι, μπορώ!** — Tomorrow morning… yes, I can!
- A: **Τέλεια! Και η Μαρία; Μπορεί;** — Perfect! And Maria? Can she?
- B: **Η Μαρία δεν μπορεί το πρωί. Μπορεί το βράδυ.** — Maria can't in the morning. She can in the evening.
- A: **Καλά. Αύριο το πρωί εμείς, και το βράδυ η Μαρία!** — Okay. Tomorrow morning us, and in the evening Maria!
- B: **Εντάξει! Γεια!** — Okay! Bye!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - When: σήμερα · σήμερα το βράδυ · αύριο · αύριο το πρωί
> - Who: η Μαρία · ο Νίκος · ο δάσκαλος · η γιαγιά
> - Answer: Ναι, μπορώ! · Λυπάμαι, δεν μπορώ. · Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Θέλεις λίγο νερό;**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - B can never make it. A has to offer three different times before B finally says yes.
> - Permission round: students have to ask Μπορώ; before taking a marker, opening the window, or sitting down.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "Do you have time today?" · B: "Tomorrow morning?" · C: "I want to, but I can't."
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** papers away. Each student writes two of today's phrases by hand on an index card, then says them with the gesture, plus one true sentence about themselves.
