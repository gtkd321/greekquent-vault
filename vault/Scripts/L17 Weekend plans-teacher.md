## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 4 new words on their printed list: ο φίλος (112) · η ταινία (333) · η βόλτα (635) · το Σαββατοκύριακο (1075)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'What will you do this weekend?'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;** | What will you do this weekend? | Roll a hand forward, then stretch out relaxed. |
> > | **Θα έχω ώρα.** | I'll have time. | Roll the hand forward, then tap the wrist and smile. |
> > | **Θα δω μια ταινία.** | I'll watch a movie. | Roll the hand forward, then mime eating popcorn. |
> > | **Θα πάμε βόλτα.** | We'll go for a walk. | Link arms with a partner and stroll a few steps. |
> > | **Θα πάω…** | I'll go… | Step forward onto the "tomorrow" line on the floor. |
>
> **Step 1: θα in front**  
>
> > [!info]- Board: today → tomorrow with θα
> > | Today | Tomorrow (θα + verb) |
> > |---|---|
> > | Σήμερα έχω δουλειά. | Αύριο θα έχω ώρα. |
> > | Θέλω να δω μια ταινία. | Θα δω μια ταινία. |
> >
> > Write this on the board at this step. Full tables: [[02 Verb Tables]]
>
> **Step 2: walk the timeline**  
> T: (stands on "today") Σήμερα δουλεύω. (steps to "tomorrow") Αύριο θα πάω στη θάλασσα. Θα δω τους φίλους μου. Θα κάνω βόλτα.  
> **Step 3: questions and the negative**  
> T: Τι θα κάνεις το Σάββατο; — S1: Θα πάω… / Θα δω μια ταινία.  
> T: Θα έχεις ώρα την Κυριακή; — S1: Ναι, θα έχω. / Όχι, δεν θα έχω.  
> **Step 4: we**  
> T: Θα πάμε βόλτα. Θα δούμε μια ταινία. Θα φάμε μαζί.  
> **Warm-up dialogue**  
> A: Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;  
> B: Θα έχω ώρα. Θα δω μια ταινία. Εσύ;  
> A: Θα πάω στο χωριό. Θα δω τη γιαγιά μου.  
> B: Ωραία! Θα πάμε βόλτα την Κυριακή;  
> A: Την Κυριακή δεν θα είμαι εδώ. Τη Δευτέρα;  
> B: Εντάξει, τη Δευτέρα!  
> **Exit ticket:** Say two θα plans for this weekend.  
>

> **Recall 1 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three of today's phrases. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

## 3 · Main scene

**Weekend plans**: Two friends talk about what they'll do this weekend, using θα + verb.

**Familiarity:** 99% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** δουλέψω

- A: **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What will you do this weekend?
- B: **Το Σάββατο θα πάω στη θάλασσα με τους φίλους μου.** — On Saturday I'll go to the beach with my friends.
- A: **Ωραία!** — Nice!
- B: **Εσύ;** — You?
- A: **Το Σάββατο θα δουλέψω. Αλλά την Κυριακή θα έχω ώρα.** — On Saturday I'll work. But on Sunday I'll have time.
- B: **Θα δεις μια ταινία;** — Will you watch a movie?
- A: **Ναι, θα δω μια ταινία με τα παιδιά. Και μετά θα πάμε βόλτα.** — Yes, I'll watch a movie with the kids. And then we'll go for a walk.
- B: **Θέλεις να έρθεις στη θάλασσα την Κυριακή;** — Do you want to come to the beach on Sunday?
- A: **Δεν μπορώ, αλλά το άλλο Σάββατο θα έρθω!** — I can't, but next Saturday I'll come!
- B: **Τέλεια. Θα φάμε ψάρι!** — Perfect. We'll eat fish!
- A: **Θα είναι πολύ ωραία. Καλό Σαββατοκύριακο!** — That will be really nice. Have a good weekend!
- B: **Επίσης!** — You too!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - When: το Σάββατο · την Κυριακή · αύριο · το άλλο Σάββατο
> - Plan: θα πάω… · θα δω μια ταινία · θα φάμε… · θα κάνω βόλτα · θα δουλέψω
> - With whom: με τους φίλους μου · με τα παιδιά · με τη γιαγιά
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Η οικογένειά μου είναι από…**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Weather forecast card: if it rains (αν βρέχει), every plan has to change.
> - Fortune teller: predict your partner's weekend in the third person: Θα πάει…, θα δει…
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Time shift: as tomorrow, with θα.
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "What will you do this weekend?" · B: "Will you watch a movie?" · C: "Do you want to come to the beach on Sunday?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Say two θα plans for this weekend. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Every evening, say 3 things you'll do tomorrow: Αύριο θα….
> - Write your weekend plan in 4 sentences.
>
> **Out in the world:** Make a real weekend plan with someone and write it in Greek on paper: Το Σάββατο θα… Check it off on Monday.
>
> **Watch-list:** Students say θα δεν; the order is δεν θα. Save the θα βλέπω vs θα δω contrast for intermediate students.
