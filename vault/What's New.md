# What's new

Back to [[Today]].

> [!info]- November 16, late night: Three layouts
> - **A calmer start.** At the top of every page there is a small switch: **Simple**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**. Everyone starts on Simple.
> - **Simple:** Today and this week's lesson stay bright. The rest of the file list is grayed out but still opens if you tap it. Tools: Look up, Send homework, My homework.
> - **Intermediate:** adds games, the word graph, your colors, past lessons, and your own word cards.
> - **Advanced:** everything, including Build a sentence, Pair it points, and Ask.
> - When you have marked enough words, Today suggests the next layout. Switch any time, in either direction. Nothing is deleted, and your choice is saved in My Work.

> [!info]- November 16, night: Smoother word graph
> - Tap a word in the word graph and its note now fades in gently instead of flashing in.
> - If your phone is set to reduce motion, it opens instantly as before.

> [!info]- November 16, evening: Your sentences on the word cards
> - Sentences you build with **Build a sentence** now show on the word note of every word they use, under Chunks, in **Yours**. That includes the ones you already saved.
> - Your own chunks and sentences show there too, each with a Hear button.
> - Nothing moved: they are still saved in My Work (My pairs and My word cards), so updates never erase them.

> [!info]- November 16, later: Your own chunks and Spider-Man
> - **Add to a word card:** on any word note, under Chunks, tap **Add a chunk or sentence**. Type the Greek, tap Translate for the English, hear it, and save. It shows on that word's note under **Yours**.
> - Everything you add is saved in My Work, in **My word cards**, so updates never erase it. Fix or delete lines there any time.
> - **Kid lesson 5 (Where is Spider-Man?):** new pass-it round. Έχεις τον Spider-Man; Ναι, έχω τον Spider-Man! Ορίστε. Ευχαριστώ!

> [!info]- November 16: Look up stays in Obsidian
> - **Bonus words are translated right here.** Type a word that is not in the 1,500 and the Greek shows up in the Look up box, with how to say it and a Hear it button. No more jumping to the browser.
> - **Words I looked up now saves the Greek** next to every bonus word.
> - Older look-ups that only had the English get their Greek filled in the next time you open Obsidian online. Anything you typed there yourself stays as it is.

> [!info]- November 14, late: Complete Greek
> - If you have the book Teach Yourself Complete Greek, each intermediate lesson now names the unit that goes with it. The audio is free in the Teach Yourself Library app.
> - Lessons 16 and 26 point to the book's revision tests.
> - No book? Nothing changes for you.

> [!info]- November 14, night: Textbook files
> - New note in Handouts: **00 Which file is which**. One page that says what every file is for.
> - The Fluent in a Year Anki deck (all 1,500 words) is now in Handouts.
> - The two textbook files in Handouts have the same lessons. Use Screen (color, lessons only) on your phone or tablet. Print (lessons plus Fluent in a Year) is the one to print, and it adds the Fluent in a Year planners.
> - The color cover is now the first page of both. The separate cover file is gone.

> [!info]- November 14, evening: Pair it
> - **Pair it with** on word notes: what to say with each word. Verbs show who, what, and how or when. Nouns show the verbs and describing words that go with them.
> - **Build a sentence:** tap one choice from each row and the app builds a correct Greek sentence. Hear it, then save it to My pairs.
> - **Your own pair** counts double. Try a partner we did not list.
> - **Today** shows your Pair it points for the week. Goal: 5 sentences.
> - **Word graph colors** now show the type of word: blue for verbs, purple for nouns, orange for describing words, teal for how, when and where.

> [!info]- November 14, later: clearer names
> - **Lesson pages:** "Student" is now **Beginner**, and "Kid variant" is now **Kid**. Your lesson pages are in Scripts/Beginner, and the kid lessons in Scripts/Kid.
> - **Setting up** now explains where the four tool buttons are on a computer and on a phone.

> [!info]- November 14: Today page and word colors
> - **Today** opens first: your 5 words for today, this week's lesson, and a short review list.
> - **Color your words** right on each word note: Don't have it, Kind of, Have it. Look up shows the color too.
> - **Play with your words:** the Today page opens the Buzzer or Cousins with today's words or your review words.
> - **Missed a word in Cousins?** Tap Save to my vault on the end screen. It shows up on Today for two weeks.
> - **Simpler toolbar:** Today, Look up, Ask, and More.
> - **Cleaner word notes:** the info line now says which lesson a word is first in.
