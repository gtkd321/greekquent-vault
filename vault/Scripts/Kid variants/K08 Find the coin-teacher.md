# Kid variant · Lesson 8: Find the coin

Play version of [[L08 Vasilopita - who has the coin-teacher|Lesson 8: Vasilopita - who has the coin]] · [[K08 Find the coin-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** A pretend βασιλόπιτα cut into pieces, with a coin hidden under one. Count the pieces, then find out who has the coin.

**You'll need:** a paper-plate "cake" cut into slices (or play-dough), a coin, cups to hide it under

## How to play

1. Count the slices out loud together, ένα to δέκα, giving one to each family member and toy.
2. Hide the coin under one slice or cup and shuffle.
3. Everyone lifts a slice in turn, and whoever finds the coin shouts Εγώ!

## Lines

- T: **Ένα, δύο, τρία… δέκα!** — One, two, three… ten!
- T: **Ένα κομμάτι για τον μπαμπά, ένα για τη μαμά…** — One piece for Dad, one for Mom…
- T: **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;** — Who has the coin?
- K: **Εγώ! Εγώ έχω το φλουρί!** — Me! I have the coin!
- Ss: **Καλή χρονιά!** — Happy New Year!

## Make it

The kid decorates the paper cake with the new year's number and gold coin stickers.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Cut a real vasilopita, or count out loud in Greek with jumps, push-ups, or claps to drill the numbers.
