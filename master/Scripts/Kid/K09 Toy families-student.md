# Toy families

**The game:** Stuffed animals become a family, and you and the kid are another family, which lets you use the plural.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Αυτή είναι η μητέρα. Αυτός είναι ο πατέρας.** — This is the mother. This is the father.
- Grown-up: **Έχουν δύο παιδιά.** — They have two kids.
- You: **Έχετε γάτα;** — Do you (all) have a cat?
- Grown-up (as toy family): **Ναι, έχουμε!** — Yes, we do!
- Grown-up: **Τι θέλουν τα παιδιά;** — What do the kids want?
- You: **Θέλουν νερό!** — They want water!
- You: **Μπορούμε;** — Can we?

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Έχετε γάτα;** — Do you (all) have a cat?
- You (teacher): **Τι θέλουν τα παιδιά;** — What do the kids want?
- You (teacher): **Μπορούμε;** — Can we?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

A handprint family tree: each person is a colored handprint with their Greek title written underneath.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Αυτή είναι η μητέρα**: Hold up the mommy toy.
- **Αυτός είναι ο πατέρας**: Hold up the daddy toy.
- **δύο παιδιά**: Two fingers up, then pat two small heads.
- **Έχετε γάτα;**: Sweep a hand over the whole toy family, then claws: meow.
- **έχουμε**: Circle a hand around you and your grown-up: we.
- **Θέλουν**: Point at the toys, then reach out: gimme.
- **Μπορούμε;**: Hands together, with a please-please face.
