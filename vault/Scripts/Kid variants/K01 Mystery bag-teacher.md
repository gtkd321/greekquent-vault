# Kid variant · Lesson 1: Mystery bag

Play version of [[L01 What's in your bag-teacher|Lesson 1: What's in your bag]] · [[K01 Mystery bag-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** The kid reaches into a bag without looking and guesses what's inside.

**You'll need:** a pillowcase or tote, 5–6 toys and household things (a ball, a toy car, a key, a book, a pen)

## How to play

1. Kid feels an object through the bag. Teacher asks; kid guesses.
2. When it's right, the kid pulls it out and holds it up high: Έχω!
3. Swap: the kid hides something, and you guess.

## Lines

- T: **Τι έχω;** — What do I have?
- K: **Έχεις ένα αυτοκίνητο;** — Do you have a car?
- T: **Όχι! / Ναι, έχω!** — No! / Yes, I do!
- T: **Έχεις μια μπάλα;** — Do you have a ball?
- K: **Ναι, έχω μια μπάλα!** — Yes, I have a ball!
- T: **Τι έχεις;** — What do you have?
- K: **Έχω ένα κλειδί.** — I have a key.

## Make it

Each object gets a picture card the kid draws and colors. The cards become his flashcards for next week.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Use a real handbag or backpack, with a 60-second timer to name everything in it.
