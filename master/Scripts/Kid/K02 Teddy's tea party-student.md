# Teddy's tea party

**The game:** The kid is the host and offers drinks to stuffed animals. The toys answer in silly voices.

## Say it

- You: **Θέλεις νερό;** — Do you want water?
- Grown-up (as teddy): **Ναι, θέλω λίγο νερό, ευχαριστώ!** — Yes, I'd like a little water, thank you!
- You: **Θέλεις ένα γλυκό;** — Do you want a sweet?
- Grown-up (as doll): **Όχι, ευχαριστώ.** — No, thank you.
- Grown-up: **Τι θέλει το αρκουδάκι;** — What does the teddy want?
- You: **Το αρκουδάκι θέλει γάλα.** — The teddy wants milk.
- You: **Η κούκλα θέλει τσάι, αλλά…** — The doll wants tea, but…

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Θέλεις νερό;** — Do you want water?
- You (teacher): **Θέλεις ένα γλυκό;** — Do you want a sweet?
- You (teacher): **Τι θέλει το αρκουδάκι;** — What does the teddy want?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The kid draws a little picture menu (water, milk, tea, a sweet) for the toy café and points to it while serving.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Θέλεις νερό;**: Pour from a pretend teapot, eyebrows up.
- **θέλω**: Reach out with both hands and open and close them: gimme.
- **λίγο**: Thumb and finger almost touching.
- **ευχαριστώ**: Hand on your heart.
- **το αρκουδάκι**: Give the teddy a big hug.
- **αλλά…**: Freeze with one finger up: wait.
