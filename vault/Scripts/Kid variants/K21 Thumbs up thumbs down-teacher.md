# Kid variant · Lesson 21: Thumbs up, thumbs down

Play version of [[L21 Likes and hobbies-teacher|Lesson 21: Likes and hobbies]] · [[K21 Thumbs up thumbs down-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Taste tests, music clips, and pictures: the kid gives a thumbs up or down and says whether he likes it.

**You'll need:** a few small snacks, 3 short songs, picture cards of activities

## How to play

1. Taste a snack: thumbs up or down, and say it.
2. Play a song: dance if he likes it, freeze if he doesn't.
3. Plural: point at a pile of things (cars, apples) for μου αρέσουν.

## Lines

- T: **Σου αρέσει;** — Do you like it?
- K: **Μου αρέσει!** — I like it!
- K: **Δεν μου αρέσει!** — I don't like it!
- K: **Μου αρέσουν τα αυτοκίνητα.** — I like cars.
- K: **Μου αρέσει να τρέχω.** — I like to run.
- K: **Μου αρέσει η μουσική!** — I like the music!

## Make it

A like / don't-like collage: two halves of a page, with pictures cut out or drawn.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Interview each other about hobbies, using μου αρέσει να + verb.
