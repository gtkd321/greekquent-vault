## Today's phrases

Make the gesture every time you say the phrase. It helps the phrase stick.

| Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| **Μιλάτε αγγλικά;** | Do you speak English? | Open and close your hand by your mouth like a talking puppet, eyebrows up. |
| **Πιο σιγά, παρακαλώ.** | More slowly, please. | Press both palms down slowly. |
| **Δεν καταλαβαίνω.** | I don't understand. | Scratch your head, frowning. |
| **Μπορείτε να το πείτε ξανά;** | Can you say it again? | Circle a finger backward ("again"). |
| **Θέλω να μάθω ελληνικά.** | I want to learn Greek. | Mime opening a book, then point at the Greek flag. |
| **Μιλάω λίγα ελληνικά.** | I speak a little Greek. | Talking-puppet hand, then pinch fingers for "a little". |

## Main scene

**Do you speak Greek?**: A learner chats with a Greek speaker and uses rescue phrases when they get lost.

**New words:** πό-σο, και-ρό, μα-θαί-νε-τε, ο μήνας, ακούσω, εκεί

- A: **Συγγνώμη, μιλάτε αγγλικά;** — Excuse me, do you speak English?
- B: **Λίγο. Μιλάτε ελληνικά;** — A little. Do you speak Greek?
- A: **Μιλάω λίγα ελληνικά.** — I speak a little Greek.
- B: **Μπράβο! Από πού είστε;** — Good for you! Where are you from?
- A: **Από την Αμερική. Θέλω να μάθω ελληνικά.** — From America. I want to learn Greek.
- B: **Τι ωραία! Πόσο καιρό μαθαίνετε;** — How lovely! How long have you been learning?
- A: **Δεν καταλαβαίνω. Πιο σιγά, παρακαλώ.** — I don't understand. Slower, please.
- B: **Πό-σο και-ρό μα-θαί-νε-τε;** — How… long… have you been learning?
- A: **Α! Τρεις μήνες.** — Oh! Three months.
- B: **Μιλάτε πολύ καλά!** — You speak very well!
- A: **Μπορείτε να το πείτε ξανά; Θέλω να το ακούσω ξανά!** — Can you say that again? I want to hear it again!
- B: **Μιλάτε πολύ καλά!** — You speak very well!
- A: **Πώς λέμε «bakery» στα ελληνικά;** — How do you say "bakery" in Greek?
- B: **Φούρνος.** — Fournos.
- A: **Φούρνος. Ευχαριστώ! Ξέρετε πού είναι ο φούρνος;** — Fournos. Thank you! Do you know where the bakery is?
- B: **Ναι, εκεί!** — Yes, over there!

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Language: ελληνικά · αγγλικά · ισπανικά · γαλλικά
> - Rescue phrase: Πιο σιγά, παρακαλώ. · Ξανά, παρακαλώ. · Δεν καταλαβαίνω. · Πώς λέμε…;
> - How long: ένα μήνα · τρεις μήνες · ένα χρόνο
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Τι ώρα είναι;**
>
> **Twists to try with a partner**
>
> - The Greek speaker talks fast on purpose, and the learner has to use every rescue phrase at least once.
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Tell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (90, 60, then 45 seconds). When you're listening, just listen.

> [!example]- Build it with tiles
> Cut out your Lesson 15 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
>
> 1. Build it from the English, then read it aloud.
> 2. Turn it into a question: swap the . for ; and raise your voice at the end.
> 3. Make it negative: put δεν right before the verb.
> 4. Swap one tile for another word from the extra tiles.
> 5. Write your last version by hand in your notebook.
>
> - **A:** Excuse me, do you speak English?
> - **B:** I speak a little Greek.
> - **C:** You speak very well!
>
> Extra tiles: δεν · ; · . · ελληνικά · αγγλικά · ισπανικά · γαλλικά
