# Kid variant · Lesson 13: Stuffed-animal taverna

Play version of [[L13 At the taverna-teacher|Lesson 13: At the taverna]] · [[K13 Stuffed-animal taverna-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** The kid is the waiter with a notepad, taking orders from the toys and bringing play food.

**You'll need:** play kitchen or toy food (or play-dough food), notepad, an apron or towel

## How to play

1. Seat the toys at the table. The kid takes each order.
2. Toys order with Θα ήθελα. Kid brings the food.
3. End by asking for the bill.

## Lines

- K: **Τι θέλετε;** — What would you like?
- T (as toy): **Θα ήθελα μια σαλάτα, παρακαλώ.** — I'd like a salad, please.
- K: **Τι πίνετε;** — What are you drinking?
- T: **Νερό. Τι τρως;** — Water. What are you eating?
- K: **Τρώω ψάρι!** — I'm eating fish!
- T: **Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ!** — The bill, please!

## Make it

Make a menu with drawings of each dish, and play-dough food to go with it.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Use a real Greek taverna menu (they're easy to find online) and role-play ordering.
