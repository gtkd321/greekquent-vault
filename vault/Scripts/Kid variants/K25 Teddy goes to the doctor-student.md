# Teddy goes to the doctor

**The game:** A toy doctor's visit: point to the body part that hurts, then buy pretend pills at the pharmacy. Ends with the Easter greeting and egg tapping.

## Say it

- You: **Τι έχεις;** — What's wrong?
- Grown-up: **Με πονάει το κεφάλι!** — My head hurts!
- Grown-up: **Με πονάει το χέρι.** — My hand hurts.
- Grown-up: **Με πονάει η κοιλιά.** — My tummy hurts.
- You: **Μπορείτε να μου δώσετε κάτι για το κεφάλι;** — Can you give me something for my head?
- Grown-up: **Χριστός Ανέστη!** — Christ is risen!
- You: **Αληθώς Ανέστη!** — Truly He is risen!

## Make it

A paper outline of a body where the kid puts sticker band-aids on the part that hurts.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Χριστός Ανέστη! – Αληθώς Ανέστη!**: Pass the flame: touch your candle to the partner's.
- **Με πονάει το κεφάλι.**: Hold your head and wince.
- **Μπορείτε να μου δώσετε…;**: Open hand reaching out, palm up.
- **Έχετε κάτι για…;**: Point at the sore spot, eyebrows up.
- **Πονάει ο λαιμός μου.**: Hold your throat and swallow painfully.
