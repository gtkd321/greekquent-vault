**Greekquent** · *Θα τα πούμε.* (See you soon; literally, we will say them.) The 1,500 most common Greek words, taught through real scenes.

1,500 common Modern Greek words, one note per word, ranked by approximate frequency.

## How the links work
Each word note has an **"Often appears with"** list. Those are the words from this list that most often occur **within four words of it in the same sentence**. That's measured over 143,058 Greek sentences: about 43,000 everyday sentences from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) and 100,000 news sentences from the [Leipzig Corpora Collection](https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/Greek).
- Ranked by the logDice association score, which favors pairs that belong together over pairs that are just both common.
- The number in parentheses is how many sentences contained the pair.
- Very common grammar words (the article, και, να, pronouns and so on) are left out as link targets so they don't swamp every note. Their own notes still show their strongest partners.
- The example sentence (quote block) comes from Tatoeba. Tatoeba sentences are used under the [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/) license, credited to the Tatoeba Project and its contributors.

## Chunks and lesson scripts
Every word note now has two more sections under **"Often appears with"**:
- **Chunks**: ready-made phrases to learn as one piece instead of word by word.
  - **From your lessons**: phrases from the Year One scripts, with English and a link to the lesson.
  - **Common in everyday Greek**: the word sequences that recur most in about 43,000 everyday sentences from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org), with a real example sentence and its English translation. The number is how many sentences used that chunk. "…" means the chunk continues, as in θέλω να … (I want to …).
  - **Common in news Greek**: only for words with few everyday chunks, taken from 100,000 news sentences in the [Leipzig Corpora Collection](https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/Greek).
- **Appears in scripts**: links to every lesson script that uses the word. A word with only one script link is a good candidate to recycle in a later lesson.

> [!info]- English cognates on word notes
> 533 of the 1,500 word notes list **English cognates**, English words that share a root with the Greek word. They are memory hooks, not translations.
> - Most are words English borrowed from Greek: χρόνος (time), chronic.
> - Some are true relatives inherited from a shared ancestor language: μητέρα, mother; νύχτα, night.
> - Some are modern loans: ταξί, taxi; καφές, coffee.
> - Some are surprising: ίδιος (own, same) gives idiom and idiot, and σπίτι (house) is related to hospital.
>
> Type a cousin in Look up a word to find the Greek word. Practice spotting them with Greekquent Cousins in [[09 Class Games and App Ideas]]. Cognate hints are based on Wiktionary data (CC BY-SA) via [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org), checked by hand.
