# Kid variant · Lesson 24: Red eggs and candles

Play version of [[L24 Holy Week-teacher|Lesson 24: Holy Week]] · [[K24 Red eggs and candles-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Dye eggs red, or color paper ones, and decorate an Easter candle.

**You'll need:** boiled eggs and red dye, or paper egg cutouts; a candle or paper candle with ribbons

## How to play

1. Color the eggs together and count them.
2. Decorate the candle with ribbon and stickers.
3. Offer eggs to each other and to the toys.

## Lines

- K: **Έχω ένα κόκκινο αυγό!** — I have a red egg!
- T: **Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό;** — Do you want a red egg?
- K: **Ναι, θέλω!** — Yes, I do!
- K: **Έχω μια λαμπάδα.** — I have a candle.
- Ss: **Καλή Ανάσταση!** — Happy Resurrection! (said in Holy Week)

## Make it

The red eggs and the decorated candle.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Learn about the customs: bake a τσουρέκι together and learn the vocabulary as you go.
