# My day charades

**The game:** The kid acts out daily activities, and the other person guesses. Use θέλω να / μπορώ να as fixed chunks.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Τι κάνεις;** — What are you doing?
- You: **Θέλω να φάω!** — I want to eat!
- You: **Θέλω να παίξω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.** — I want to play, but I can't.
- Grown-up: **Γιατί;** — Why?
- You: **Γιατί είναι βράδυ!** — Because it's evening!
- Grown-up: **Το πρωί… το βράδυ…** — In the morning… in the evening…

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι κάνεις;** — What are you doing?
- You (teacher): **Γιατί;** — Why?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

A paper-plate clock with the sun on one side and the moon on the other. The kid colors the sky for each part of the day.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Τι κάνεις;**: Palms up, head tilted.
- **Θέλω να φάω!**: Fingers to your mouth: munch.
- **Θέλω να παίξω**: Throw a pretend ball.
- **αλλά δεν μπορώ**: Let your arms flop.
- **Γιατί;**: Both palms up, shoulders up.
- **πρωί**: Stretch your arms up and yawn awake.
- **βράδυ**: Hands together under your cheek: sleepy.
