Make the gesture every time you say the phrase. It helps the phrase stick.

| Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| **Τι έκανες το Σαββατοκύριακο;** | What did you do on the weekend? | Thumb over the shoulder, palms up. |
| **Πήγα στη θάλασσα.** | I went to the sea. | Step back onto the "past" line, then make waves. |
| **Είδα τη Μαρία.** | I saw Maria. | Point to your eyes, then a fingers-together wave at "Maria". |

## Main scene

**What did you do last weekend?**: Friends swap weekend news with the common "did once" past forms: πήγα, έκανα, είδα, είπα, έφαγα, ήπια.

**New words:** ποιον, έφαγε, παγωτό, έφαγες, ότι

- A: **Τι έκανες το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What did you do last weekend?
- B: **Το Σάββατο πήγα στη θάλασσα.** — On Saturday I went to the beach.
- A: **Ωραία! Με ποιον;** — Nice! Who with?
- B: **Με τη Μαρία. Είδα και τον Νίκο εκεί!** — With Maria. I saw Nikos there too!
- A: **Αλήθεια; Τι είπε;** — Really? What did he say?
- B: **Είπε «Γεια σας!» και μετά έφαγε ένα παγωτό.** — He said "Hi!" and then he ate an ice cream.
- A: **Χα! Κι εσύ; Τι έφαγες;** — Ha! And you? What did you eat?
- B: **Έφαγα ψάρι και ήπια μια μπίρα. Εσύ τι έκανες;** — I ate fish and had a beer. What did you do?
- A: **Είχα πολλή δουλειά. Την Κυριακή πήγα στην εκκλησία και μετά ήπια έναν καφέ με τη γιαγιά μου.** — I had a lot of work. On Sunday I went to church and then had a coffee with my grandmother.
- B: **Πώς είναι η γιαγιά σου;** — How's your grandmother?
- A: **Καλά! Είπε ότι θέλει να σε δει.** — Good! She said she wants to see you.
- B: **Κι εγώ!** — Me too!

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Where: πήγα στη θάλασσα · πήγα στην εκκλησία · πήγα στη λαϊκή
> - What: είδα… · έφαγα… · ήπια… · έκανα… · είπα…
> - Who: με τη Μαρία · με τον Νίκο · με την οικογένεια
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Χθες ήθελα να πάω, αλλά δεν μπορούσα.**
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Tell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Tell it as yesterday (ήθελα, πήγα…) or as tomorrow (θα…).
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). When you're listening, just listen.

> [!example]- Build it with tiles
> Cut out your Lesson 23 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
>
> 1. Build it from the English, then read it aloud.
> 2. Turn it into a question: swap the . for ; and raise your voice at the end.
> 3. Make it negative: put δεν right before the verb.
> 4. Swap one tile for another word from the extra tiles.
> 5. Write your last version by hand in your notebook.
>
> - **A:** What did you do last weekend?
> - **B:** On Saturday I went to the beach.
> - **C:** How's your grandmother?
>
> Extra tiles: δεν · ; · . · πήγα · στη θάλασσα · στην εκκλησία · στη λαϊκή
