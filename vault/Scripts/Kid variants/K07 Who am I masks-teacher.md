# Kid variant · Lesson 7: Who am I? masks

Play version of [[L07 Introductions and Christmas-teacher|Lesson 7: Introductions and Christmas]] · [[K07 Who am I masks-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Paper-plate masks turn the kid into a character, and he introduces himself as that character.

**You'll need:** paper plates, markers, string or a craft stick; a jingle bell for the κάλαντα

## How to play

1. Kid makes a mask of a superhero, an animal, or a family member.
2. Wearing it, he introduces himself. Teacher guesses who it is.
3. Christmas: ring the bell and sing the opening of the κάλαντα together; the kid shakes the bell on every line.

## Lines

- T: **Ποιος είσαι;** — Who are you?
- K: **Είμαι ο Spider-Man!** — I'm Spider-Man!
- T: **Είσαι από την Αμερική;** — Are you from America?
- K: **Ναι, είμαι από την Αμερική.** — Yes, I'm from America.
- K: **Είμαι η γιαγιά! Είμαι από την Ελλάδα.** — I'm Grandma! I'm from Greece.
- Ss: **Καλά Χριστούγεννα!** — Merry Christmas!

## Make it

The mask, plus a little name tag in Greek letters.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Sticky-note guessing game: a famous name goes on your forehead, and you ask yes/no questions (Είμαι από την Ελλάδα; Είμαι ηθοποιός;).
