# 00 Greekquent Index

**Greekquent** · Θα τα πούμε. The 1,500 most common Greek words, taught through real scenes.

**Start here:** [[Today]] · [[Setting up]] · [[What's New]] · [[11 Classroom Greek]]

> [!summary]- Lessons
> - Your lesson pages are in **Scripts/Beginner**, one per class. The whole year is also one printable workbook on the [[Print and download]] page, with the textbook, the Verb Sheet, Sentence Tiles and the flashcard decks.
> - Verbs: [[02 Verb Tables]].
> - Five words a day for a year: [[07 Fluent in a Year]].

> [!summary]- Words
> - Every one of the 1,500 words has a note in the **Words** folder: meaning, how to say it, an example sentence, words it often appears with, and ready-made phrases (chunks).
> - Tap **Hear it** to hear the word and the color buttons to mark how well you know it.
> - **Word web** (More menu, or the button on a word note) shows up to 9 words that go with it. Tap a word to move to it, hold a word to see its links and hear the pairing.
> - About a third of the words have **English cognates**, related English words (χρόνος, chronic). They are memory hooks, not translations.
> - The words in rank order, 150 per page, are in the **Word lists** folder.

> [!summary]- Your work and feedback
> - Type in the **My Work** folder. Updates never change it.
> - **Send homework** (More menu) texts the newest dated section of [[My Work/Homework|Homework]] to the teacher.
> - Every look-up is saved in [[My Work/Words I looked up|Words I looked up]], with the Greek.
> - Chunks and sentences you add to word notes are saved in [[My Work/My word cards|My word cards]].
> - Spotted a mistake or have a question? More, then **Ask the class**, by text or video. Texts to the teacher are for homework only.

> [!info]- Where the data comes from
> - "Often appears with" counts words found within four words of each other in 143,058 Greek sentences: about 43,000 everyday sentences from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) and 100,000 news sentences from the [Leipzig Corpora Collection](https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/Greek), ranked by the logDice score.
> - Example sentences come from Tatoeba under [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/), credited to the Tatoeba Project and its contributors.
> - Cognate hints are based on Wiktionary data (CC BY-SA) via [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org), checked by hand.
