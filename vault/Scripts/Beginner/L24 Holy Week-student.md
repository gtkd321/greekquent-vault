## Main scene

**Getting ready for Easter**: Friends talk about candles, red eggs, and τσουρέκι during Holy Week.

**New words:** λαμπάδες, βάφεις

- A: **Καλή Ανάσταση!** — Have a blessed Resurrection!
- B: **Καλή Ανάσταση! Έχεις λαμπάδα για την Ανάσταση;** — Have a blessed Resurrection! Do you have a candle for the Resurrection service?
- A: **Ναι, έχω μια άσπρη λαμπάδα. Τα παιδιά έχουν λαμπάδες με χρώματα.** — Yes, I have a white candle. The kids have colorful ones.
- B: **Βάφεις αυγά;** — Do you dye eggs?
- A: **Ναι, τη Μεγάλη Πέμπτη βάφουμε κόκκινα αυγά. Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό;** — Yes, on Holy Thursday we dye red eggs. Do you want a red egg?
- B: **Ναι, θέλω! Και τσουρέκι;** — Yes, I do! And tsoureki?
- A: **Έχω τσουρέκι από τη γιαγιά. Είναι πολύ νόστιμο.** — I have tsoureki from Grandma. It's delicious.
- B: **Πού θα είσαι το Πάσχα;** — Where will you be for Easter?
- A: **Θα είμαι εδώ, στην εκκλησία. Εσύ;** — I'll be here, at church. You?
- B: **Θα πάω στο χωριό.** — I'll go to the village.
- A: **Καλό Πάσχα!** — Happy Easter!
- B: **Καλό Πάσχα!** — Happy Easter!

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Item: μια λαμπάδα · κόκκινα αυγά · τσουρέκι · λουλούδια
> - Day: τη Μεγάλη Πέμπτη · τη Μεγάλη Παρασκευή · το Μεγάλο Σάββατο · την Κυριακή του Πάσχα
> - Wish: Καλή Ανάσταση! · Καλό Πάσχα!
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Τι έκανες το Σαββατοκύριακο;**
>
> **Twists to try with a partner**
>
> - Real egg-tapping (τσούγκρισμα) at the end. Whoever's egg survives says the greeting first.
> - Holy Week calendar: put the day cards in order, then say what happens on each day.
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Tell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Tell it as yesterday (ήθελα, πήγα…) or as tomorrow (θα…).
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). When you're listening, just listen.

> [!example]- Build it with tiles
> Cut out your Lesson 24 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
>
> 1. Build it from the English, then read it aloud.
> 2. Turn it into a question: swap the . for ; and raise your voice at the end.
> 3. Make it negative: put δεν right before the verb.
> 4. Swap one tile for another word from the extra tiles.
> 5. Write your last version by hand in your notebook.
>
> - **A:** Where will you be for Easter?
> - **B:** I'll go to the village.
> - **C:** I have an Easter candle.
>
