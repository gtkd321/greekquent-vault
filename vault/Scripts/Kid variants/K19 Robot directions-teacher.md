# Kid variant · Lesson 19: Robot directions

Play version of [[L19 March 25th and directions-teacher|Lesson 19: March 25th and directions]] · [[K19 Robot directions-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** The teacher is a robot, and the kid guides it to a hidden flag using only Greek directions.

**You'll need:** a paper Greek flag (blue and white) hidden in the room; optional blindfold

## How to play

1. Hide the flag. The robot walks only when told.
2. Kid gives directions: right, left, straight, stop.
3. Swap: the kid is the robot.

## Lines

- T: **Πού είναι η σημαία;** — Where is the flag?
- K: **Ευθεία!** — Straight ahead!
- K: **Δεξιά! Αριστερά!** — Right! Left!
- K: **Σταμάτα!** — Stop!
- T: **Δεν βλέπω τη σημαία!** — I can't see the flag!
- K: **Είναι κοντά!** — It's close!

## Make it

Make the Greek flag together for the 25th of March: blue and white stripes, with a cross in the corner.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Give real directions on a printed map of an Athens neighborhood: Μπορείτε να μου πείτε πού είναι η πλατεία;
