# Shoebox house and color hunt

**The game:** Draw or build a house, describe it with colors and sizes, then race to find colors around the room.

## Say it

- You: **Έχω ένα μικρό σπίτι.** — I have a small house.
- You: **Η κουζίνα είναι κίτρινη.** — The kitchen is yellow.
- Grown-up: **Βρες κάτι κόκκινο!** — Find something red!
- You: **Ένα κόκκινο αυτοκίνητο!** — A red car!
- You: **Μια κόκκινη μπάλα!** — A red ball!
- You: **Βλέπω ένα μεγάλο δωμάτιο.** — I see a big room.

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Βρες κάτι κόκκινο!** — Find something red!
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The shoebox house, painted and colored, with a room for every color.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **μικρό**: Pinch your fingers close: tiny.
- **μεγάλο**: Arms out wide: big.
- **Η κουζίνα**: Stir a pretend pot.
- **Βρες κάτι κόκκινο!**: Hand over your eyes, then run and look.
- **κόκκινο αυτοκίνητο**: Drive the red car through the air.
- **κόκκινη μπάλα**: Bounce the red ball.
