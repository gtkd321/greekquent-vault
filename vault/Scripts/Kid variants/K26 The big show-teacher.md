# Kid variant · Lesson 26: The big show

Play version of [[L26 Capstone and celebration-teacher|Lesson 26: Capstone and celebration]] · [[K26 The big show-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** The kid puts on a short show for the family with his toys, using his favorite scenes from the year.

**You'll need:** toys, the masks, menus, kite, and comics from the year

## How to play

1. Kid picks three favorite scenes (the café, Spider-Man, the market…).
2. He performs them for the family with the toys.
3. Finish with a certificate he colors.

## Lines

- K: **Γεια σας! Με λένε ___.** — Hello! My name is ___.
- K: **Θέλετε νερό;** — Would you like water?
- K: **Πού είναι ο Spider-Man;** — Where is Spider-Man?
- K: **Θα πάω στη θάλασσα!** — I'll go to the sea!
- Ss: **Μπράβο!** — Bravo!

## Make it

A certificate, colored and signed: Μπράβο!

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** The same three-scenario capstone from the main lesson, performed for friends or family.
