## Group meet prompt

**Lost and found**

- **Πού είναι το κλειδί μου;** (Where is my key?)
- **Δεν έχω το κινητό μου. Το έχεις εσύ;** (I don't have my phone. Do you have it?)

**Bridge:** One person hides an object. The others ask with μου/σου until they find it.
