## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 12 new words on their printed list: θα (7) · αμέσως (219) · το άτομο (319) · ο λογαριασμός (572) · η μπίρα (654) · καλησπέρα (677) · το ψάρι (798) · τρώω (902) · το ποτήρι (951) · το κρέας (1078) · η σαλάτα (1403) · καλή όρεξη (1415)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'I would like…'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Θα ήθελα…**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Θα ήθελα…** | I would like… | Hand on chest, then a polite palm-up "may I". |
> > | **Έχετε ψάρι σήμερα;** | Do you have fish today? | Swim one flat hand like a fish. |
> > | **Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ.** | The bill, please. | Mime signing in the air toward the waiter. |
> > | **Πολύ νόστιμο!** | Very tasty! | Kiss your fingertips, then pat your stomach. |
> > | **Καλή όρεξη!** | Enjoy your meal! | Both hands sweep over the table, offering the food. |
>
> **Step 1: τρώω and πίνω**  
> T: (mimes) Τρώω ψωμί. Πίνω νερό.  
>
> > [!info]- Board: τρώω (I eat) and πίνω (I drink)
> > | | τρώω (eat) | πίνω (drink) |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | I | τρώω | πίνω |
> > | you | τρως | πίνεις |
> > | he, she, it | τρώει | πίνει |
> > | we | τρώμε | πίνουμε |
> > | you all / polite | τρώτε | πίνετε |
> > | they | τρώνε | πίνουν |
> >
> > Write this on the board at this step. Full tables: [[τρώω]] · [[πίνω]] · [[02 Verb Tables]]
>
> T: Τι τρως το πρωί; — S1: Τρώω ψωμί. T: Τι πίνεις; — S1: Πίνω καφέ.  
> **Step 2: the menu**  
> T: (points) σαλάτα, ψάρι, κρέας, πατάτες, κρασί, μπίρα.  
> T: Τρως κρέας; — S1: Ναι, τρώω. / Όχι, δεν τρώω κρέας.  
> **Step 3: Θα ήθελα**  
> T: Θέλω ψάρι. — Ss: Θα ήθελα ψάρι.  
> T: Θέλω μια σαλάτα. — Ss: Θα ήθελα μια σαλάτα.  
> **Step 4: around the meal**  
> T: Καλή όρεξη! · Πολύ νόστιμο! · Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ.  
> **Warm-up dialogue: ordering**  
> Waiter: Καλησπέρα! Τι θα πάρετε;  
> A: Έχετε ψάρι σήμερα;  
> Waiter: Ναι, έχουμε φρέσκο ψάρι.  
> A: Θα ήθελα ένα ψάρι και μια σαλάτα.  
> Waiter: Και τι θα πιείτε;  
> A: Ένα κρασί, παρακαλώ.  
> Waiter: Καλή όρεξη!  
> A: (later) Πολύ νόστιμο! Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ.

## 3 · Main scene

**Dinner at the taverna**: Two friends order dinner at a taverna and ask for the bill.

**Familiarity:** 93% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** το άτομο, το ποτήρι, σουβλάκι, αμέσως

- Waiter: **Καλησπέρα! Πόσα άτομα;** — Good evening! How many people?
- A: **Δύο, παρακαλώ.** — Two, please.
- Waiter: **Ελάτε. Τι θα πιείτε;** — Come this way. What will you drink?
- A: **Θα ήθελα ένα ποτήρι κρασί.** — I'd like a glass of wine.
- B: **Εγώ νερό, παρακαλώ.** — Water for me, please.
- Waiter: **Και να φάτε;** — And to eat?
- A: **Έχετε ψάρι σήμερα;** — Do you have fish today?
- Waiter: **Ναι, έχουμε φρέσκο ψάρι.** — Yes, we have fresh fish.
- A: **Θα ήθελα ψάρι και μια σαλάτα.** — I'd like the fish and a salad.
- B: **Εγώ δεν τρώω ψάρι. Θα ήθελα κρέας, ένα σουβλάκι.** — I don't eat fish. I'd like meat, a souvlaki.
- Waiter: **Πολύ ωραία.** — Very good.
- Waiter (with the food): **Καλή όρεξη!** — Enjoy your meal!
- B: **Πολύ νόστιμο!** — Delicious!
- A: **Ναι! Το ψάρι είναι πολύ νόστιμο.** — Yes! The fish is delicious.
- A: **Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ.** — The bill, please.
- Waiter: **Αμέσως.** — Right away.

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Drink: ένα ποτήρι κρασί · νερό · μια μπίρα · μια πορτοκαλάδα
> - Food: ψάρι · κρέας · μια σαλάτα · ένα σουβλάκι · πατάτες
> - Reaction: Πολύ νόστιμο! · Τέλειο! · Καλή όρεξη!
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Πόσο κάνουν;**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - The waiter keeps saying Δυστυχώς δεν έχουμε… so the customers have to order three times.
> - Waiter role: the waiter writes the orders down in Greek and reads them back to check.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (90, 60, then 45 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "I'd like a glass of wine." · B: "And to eat?" · C: "Yes, we have fresh fish."
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Order a full meal with Θα ήθελα…. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - At each meal this week, say what you're eating and drinking: Τρώω…, Πίνω….
> - Read a real Greek taverna menu online and pick three dishes.
>
> **Out in the world:** Eat at a Greek restaurant if there's one nearby, and order one thing in Greek (Θα ήθελα…). If not, read a real Greek menu online and write your order by hand.
>
> **Watch-list:** τρώω is TRO-o, with two clear o's. κρέας is KRE-as. λογαριασμός is lo-ghar-ya-ZMOS. Students say "θέλω" to the waiter; nudge them toward Θα ήθελα.
