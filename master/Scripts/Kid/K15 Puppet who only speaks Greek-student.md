# Puppet who only speaks Greek

**The game:** A sock puppet doesn't speak English, so the kid has to slow it down, ask it to repeat, and teach it words.

## Say it

- Grown-up (puppet): **Γεια! Μιλάς αγγλικά;** — Hi! Do you speak English?
- You: **Ναι! Μιλάς ελληνικά;** — Yes! Do you speak Greek?
- You: **Πιο σιγά, παρακαλώ!** — Slower, please!
- You: **Ξανά, παρακαλώ!** — Again, please!
- You: **Δεν καταλαβαίνω!** — I don't understand!
- Grown-up (puppet): **Δεν ξέρω αγγλικά…** — I don't know English…

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the puppet. Talk to the grown-up.

- You (teacher): **Γεια! Μιλάς αγγλικά;** — Hi! Do you speak English?
- You (teacher): **Ναι! Μιλάς ελληνικά;** — Yes! Do you speak Greek?
- You (puppet): **Γεια! Μιλάς αγγλικά;** — Hi! Do you speak English?
- You (puppet): **Δεν ξέρω αγγλικά…** — I don't know English…
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

Making the puppet: a sock, buttons, yarn hair.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Γεια!**: Wave the puppet.
- **Μιλάς**: Open and close your hand by your mouth, like a puppet talking.
- **Πιο σιγά**: Press both palms down slowly.
- **Ξανά**: Roll one finger backward in a circle.
- **Δεν καταλαβαίνω!**: Scratch your head.
- **Δεν ξέρω**: Shrug, palms up.
