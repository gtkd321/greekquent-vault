# Greekquent Groups

**Real Greek, real people, real conversation.** Θα τα πούμε.

You have learned the words. Now use them. Greekquent Groups are small live conversations where you talk with other Greekquent students. Your teacher guides the conversation and keeps it moving, but you and the other students do most of the talking.

> [!tip]- Why join
> - **More speaking time.** In a lesson you answer your teacher. In a group you have to keep a conversation going, the way you will in Greece.
> - **Real situations.** Each session builds on the scenario you are studying: ordering at a taverna, visiting a Greek home, asking for directions, shopping and making plans.
> - **Just hard enough.** The topics are set so you get most of it right, and the parts you miss show you what to work on next.
> - **People to practice with.** You will meet other learners at your level, and you can keep practicing with them between sessions.
> - **Extra practice.** Groups don't replace your regular lessons. They give you more practice on top of them.

> [!info]- How to unlock it
> You can join once you reach one of these:
> - you have finished all 26 lessons of Year One
> - you have reached a Fluent in a Year milestone (750 words)
> - your teacher invites you
>
> Not there yet? Keep going. Every lesson and every set of 5 words gets you closer.

> [!example]- What a session looks like
> - **Size:** 4 to 6 students, so everyone gets to talk.
> - **Length:** about an hour.
> - **Format:**
>   - a quick warm-up
>   - a scenario or discussion prompt
>   - conversation in pairs and as a whole group
>   - a short wrap-up with useful phrases to take home
> - **Bring:** your textbook and planner, plus any words you marked red or yellow this week.
