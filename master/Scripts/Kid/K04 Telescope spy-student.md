# Telescope spy

**The game:** The kid spies on the room through a paper telescope and reports what he sees, practicing το / τον / τη.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Τι βλέπεις;** — What do you see?
- You: **Βλέπω το τραπέζι.** — I see the table.
- You: **Βλέπω τον Spider-Man!** — I see Spider-Man!
- You: **Βλέπω τη γάτα.** — I see the cat.
- You: **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα και ένα σπίτι.** — I see the sea and a house.
- Grown-up: **Βλέπεις τον ήλιο;** — Do you see the sun?

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι βλέπεις;** — What do you see?
- You (teacher): **Βλέπεις τον ήλιο;** — Do you see the sun?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The telescope itself: stripes, stars, his name in Greek letters he knows so far.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Τι βλέπεις;**: Lift the telescope to your eye.
- **Βλέπω**: Point through the telescope.
- **τη γάτα**: Claws up: meow.
- **τη θάλασσα**: Make waves with one hand.
- **ένα σπίτι**: Hands make a roof over your head.
- **τον ήλιο**: Arms make a big circle over your head.
