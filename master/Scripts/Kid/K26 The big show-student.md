# The big show

**The game:** The kid puts on a short show for the family with his toys, using his favorite scenes from the year.

## Say it

- You: **Γεια σας! Με λένε ___.** — Hello! My name is ___.
- You: **Θέλετε νερό;** — Would you like water?
- You: **Πού είναι ο Spider-Man;** — Where is Spider-Man?
- You: **Θα πάω στη θάλασσα!** — I'll go to the sea!
- Everyone: **Μπράβο!** — Bravo!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Run the game and tell the grown-up what to say.

- You (teacher): **Θέλετε νερό;** — Would you like water?
- You (teacher): **Πού είναι ο Spider-Man;** — Where is Spider-Man?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

A certificate, colored and signed: Μπράβο!

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Γεια σας!**: Big wave to the whole room.
- **Με λένε**: Hand flat on your chest.
- **Θέλετε νερό;**: Pour from a pretend teapot.
- **Πού είναι**: Palms up, look left and right.
- **στη θάλασσα**: Swim with your arms.
- **Μπράβο!**: Everyone claps.
