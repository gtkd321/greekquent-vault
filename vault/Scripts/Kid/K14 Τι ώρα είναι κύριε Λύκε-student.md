# Τι ώρα είναι, κύριε Λύκε;

**The game:** Mr. Wolf, in Greek: kids ask the time, take that many steps, and get chased at dinnertime.

## Say it

- You: **Τι ώρα είναι, κύριε Λύκε;** — What time is it, Mr. Wolf?
- Grown-up: **Είναι τρεις η ώρα!** — It's three o'clock!
- You: **Ένα, δύο, τρία!** — One, two, three!
- Grown-up: **Είναι ώρα για φαγητό!** — It's dinnertime!
- Grown-up: **Μπορείς την Τρίτη;** — Can you on Tuesday?
- You: **Ναι, την Τρίτη μπορώ!** — Yes, on Tuesday I can!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι ώρα είναι, κύριε Λύκε;** — What time is it, Mr. Wolf?
- You (teacher): **Μπορείς την Τρίτη;** — Can you on Tuesday?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The paper-plate clock with numbers and hands.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Τι ώρα είναι;**: Tap your wrist, eyebrows up.
- **Μπορείς την Τρίτη στις έξι;**: Count two, then six, on your fingers with the palms facing you, eyebrows up.
- **στις έξι και μισή**: Count six with the palms facing you, then cut the air in half.
- **και τέταρτο**: Draw a quarter circle on a pretend clock.
- **Είναι νωρίς. / Είναι αργά.**: Yawn and stretch for νωρίς; tap the wrist anxiously for αργά.
