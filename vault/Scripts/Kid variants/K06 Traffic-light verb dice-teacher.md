# Kid variant · Lesson 6: Traffic-light verb dice

Play version of [[L06 Coffee plans at the kafeneio-teacher|Lesson 6: Coffee plans at the kafeneio]] · [[K06 Traffic-light verb dice-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Roll a homemade die for the verb, then draw a color for the kind of sentence, using your red/yellow/green colors.

**You'll need:** a paper die (or a dice template) with 4 pictures: bag = έχω, heart = θέλω, strong arm = μπορώ, eye = βλέπω; two faces say "your pick"; green, red, and yellow cards

## How to play

1. Roll the die for the verb.
2. Draw a color: green = a yes sentence, red = a δεν sentence, yellow = a question.
3. Kid says the sentence; if it's right, he keeps the card. Most cards wins.

## Lines

- K (green): **Έχω καφέ.** — I have coffee.
- K (red): **Δεν θέλω ψωμί.** — I don't want bread.
- K (yellow): **Μπορείς αύριο;** — Can you tomorrow?
- K (yellow): **Βλέπεις τη θάλασσα;** — Do you see the sea?
- T: **Μπράβο!** — Well done!

## Make it

Build the die together and let the kid draw the four pictures. The colors match the laminated packet, so the game teaches the system too.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Same game, against a timer. Adults can add a word card so every sentence needs a new noun.
