# καληνύχτα

**good night** · say it: kaliníhta · rank **#417** of 1,500

> Καληνύχτα, μαμά.

> [!tip]- When to say it
> A goodbye only, late, when people go to bed. Arriving in the evening is Καλησπέρα.
> [Play Τι λέμε;](obsidian://greekquent?open=hello) to practice hello and goodbye through the day.
