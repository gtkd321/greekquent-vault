## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 6 new words on their printed list: μόνο (49) · ούτε (120) · η εβδομάδα (303) · η λέξη (346) · το μάθημα (724) · φέτος (1358)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'I would like…, please.'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Θα ήθελα…, παρακαλώ.**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Θα ήθελα…, παρακαλώ.** | I would like…, please. | Hand on chest, then a polite palm-up gesture. |
> > | **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.** | I want to, but I can't. | Reach out eagerly, then pull back and shake your head. |
> > | **Πόσο κάνουν…;** | How much are…? | Rub thumb and fingers over the whole pile. |
> > | **Φέτος έμαθα…** | This year I learned… | Point down at the floor ("this year"), then tap your temple. |
> > | **Το καλοκαίρι θα πάω…** | This summer I'll go… | Fan your face, then roll a hand forward. |
> > | **Καλό καλοκαίρι!** | Have a good summer! | Push imaginary sunglasses up, then wave with the fingers together. |
>
> **Prep (5 min)**  
> **Scene 1: the café**  
> Waiter: Καλημέρα! Τι θα πάρετε;  
> A: Θα ήθελα ___ και ___, παρακαλώ.  
> Waiter: Τίποτα άλλο;  
> A: ___ / Όχι, ευχαριστώ.  
> **Scene 2: making plans**  
> A: Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;  
> B: Θα ___. Θέλεις να έρθεις;  
> A: Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ το ___. Μπορώ την ___ στις ___.  
> **Scene 3: the market**  
> A: Πόσο κάνουν ___;  
> Seller: ___ ευρώ το κιλό.  
> A: ___, παρακαλώ.  
> **Reading card (each student reads two lines)**  
> Reading: Πέρσι δεν ήξερα ελληνικά. Δεν μπορούσα να διαβάσω ούτε μία λέξη.  
> Reading: Φέτος πήγα στο μάθημα κάθε εβδομάδα. Έμαθα το αλφάβητο, τα ρήματα και πολλές λέξεις.  
> Reading: Τώρα μπορώ να πω «Θα ήθελα έναν καφέ» και «Πόσο κάνουν οι ελιές;».  
> Reading: Το καλοκαίρι θα πάω στην Ελλάδα. Θα μιλάω μόνο ελληνικά!  
> **Celebration**  
> T: Μπράβο σε όλους! Καλό καλοκαίρι! — Ss: Καλό καλοκαίρι!  
> **Exit ticket:** Each student says one sentence: Φέτος έμαθα ___. Το καλοκαίρι θα ___.

## 3 · Main scene

**Capstone: three scenes**: Students perform three short scenes (café, plans, market), then celebrate the year.

**Familiarity:** 99% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** μπακλαβά

- Waiter: **Καλημέρα! Τι θέλετε;** — Good morning! What would you like?
- A: **Έναν καφέ και ένα νερό, παρακαλώ.** — A coffee and a water, please.
- Waiter: **Τίποτα άλλο;** — Anything else?
- A: **Έχετε γλυκό;** — Do you have anything sweet?
- Waiter: **Ναι, έχουμε μπακλαβά.** — Yes, we have baklava.
- A: **Ένα, παρακαλώ.** — One, please.
- B: **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What will you do this weekend?
- A: **Θα πάω στη θάλασσα. Θέλεις να έρθεις;** — I'll go to the beach. Do you want to come?
- B: **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ το Σάββατο. Έχω δουλειά. Την Κυριακή μπορώ.** — I'd like to, but I can't on Saturday. I have work. I can on Sunday.
- A: **Τέλεια, την Κυριακή στις δέκα!** — Perfect, Sunday at ten!
- A: **Πόσο κάνουν οι ελιές;** — How much are the olives?
- Seller: **Πέντε ευρώ το κιλό.** — Five euros a kilo.
- A: **Μισό κιλό, παρακαλώ.** — Half a kilo, please.
- Seller: **Ορίστε. Τίποτα άλλο;** — Here you go. Anything else?
- A: **Όχι, ευχαριστώ. Καλή σας μέρα!** — No, thank you. Have a good day!
- T: **Μπράβο σε όλους! Καλό καλοκαίρι!** — Well done, everyone! Have a great summer!
- Ss: **Καλό καλοκαίρι!** — Have a great summer!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Café: καφέ · τσάι · νερό · γλυκό
> - Plans: θα πάω… · θα δω… · θα φάμε…
> - Market: ελιές · ντομάτες · ένα κιλό · μισό κιλό
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Χριστός Ανέστη! – Αληθώς Ανέστη!**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - The teacher adds one surprise problem to each scene, and the students solve it live.
> - Family audience: the families ask the students one question each in Greek.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Time shift: as yesterday (ήθελα, πήγα…) or as tomorrow (θα…).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "A coffee and a water, please." · B: "What will you do this weekend?" · C: "How much are the olives?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Each student says one sentence: Φέτος έμαθα ___. Το καλοκαίρι θα ___. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Over the summer, re-read one main scene a week.
> - Keep using the Anki deck, even 5 minutes a day.
> - Record your reading card now, then again in September.
>
> **Out in the world:** Plan a real outing in Greek for the summer: a Greek festival, a Greek restaurant, or a trip. Write the plan by hand, and keep your word list. It shows how far you've come.
>
> **Watch-list:** Don't correct during performances. Write notes, and give each student one praise and one tip afterward.
