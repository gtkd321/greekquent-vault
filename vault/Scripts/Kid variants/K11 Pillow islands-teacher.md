# Kid variant · Lesson 11: Pillow islands

Play version of [[L11 Going places-teacher|Lesson 11: Going places]] · [[K11 Pillow islands-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Pillows or paper signs around the room become places. Say where you're going, then jump there.

**You'll need:** pillows or paper signs: θάλασσα, σπίτι, σχολείο, εκκλησία, πάρκο; a toy car

## How to play

1. Kid stands on one island. Teacher asks where he's going; he names a place and jumps (or drives the car) there.
2. Then he invites you: Πάμε!
3. Use the σε + article form every time (στο / στη / στον).

## Lines

- T: **Πού πας;** — Where are you going?
- K: **Πάω στη θάλασσα!** — I'm going to the sea!
- K: **Πάω στο πάρκο.** — I'm going to the park.
- K: **Θέλω να πάω στο σπίτι.** — I want to go home.
- K: **Πάμε!** — Let's go!
- T: **Μπορούμε να πάμε στο σχολείο;** — Can we go to school?

## Make it

The kid draws each island's sign with a picture (waves, a church dome, a swing).

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Use a map of a Greek village or island and "travel" between places with a token.
