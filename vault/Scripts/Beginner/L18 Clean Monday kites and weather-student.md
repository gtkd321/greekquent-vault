## Today's phrases

Make the gesture every time you say the phrase. It helps the phrase stick.

| Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| **Τι καιρό κάνει;** | What's the weather like? | Look up at the sky, palm up. |
| **Κάνει κρύο.** | It's cold. | Hug yourself and shiver. |
| **Κάνει ζέστη.** | It's hot. | Fan your face. |
| **Βρέχει.** | It's raining. | Wiggle your fingers downward like rain. |
| **Έχει αέρα.** | It's windy. | Sway like a tree and blow. |
| **Βλέπω τον χαρταετό!** | I see the kite! | Shade your eyes and tug a kite string. |

## Main scene

**Clean Monday kites**: Friends check the weather and fly kites on Καθαρά Δευτέρα.

**New words:** μεγάλος, λόφο, χαλβά, πειράζει

- A: **Καλή Σαρακοστή!** — Have a good Lent!
- B: **Καλή Σαρακοστή! Τι καιρό κάνει σήμερα;** — Have a good Lent! What's the weather like today?
- A: **Κάνει λίγο κρύο, αλλά έχει ήλιο.** — It's a little cold, but it's sunny.
- B: **Έχει αέρα;** — Is it windy?
- A: **Ναι, έχει πολύ αέρα! Είναι καλή μέρα για χαρταετό.** — Yes, it's very windy! It's a good day for a kite.
- B: **Έχεις χαρταετό;** — Do you have a kite?
- A: **Ναι, έχω έναν μεγάλο χαρταετό. Πάμε στον λόφο!** — Yes, I have a big kite. Let's go to the hill!
- B: **Πάμε! Θέλω λαγάνα και ελιές.** — Let's go! I want lagana and olives.
- A: **Έχω λαγάνα, ελιές και χαλβά.** — I have lagana, olives, and halva.
- B (on the hill): **Βλέπω τον χαρταετό σου! Είναι πολύ ψηλά!** — I see your kite! It's really high!
- A: **Ωχ, βρέχει!** — Oh no, it's raining!
- B: **Δεν πειράζει. Πάμε στο σπίτι. Κάνει κρύο τώρα.** — Never mind. Let's go home. It's cold now.

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Weather: Κάνει κρύο. · Κάνει ζέστη. · Βρέχει. · Έχει ήλιο. · Έχει αέρα.
> - Food: λαγάνα · ελιές · χαλβάς · ταραμοσαλάτα
> - Place: στον λόφο · στο πάρκο · στη θάλασσα
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;**
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Tell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Tell it as tomorrow, with θα.
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). When you're listening, just listen.

> [!example]- Build it with tiles
> Cut out your Lesson 18 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
>
> 1. Build it from the English, then read it aloud.
> 2. Turn it into a question: swap the . for ; and raise your voice at the end.
> 3. Make it negative: put δεν right before the verb.
> 4. Swap one tile for another word from the extra tiles.
> 5. Write your last version by hand in your notebook.
>
> - **A:** It's a little cold, but it's sunny.
> - **B:** I have lagana, olives, and halva.
> - **C:** What's the weather like?
>
> Extra tiles: δεν · ; · . · κάνει · κρύο · ζέστη · βρέχει · έχει · ήλιο
