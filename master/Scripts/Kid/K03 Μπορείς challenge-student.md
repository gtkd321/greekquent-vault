# Μπορείς; challenge

**The game:** Quick physical challenges. Easy ones get Ναι, μπορώ! and impossible ones get the keystone sentence.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Μπορείς;** — Can you?
- You: **Ναι, μπορώ!** — Yes, I can!
- Grown-up: **Μπορεί!** — He can!
- You: **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ!** — I want to, but I can't!
- You: **Μπορώ;** — May I?
- Grown-up: **Ναι, βέβαια.** — Yes, of course.

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Μπορείς;** — Can you?
- You (teacher): **Μπορώ;** — May I?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

A sticker chart with a strong-arm picture at the top. One sticker per Μπορώ; he asks correctly.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Μπορείς;**: Point at the other person, eyebrows up.
- **Ναι, μπορώ!**: Strong arms, fists up.
- **Μπορεί!**: Point at him and clap.
- **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ!**: Reach out with both hands, then let them flop down.
- **Μπορώ;**: Hand up, like at school.
- **Ναι, βέβαια.**: Thumbs up.
