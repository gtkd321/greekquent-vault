# Red eggs and candles

**The game:** Dye eggs red, or color paper ones, and decorate an Easter candle.

## Say it

- You: **Έχω ένα κόκκινο αυγό!** — I have a red egg!
- Grown-up: **Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό;** — Do you want a red egg?
- You: **Ναι, θέλω!** — Yes, I do!
- You: **Έχω μια λαμπάδα.** — I have a candle.
- Ss: **Καλή Ανάσταση!** — Happy Resurrection! (said in Holy Week)

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό;** — Do you want a red egg?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The red eggs and the decorated candle.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Καλή Ανάσταση!**: Hold up an imaginary lit candle.
- **Καλό Πάσχα!**: Crack an imaginary red egg against a partner's.
- **Έχω μια λαμπάδα.**: Hold a tall candle and cup the flame.
- **Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό;**: Offer a cupped red egg.
