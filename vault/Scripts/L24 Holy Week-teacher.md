## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 2 new words on their printed list: το αυγό (1283) · το Πάσχα (1484)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'Blessed Resurrection! (Holy Week greeting)'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Καλή Ανάσταση!**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Καλή Ανάσταση!** | Blessed Resurrection! (Holy Week greeting) | Hold up an imaginary lit candle. |
> > | **Καλό Πάσχα!** | Happy Easter! | Crack an imaginary red egg against a partner's. |
> > | **Έχω μια λαμπάδα.** | I have an Easter candle. | Hold a tall candle and cup the flame. |
> > | **Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό;** | Do you want a red egg? | Offer a cupped red egg. |
>
> **Step 1: the week, day by day**  
> T: (day cards) Μεγάλη Πέμπτη: βάφουμε κόκκινα αυγά. Μεγάλη Παρασκευή: ο Επιτάφιος. Μεγάλο Σάββατο: η Ανάσταση, με τη λαμπάδα. Κυριακή του Πάσχα: αρνί και κόκκινα αυγά.  
> **Step 2: things**  
> T: η λαμπάδα, το κόκκινο αυγό, τα κόκκινα αυγά, το τσουρέκι, το αρνί.  
> **Step 3: greetings**  
> T: Καλή Ανάσταση! — Ss: Καλή Ανάσταση!  
> **Step 4: egg-tapping (anchor recycling)**  
> T: Έχω ένα κόκκινο αυγό. Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό; — S1: Ναι, θέλω!  
> T: Τσουγκρίζουμε; (Shall we tap eggs?) — Pairs tap eggs. Whoever's egg survives says Χριστός Ανέστη! first.  
> **Warm-up dialogue**  
> A: Καλή Ανάσταση!  
> B: Καλή Ανάσταση! Έχεις λαμπάδα;  
> A: Ναι, έχω μια άσπρη λαμπάδα. Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό;  
> B: Ναι! Τσουγκρίζουμε;  
> A: (they tap) Ωχ! Το αυγό μου!  
> B: Χα! Καλό Πάσχα!  
> **Exit ticket:** One Holy Week greeting and one sentence about a custom.

## 3 · Main scene

**Getting ready for Easter**: Friends talk about candles, red eggs, and τσουρέκι during Holy Week.

**Familiarity:** 97% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** λαμπάδες, βάφεις

- A: **Καλή Ανάσταση!** — Have a blessed Resurrection!
- B: **Καλή Ανάσταση! Έχεις λαμπάδα για την Ανάσταση;** — Have a blessed Resurrection! Do you have a candle for the Resurrection service?
- A: **Ναι, έχω μια άσπρη λαμπάδα. Τα παιδιά έχουν λαμπάδες με χρώματα.** — Yes, I have a white candle. The kids have colorful ones.
- B: **Βάφεις αυγά;** — Do you dye eggs?
- A: **Ναι, τη Μεγάλη Πέμπτη βάφουμε κόκκινα αυγά. Θέλεις κόκκινο αυγό;** — Yes, on Holy Thursday we dye red eggs. Do you want a red egg?
- B: **Ναι, θέλω! Και τσουρέκι;** — Yes, I do! And tsoureki?
- A: **Έχω τσουρέκι από τη γιαγιά. Είναι πολύ νόστιμο.** — I have tsoureki from Grandma. It's delicious.
- B: **Πού θα είσαι το Πάσχα;** — Where will you be for Easter?
- A: **Θα είμαι εδώ, στην εκκλησία. Εσύ;** — I'll be here, at church. You?
- B: **Θα πάω στο χωριό.** — I'll go to the village.
- A: **Καλό Πάσχα!** — Happy Easter!
- B: **Καλό Πάσχα!** — Happy Easter!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Item: μια λαμπάδα · κόκκινα αυγά · τσουρέκι · λουλούδια
> - Day: τη Μεγάλη Πέμπτη · τη Μεγάλη Παρασκευή · το Μεγάλο Σάββατο · την Κυριακή του Πάσχα
> - Wish: Καλή Ανάσταση! · Καλό Πάσχα!
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Τι έκανες το Σαββατοκύριακο;**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Real egg-tapping (τσούγκρισμα) at the end. Whoever's egg survives says the greeting first.
> - Holy Week calendar: put the day cards in order, then say what happens on each day.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Time shift: as yesterday (ήθελα, πήγα…) or as tomorrow (θα…).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "Where will you be for Easter?" · B: "I'll go to the village." · C: "I have an Easter candle."
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** One Holy Week greeting and one sentence about a custom. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Tell a family member in Greek what happens on each day of Holy Week.
> - If you dye eggs, name the colors in Greek as you go.
>
> **Out in the world:** Dye eggs red, visit a Greek Orthodox church service if you can, or cook one Easter dish (τσουρέκι, μαγειρίτσα). Write down three words you heard or read.
>
> **Watch-list:** Ανάσταση is a-NA-sta-see. τσουρέκι is tsoo-RE-kee. λαμπάδα is lam-BA-dha (μπ = b).
