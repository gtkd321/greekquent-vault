# Card ideas

Ideas for paper cards that stick. Some come from the course. The rest come from classmates' cards, shared without names and only when the student said yes. Back to [[Today]].

> [!info]- How paper cards work
> 1. **Make the card.** Greek on the front (with ο, η or το). A picture, a gesture or a real moment on the back.
> 2. **Log it.** Open that word's note and tap **On paper**. +1 φόρα, once per word.
> 3. **Pile it.** Keep three piles: red, yellow, green. A card goes in the pile of its color on the word note. No color yet means the red pile.
> 4. **Pull the piles.** Red every day, yellow every other day, green on Sundays. **Paper round** on Today tells you which piles and walks you through them.
> 5. **In the round.** Say the other side out loud, then flip. **Got it**: the app asks if the card moves up a pile. **Not yet**: back to red. **Not in my stack**: you haven't made that card yet, so it skips with no change.
> 6. **Share it if you like.** Add a photo on the word note, then Send to teacher, and pick GroupMe so the whole class sees it right away. Notebook pages count too. Your teacher collects the best ideas here, without names.

> [!tip]- Start here
> - Put a picture, a gesture note or a real moment on the back, not only the English.
> - Write the article with the word (ο, η, το) so the gender comes along.
> - Add a short sentence of your own, from something that happened this week.
> - If an English word sounds like it, write that link down.
> - Mark its color in a corner (red, yellow, green) and change it when it changes.

> [!example]- From the class
> - **Nov 2026: one color per language.** Greek in one color, English in another, and if you write how it sounds, a third. Your eye finds the Greek first, and the page reads at a glance.
> - **Nov 2026: a "how it sounds" column.** Greek, then how you say it, then English. Copy the "say it" line from the word note, stress mark included: Δρόμος, Drómos.
> - **Nov 2026: start a topic page with a phrase.** Head the food page with Θέλω... (I want), then list the foods. Every word on the page becomes something you can say at a table.
> - **Nov 2026: a verb grid.** Who does it down the side (εγώ, εσύ, εμείς), when across (now, before, later). Put the English in brackets under each form.
> - **Nov 2026: star your favorite pages.** A star on the pages you come back to makes them easy to find.
>
> Your teacher adds the best ideas from cards students send. To send yours: on a word note, tap On paper, add a photo of your card, then tap Send to teacher.
