## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 10 new words on their printed list: ο, η, το (1) · δεν (6) · αλλά (27) · θέλω (29) · λίγο (85) · καλώς ήρθες (400) · ο καφές (409) · το κρασί (704) · το τσάι (732) · το ψωμί (1269)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'Would you like a little water?'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Θέλεις λίγο νερό;**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Θέλεις λίγο νερό;** | Would you like a little water? | Offer an imaginary glass with both hands. |
> > | **Ναι, θέλω λίγο…, ευχαριστώ.** | Yes, I'd like a little…, thank you. | Pinch thumb and finger close together for "a little", then hand to heart. |
> > | **Όχι, ευχαριστώ.** | No, thank you. | Greek "no": tilt the head up with a small click of the tongue, then hand to heart. |
> > | **Θέλεις ένα γλυκό;** | Would you like a sweet? | Offer an imaginary spoon of spoon-sweet. |
> > | **Έχεις ψωμί;** | Do you have bread? | Mime tearing a piece of bread, eyebrows up. |
> > | **Ο Νίκος θέλει τσάι.** | Nikos wants tea. | Point at "Nikos", then mime holding a hot cup and blowing on it. |
> > | **…αλλά…** | …but… | One hand out, then the other hand out on the other side. |
>
> **Anchor verb: θέλω**  
> T: Θέλω νερό. Θέλω τσάι. Θέλω ένα γλυκό!  
> T: Θέλεις λίγο νερό; — S1: Ναι, θέλω λίγο νερό, ευχαριστώ.  
> T: Θέλεις ένα γλυκό; — S2: Όχι, ευχαριστώ.  
> T: Ο Νίκος θέλει τσάι. Η Μαρία θέλει κρασί.  
> **Scenario: a guest at a Greek home**  
> Host: Θέλεις νερό;  
> Guest: Ναι, θέλω λίγο νερό, ευχαριστώ.  
> Host: Θέλεις ένα γλυκό;  
> Guest: Όχι, ευχαριστώ.  
> Host: Έχεις ψωμί; — Ναι, έχω! / Όχι.  
> Report: Ο ___ θέλει τσάι, αλλά…  
> **Reading card:** Θέλεις; – Θέλω. / Έχεις; – Έχω. / Λες; / Θέλει, αλλά…  
>

>

## 3 · Main scene

**Visiting the neighbor**: A guest arrives at a Greek neighbor's house. In Greece a host offers more than once, and a first "no" is expected.

**Familiarity:** 88% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** καλώς ήρθες, ο καφές, δεν

- Host: **Γεια σου! Καλώς ήρθες!** — Hi! Welcome!
- Guest: **Γεια σου, Μαρία! Ευχαριστώ.** — Hi, Maria! Thank you.
- Host: **Θέλεις λίγο νερό;** — Would you like a little water?
- Guest: **Ναι, θέλω λίγο νερό, ευχαριστώ.** — Yes, I'd like a little water, thank you.
- Host: **Θέλεις τσάι;** — Do you want tea?
- Guest: **Ναι, θέλω τσάι, ευχαριστώ.** — Yes, I'd like tea, thank you.
- Host: **Έχω και ένα γλυκό. Θέλεις;** — I also have a sweet. Would you like one?
- Guest: **Όχι, ευχαριστώ.** — No, thank you.
- Host: **Λίγο; Θέλεις λίγο;** — A little? Do you want a little?
- Guest: **Ναι, λίγο! Ευχαριστώ.** — Yes, a little! Thank you.
- Host: **Νίκο, θέλεις κι εσύ τσάι;** — Nikos, do you want tea too?
- Nikos: **Όχι, θέλω καφέ.** — No, I want coffee.
- Host: **Ο Νίκος θέλει καφέ, αλλά δεν έχω καφέ! Έχω τσάι.** — Nikos wants coffee, but I don't have coffee! I have tea.
- Nikos: **Ναι, τσάι!** — Yes, tea!
- Guest (to the class): **Η Μαρία έχει ένα γλυκό. Ο Νίκος θέλει καφέ, αλλά…** — Maria has a sweet. Nikos wants coffee, but…

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Drinks: νερό · τσάι · καφέ · κρασί · γάλα
> - Food: ένα γλυκό · ψωμί · λίγο τυρί
> - Answer: Ναι, θέλω λίγο… · Όχι, ευχαριστώ. · Καλά, λίγο!
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Γεια σας**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - The host insists three times. The guest has to hold out until the third offer, then accept.
> - Report round: each student tells the class what someone else wants (Ο/Η ___ θέλει…), which practices θέλει.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "Would you like a little water?" · B: "Yes, I'd like a little water, thank you." · C: "Nikos, do you want tea too?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** papers away. Each student writes two of today's phrases by hand on an index card, then says them with the gesture, plus one true sentence about themselves.
