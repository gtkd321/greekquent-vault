# Kid variant · Lesson 20: Shoebox house and color hunt

Play version of [[L20 My house-teacher|Lesson 20: My house]] · [[K20 Shoebox house and color hunt-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Draw or build a house, describe it with colors and sizes, then race to find colors around the room.

**You'll need:** a shoebox or paper, markers, toys of different colors

## How to play

1. Kid draws or builds a small house with rooms.
2. Describe it together, matching the color word to the noun's gender.
3. Color hunt: the teacher calls a color, and the kid runs to touch something that color.

## Lines

- K: **Έχω ένα μικρό σπίτι.** — I have a small house.
- K: **Η κουζίνα είναι κίτρινη.** — The kitchen is yellow.
- T: **Βρες κάτι κόκκινο!** — Find something red!
- K: **Ένα κόκκινο αυτοκίνητο!** — A red car!
- K: **Μια κόκκινη μπάλα!** — A red ball!
- K: **Βλέπω ένα μεγάλο δωμάτιο.** — I see a big room.

## Make it

The shoebox house, painted and colored, with a room for every color.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Describe your real home, or a dream house in Greece.
