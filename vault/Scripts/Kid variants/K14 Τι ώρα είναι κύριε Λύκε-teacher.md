# Kid variant · Lesson 14: Τι ώρα είναι, κύριε Λύκε;

Play version of [[L14 Time and days-teacher|Lesson 14: Time and days]] · [[K14 Τι ώρα είναι κύριε Λύκε-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Mr. Wolf, in Greek: kids ask the time, take that many steps, and get chased at dinnertime.

**You'll need:** a hallway or yard; a paper-plate clock with movable hands

## How to play

1. The wolf (teacher) stands at the far end with his back turned.
2. The kid asks what time it is; the wolf answers a number, and the kid takes that many steps.
3. When the wolf says it's dinnertime, he turns and chases.
4. Calm round: set the clock and read it together.

## Lines

- K: **Τι ώρα είναι, κύριε Λύκε;** — What time is it, Mr. Wolf?
- T: **Είναι τρεις η ώρα!** — It's three o'clock!
- K: **Ένα, δύο, τρία!** — One, two, three!
- T: **Είναι ώρα για φαγητό!** — It's dinnertime!
- T: **Μπορείς την Τρίτη;** — Can you on Tuesday?
- K: **Ναι, την Τρίτη μπορώ!** — Yes, on Tuesday I can!

## Make it

The paper-plate clock with numbers and hands.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Plan real meetups on a calendar: who can when, and at what time.
