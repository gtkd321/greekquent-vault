## Today's phrases

Make the gesture every time you say the phrase. It helps the phrase stick.

| Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα.** | I see the sea. | Shade your eyes with one hand, then make waves with the other. |
| **Τι βλέπεις;** | What do you see? | Point to your eyes, then to the partner, palms up. |
| **Τι κάνεις; – Καλά.** | How are you? – Good. | Palm-up hand toward the partner, then thumbs up for "Καλά". |
| **Τι θέλετε;** | What would you like? (polite / group) | Waiter pose: pretend to hold a notepad and pen. |
| **Ένα νερό, παρακαλώ.** | A water, please. | Hold up one finger, then mime a glass. |
| **Τίποτα άλλο;** | Anything else? | Tap the imaginary notepad with the pen, eyebrows up. |
| **Όχι, τίποτα.** | No, nothing. | Greek "no": head tilt up, then both hands sweep flat and apart. |
| **Μπορείς τώρα;** | Can you now? | Point at the floor twice, quickly ("now"). |

## Main scene

**Postcard and a café order**: Part 1: describe a postcard from Greece. Part 2: order at a café.

**New words:** καλημέρα

- A: **Τι βλέπεις;** — What do you see?
- B: **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα. Βλέπω και μια βάρκα.** — I see the sea. I also see a boat.
- A: **Βλέπεις τον ήλιο;** — Do you see the sun?
- B: **Ναι, βλέπω τον ήλιο. Και ένα βουνό.** — Yes, I see the sun. And a mountain.
- A: **Βλέπεις μια εκκλησία;** — Do you see a church?
- B: **Ναι, βλέπω μια εκκλησία και ένα σπίτι.** — Yes, I see a church and a house.
- Waiter: **Καλημέρα! Τι θέλετε;** — Good morning! What would you like?
- A: **Καλημέρα! Ένα νερό και έναν καφέ, παρακαλώ.** — Good morning! A water and a coffee, please.
- Waiter: **(to B) Τι θέλετε;** — What would you like?
- B: **Ένα τσάι, παρακαλώ.** — A tea, please.
- Waiter: **Τίποτα άλλο;** — Anything else?
- A: **Όχι, τίποτα. Ευχαριστώ.** — No, nothing. Thank you.
- Waiter: **Ορίστε.** — Here you are.
- A: **Τι κάνεις, Μαρία;** — How are you, Maria?
- B: **Καλά! Εσύ;** — Good! You?

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Postcard: τη θάλασσα · τον ήλιο · ένα βουνό · μια βάρκα · μια εκκλησία · ένα σπίτι
> - Order: ένα νερό · έναν καφέ · ένα τσάι · ένα κρασί · ένα γλυκό
> - Article check: τη (feminine) · τον (masculine) · το (neuter)
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Μπορώ;**
>
> **Twists to try with a partner**
>
> - Mystery postcard: B describes a postcard A can't see, and A has to pick it out of three.
> - The waiter brings the wrong thing on purpose. The customer says Όχι, θέλω… and tries again.
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). When you're listening, just listen.

> [!example]- Build it with tiles
> Cut out your Lesson 4 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
>
> 1. Build it from the English, then read it aloud.
> 2. Turn it into a question: swap the . for ; and raise your voice at the end.
> 3. Make it negative: put δεν right before the verb.
> 4. Swap one tile for another word from the extra tiles.
> 5. Write your last version by hand in your notebook.
>
> - **A:** Do you see the sun?
> - **B:** Do you see a church?
> - **C:** A tea, please.
>
> Extra tiles: δεν · ; · . · τη θάλασσα · τον ήλιο · ένα βουνό · μια βάρκα
