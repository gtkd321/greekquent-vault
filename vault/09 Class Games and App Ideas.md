# 09 Class Games and App Ideas

Links: [[00 Greekquent Index]] · [[07 Fluent in a Year]] · [[08 Online Course Studio Plan]]

> [!summary]- Greekquent Buzzer (fastest fingers)
> [Open the Greekquent Buzzer](https://www.perplexity.ai/computer/a/greekquent-buzzer-f3uOHxBFS6Ko8YnCc99fzw)
> - Lay the phone flat between two students sitting face to face. The screen is split, so each student reads their own half the right way up.
> - Each round shows an English word and two Greek choices. First correct tap wins the point. A wrong tap locks that student out, so the other can still answer.
> - **Words from:** a lesson (phrases and words), a Fluent in a Year week, or the top 50 to 1,500 words. You can mix in earlier lessons or weeks.
> - **Options:** play to 5, 10, or 15. Reverse mode (Greek prompt, English choices). The phone says the Greek answer aloud. 7-second rounds.
> - Missed words come back a few rounds later. At the end, the app lists them so students can mark them red on their printed list.
> - The end screen shows each player's hit rate. Aim for about 85% right over a few games ([[06 Addenda]], A5).
> - Free, with no accounts and nothing saved. Turn off auto-lock and silent mode before class.

> [!summary]- Run it in class
> 1. Pick the current lesson, play to 5.
> 2. Winner stays, the next student challenges.
> 3. Last round of the night: the Fluent in a Year week with earlier weeks mixed in.

> - **Fits in Obsidian now:** group meet prompt cards. The prompts already live in the week notes; a random-note button picks one.
> - **App only:** say it and compare. Record yourself, then play back the model audio next to it.
> - **App only:** fork round builder. Drag word tiles to swap words into the lesson scene, like the paper tiles.
> - **App only:** Fluent in a Year streak. Five words a day with a daily check-in and a Sunday review.
> - **App only:** buzzer for teams. Four players, one in each corner of a tablet.
> - **Built in:** the buzzer end screen shows each player's hit rate against the 85% target ([[06 Addenda]], A5).
