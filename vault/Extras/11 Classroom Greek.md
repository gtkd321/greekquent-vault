# Classroom Greek

The same phrases every class, all year. Learn your side once and you can stay in Greek even when you're lost. Do the gesture every time you say it. Back to [[Today]].

> [!tip]- Why this works
> Set phrases you hear every week stick without studying, because they come up so often and you need them. They also buy you time to think. Saying "Ένα λεπτό…" beats switching to English.

## Your side: staying in Greek

```greekquent-say
Πώς λέμε «…» στα ελληνικά; | How do you say "…" in Greek? | Point at your mouth, then shrug.
Τι σημαίνει «…»; | What does "…" mean? | Tap your temple, eyebrows up.
Δεν κατάλαβα. | I didn't understand. | Shake your head slowly, hand flat by your ear.
Ξανά, παρακαλώ. | Again, please. | Roll your hand forward in a circle.
Πιο αργά, παρακαλώ. | Slower, please. | Push both palms down slowly.
Ένα λεπτό… | One moment… | Hold up one finger.
Δεν ξέρω. | I don't know. | Shrug with palms up.
Νομίζω… | I think… | Tap your temple.
Έτσι; | Like this? | Palms out, as if offering it.
Σωστά; | Right? | Thumb half up, eyebrows up.
Συγγνώμη. | Sorry. | Hand on your chest.
Μπορώ να το πω στα αγγλικά; | Can I say it in English? | Make a small "E" with your fingers. Use it only when you really need to.
```

## The teacher's side: what you will hear

```greekquent-say
Γεια σας! Ξεκινάμε. | Hello, everyone. Let's begin. | Big wave, then clap once.
Ακούστε. | Listen. | Cup your hand behind your ear.
Επαναλάβετε. | Repeat. | Point to your mouth, then to the class.
Όλοι μαζί. | All together. | Sweep both arms in.
Ξανά. | Again. | Roll your hand forward in a circle.
Πιο δυνατά. | Louder. | Palms up, lift them.
Σειρά σου. | Your turn. | Open hand toward one person.
Ποιος θέλει; | Who wants to? | Hand up high, looking around.
Σε ζευγάρια. | In pairs. | Two fingers, then point at a partner.
Γυρίστε τα χαρτιά. | Turn your papers over. | Flip your hand over.
Μπράβο! Σωστά! | Well done. That's right. | Thumbs up.
Σχεδόν. | Almost. | Flat hand, tilt it side to side.
Καταλάβατε; | Did you understand? | Answer with a thumb: up, sideways or down.
Στα αγγλικά: | In English (a short English moment). | Fingers make an "E". It ends when the teacher says "Πάμε πάλι στα ελληνικά."
Τελειώσαμε. Θα τα πούμε! | We're done. See you. | Wave.
```

## Thumbs mean colors

When the teacher asks **Καταλάβατε;**, answer with your thumb. **Up** is green (got it), **sideways** is yellow (kind of), **down** is red (not yet). These are the same three colors as your word list.

## The weekly rally

Every class starts with a quick back-and-forth in Greek, the same shape each week: the teacher asks, you answer, then you swap and ask. The lines are on each lesson page under **Rally**. Practice your side before class: on Today, tap **Practice my side**.
