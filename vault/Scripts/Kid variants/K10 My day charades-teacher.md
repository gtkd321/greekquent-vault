# Kid variant · Lesson 10: My day charades

Play version of [[L10 My day-teacher|Lesson 10: My day]] · [[K10 My day charades-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** The kid acts out daily activities, and the other person guesses. Use θέλω να / μπορώ να as fixed chunks.

**You'll need:** picture cards: wake up, eat, play, sleep; a paper-plate sun and moon

## How to play

1. Hold up the sun for morning, sun high for midday, moon for evening.
2. Kid acts out an activity; you guess it.
3. Then play the keystone: say something he wants to do, then why he can't.

## Lines

- T: **Τι κάνεις;** — What are you doing?
- K: **Θέλω να φάω!** — I want to eat!
- K: **Θέλω να παίξω, αλλά δεν μπορώ.** — I want to play, but I can't.
- T: **Γιατί;** — Why?
- K: **Γιατί είναι βράδυ!** — Because it's evening!
- T: **Το πρωί… το βράδυ…** — In the morning… in the evening…

## Make it

A paper-plate clock with the sun on one side and the moon on the other. The kid colors the sky for each part of the day.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Describe your real day, then plan one thing you want to do and one you can't.
