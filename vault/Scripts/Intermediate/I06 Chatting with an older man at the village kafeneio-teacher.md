# Intermediate · Lesson 6: Chatting with an older man at the village kafeneio

Same theme as [[L06 Coffee plans at the kafeneio-teacher|Lesson 6: Coffee plans at the kafeneio]], with a longer scene · [[I06 Chatting with an older man at the village kafeneio-student|Student version]] · all lessons in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**Setting:** Adults sit with a regular at a village kafeneio. It mixes all four anchor verbs with natural small talk.

**Review first:** run the spaced review from [[L06 Coffee plans at the kafeneio-teacher|Lesson 6]], but answer each card in 2 sentences with a reason (γιατί…) or a time (χθες, αύριο, κάθε μέρα).

## Scene

- Old man: **Καλημέρα, παιδιά! Πρώτη φορά εδώ;** — Good morning, folks! First time here?
- A: **Καλημέρα! Ναι. Μπορούμε να καθίσουμε εδώ;** — Good morning! Yes. May we sit here?
- Old man: **Βέβαια, καθίστε. Από πού είστε;** — Of course, sit down. Where are you from?
- A: **Από την Αμερική. Μαθαίνουμε ελληνικά.** — From America. We're learning Greek.
- Old man: **Μπράβο! Και τι θέλετε να πιείτε; Εδώ ο καφές είναι ο καλύτερος στο χωριό.** — Good for you! And what would you like to drink? The coffee here is the best in the village.
- B: **Θέλω έναν ελληνικό. Εσύ, Μαρία;** — I'd like a Greek coffee. You, Maria?
- A: **Δεν πίνω καφέ το απόγευμα, γιατί μετά δεν μπορώ να κοιμηθώ. Ένα τσάι του βουνού.** — I don't drink coffee in the afternoon, because then I can't sleep. A mountain tea.
- Old man: **Σωστά. Βλέπετε εκείνο το βουνό; Από εκεί είναι το τσάι.** — Right. Do you see that mountain? That's where the tea comes from.
- B: **Αλήθεια; Μπορούμε να πάμε εκεί με τα πόδια;** — Really? Can we walk there?
- Old man: **Μπορείτε, αλλά είναι μακριά. Δύο ώρες. Έχετε καλά παπούτσια;** — You can, but it's far. Two hours. Do you have good shoes?
- A: **Όχι ακόμα! Ίσως την επόμενη φορά.** — Not yet! Maybe next time.
- Old man: **Ελάτε όποτε θέλετε. Εγώ είμαι εδώ κάθε μέρα.** — Come whenever you like. I'm here every day.

## After the scene

> [!question]- Talk: conversation questions
> Open questions for pairs. Answer in at least two sentences.
>
> - **Έχεις ένα αγαπημένο καφέ ή καφενείο;** — Do you have a favorite café or kafeneio?
> - **Τι θέλεις να κάνεις αυτό το Σαββατοκύριακο, και μπορείς;** — What do you want to do this weekend, and can you?
> - **Τι βλέπεις όταν πίνεις τον καφέ σου το πρωί;** — What do you see when you drink your morning coffee?

> [!tip]- Push it further and fork it
> - The old man asks lots of personal questions (married? kids? job?). Answer, then turn the questions back on him.
> - Ask for directions to the mountain path, and the old man gives them in detail.
> - Retell the visit as "we": Είδαμε…, θέλαμε…, δεν μπορούσαμε…
>
> The swaps and twists from [[L06 Coffee plans at the kafeneio-teacher|Lesson 6]] work here too. Change the ending each time you replay the scene.
