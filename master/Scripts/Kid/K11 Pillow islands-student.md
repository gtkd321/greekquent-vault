# Pillow islands

**The game:** Pillows or paper signs around the room become places. Say where you're going, then jump there.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Πού πας;** — Where are you going?
- You: **Πάω στη θάλασσα!** — I'm going to the sea!
- You: **Πάω στο πάρκο.** — I'm going to the park.
- You: **Θέλω να πάω στο σπίτι.** — I want to go home.
- You: **Πάμε!** — Let's go!
- Grown-up: **Μπορούμε να πάμε στο σχολείο;** — Can we go to school?

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Πού πας;** — Where are you going?
- You (teacher): **Μπορούμε να πάμε στο σχολείο;** — Can we go to school?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The kid draws each island's sign with a picture (waves, a church dome, a swing).

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Πού πας;**: Palms up, eyebrows up.
- **στη θάλασσα**: Swim with your arms.
- **στο πάρκο**: Pump your legs like on a swing.
- **στο σπίτι**: Hands make a roof over your head.
- **Πάμε!**: Wave come on, and jump onto the pillow.
- **στο σχολείο**: Put on a pretend backpack.
