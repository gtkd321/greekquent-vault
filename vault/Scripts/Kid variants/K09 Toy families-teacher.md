# Kid variant · Lesson 9: Toy families

Play version of [[L09 My family-teacher|Lesson 9: My family]] · [[K09 Toy families-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Stuffed animals become a family, and you and the kid are another family, which lets you use the plural.

**You'll need:** two groups of toys (a big animal and small animals), family photos

## How to play

1. Line up the toy family. Introduce each one: mom, dad, kids, grandma.
2. You and the kid are the "we" family; the toys are the "you all" family.
3. Ask each other questions across families.

## Lines

- T: **Αυτή είναι η μητέρα. Αυτός είναι ο πατέρας.** — This is the mother. This is the father.
- T: **Έχουν δύο παιδιά.** — They have two kids.
- K: **Έχετε γάτα;** — Do you (all) have a cat?
- T (as toy family): **Ναι, έχουμε!** — Yes, we do!
- T: **Τι θέλουν τα παιδιά;** — What do the kids want?
- K: **Θέλουν νερό!** — They want water!
- K: **Μπορούμε;** — Can we?

## Make it

A handprint family tree: each person is a colored handprint with their Greek title written underneath.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Bring real family photos and do the lesson's scenario, with photos of extended family in Greece if you have them.
