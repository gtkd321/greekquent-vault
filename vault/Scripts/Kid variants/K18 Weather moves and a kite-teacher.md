# Kid variant · Lesson 18: Weather moves and a kite

Play version of [[L18 Clean Monday kites and weather-teacher|Lesson 18: Clean Monday kites and weather]] · [[K18 Weather moves and a kite-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Each kind of weather gets a body movement. Then make a small kite for Καθαρά Δευτέρα.

**You'll need:** tissue paper, string, a straw or stick for the kite; optional fan

## How to play

1. Teach a move for each weather: shiver for cold, fan yourself for hot, finger rain for rain, arms in a circle for sun, sway for wind.
2. Teacher calls the weather; kid does the move. Then swap.
3. Make the kite and run with it: Βλέπω τον χαρταετό!

## Lines

- T: **Τι καιρό κάνει;** — What's the weather like?
- K: **Κάνει κρύο!** — It's cold!
- K: **Κάνει ζέστη!** — It's hot!
- K: **Βρέχει!** — It's raining!
- K: **Έχει ήλιο!** — It's sunny!
- K: **Βλέπω τον χαρταετό!** — I see the kite!

## Make it

The kite, in bright colors, with a tail of tissue paper.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Give the weather report like a TV forecaster, using a real map of Greece.
