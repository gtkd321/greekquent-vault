## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'Do you want a coffee?'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Θέλεις έναν καφέ;**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Θέλεις έναν καφέ;** | Do you want a coffee? | Mime a tiny Greek coffee cup, little finger out. |
> > | **Καλά, εσύ;** | Fine, and you? | Thumbs up, then a palm-up hand toward the partner. |
> > | **Δεν έχω ώρα σήμερα.** | I don't have time today. | Tap your wrist, then shake your head. |
> > | **Αύριο μπορείς;** | Can you tomorrow? | Roll a hand forward, then point at the partner. |
> > | **Τέλεια!** | Perfect! | Kiss your fingertips. |
> > | **Τα λέμε αύριο.** | See you tomorrow. | Small fingers-together wave while rolling the hand forward. |
> > | **Ξέρεις; – Δεν ξέρω.** | Do you know? – I don't know. | Tap your temple, then shrug. |
> > | **στο καφενείο** | at the café (kafeneio) | Mime moving a backgammon (τάβλι) piece on a board. |
>
> **Step 1: the verb grid (yes, no, question)**  
>
> > [!info]- Verb grid: the four anchors, yes / no / question
> > | | + | – | ? |
> > |---|---|---|---|
> > | έχω | Έχω ώρα. | Δεν έχω ώρα. | Έχεις ώρα; |
> > | θέλω | Θέλω έναν καφέ. | Δεν θέλω καφέ. | Θέλεις έναν καφέ; |
> > | μπορώ | Μπορώ αύριο. | Δεν μπορώ αύριο. | Μπορείς αύριο; |
> > | βλέπω | Βλέπω τη Μαρία. | Δεν βλέπω τη Μαρία. | Βλέπεις τη Μαρία; |
> >
> > Every sentence here comes back in the reading and the main scene. Teams race to fill it aloud. Then call "verb, form, person" (θέλω, question, you!) and students build the sentence.
> >
> > Write this on the board at this step. Full tables: [[έχω]] · [[θέλω]] · [[μπορώ]] · [[βλέπω]] · [[ξέρω]] · [[02 Verb Tables]]
>
> Bonus: Ξέρεις; – Δεν ξέρω. (shrug)

## 3 · Main scene

**Coffee plans at the kafeneio**: Two friends make a plan, then meet at the kafeneio the next morning. All four anchor verbs come back.

**Familiarity:** 100% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** none

- A: **Γεια σου, Νίκο! Τι κάνεις;** — Hi, Nikos! How are you?
- B: **Καλά, εσύ;** — Good, you?
- A: **Καλά. Θέλεις έναν καφέ;** — Good. Do you want to get a coffee?
- B: **Θέλω, αλλά δεν έχω ώρα σήμερα.** — I'd like to, but I don't have time today.
- A: **Αύριο μπορείς;** — Can you tomorrow?
- B: **Αύριο το πρωί μπορώ. Ξέρεις το καφενείο;** — Tomorrow morning I can. Do you know the kafeneio?
- A: **Ναι, ξέρω!** — Yes, I know it!
- B: **Και η Μαρία; Θέλει;** — And Maria? Does she want to come?
- A: **Η Μαρία θέλει, αλλά δεν μπορεί. Έχει δουλειά.** — Maria wants to, but she can't. She has work.
- B: **Τέλεια. Τα λέμε αύριο!** — Perfect. See you tomorrow!
- Waiter (next morning): **Καλημέρα! Τι θέλετε;** — Good morning! What would you like?
- A: **Ένα καφέ και ένα τσάι, παρακαλώ.** — A coffee and a tea, please.
- B: **Και ένα νερό.** — And a water.
- Waiter: **Τίποτα άλλο;** — Anything else?
- A: **Όχι, ευχαριστούμε.** — No, thank you.
- B: **Βλέπεις τη Μαρία; Είναι εδώ!** — Do you see Maria? She's here!
- A: **Μαρία! Μπορείς τώρα;** — Maria! Can you now?
- Maria: **Τώρα μπορώ! Θέλω έναν καφέ!** — Now I can! I want a coffee!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Drink: καφέ · τσάι · νερό · κρασί
> - When: σήμερα · αύριο · αύριο το πρωί · το βράδυ
> - Who's missing: η Μαρία · ο Νίκος · ο παππούς
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Δεν έχω…**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Four-verb rule: every line has to use έχω, θέλω, μπορώ, or βλέπω.
> - Someone arrives late, and the others catch them up on everything that happened so far.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "I'd like to, but I don't have time today." · B: "A coffee and a tea, please." · C: "And a water."
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** papers away. Each student writes two of today's phrases by hand on an index card, then says them with the gesture, plus one true sentence about themselves.
