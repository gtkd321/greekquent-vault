# Mystery bag

**The game:** The kid reaches into a bag without looking and guesses what's inside.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Τι έχω;** — What do I have?
- You: **Έχεις ένα αυτοκίνητο;** — Do you have a car?
- Grown-up: **Όχι! / Ναι, έχω!** — No! / Yes, I do!
- Grown-up: **Έχεις μια μπάλα;** — Do you have a ball?
- You: **Ναι, έχω μια μπάλα!** — Yes, I have a ball!
- Grown-up: **Τι έχεις;** — What do you have?
- You: **Έχω ένα κλειδί.** — I have a key.

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι έχω;** — What do I have?
- You (teacher): **Έχεις ένα αυτοκίνητο;** — Do you have a car?
- You (teacher): **Έχεις μια μπάλα;** — Do you have a ball?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

Each object gets a picture card the kid draws and colors. The cards become his flashcards for next week.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Γεια σας**: Big wave to the whole room, fingers together and slightly bent.
- **Γεια σου**: Small wave to one person, fingers together.
- **Πώς σε λένε;**: Palm-up hand toward the partner, eyebrows up.
- **Με λένε…**: Hand flat on your own chest.
- **Έχω ένα…**: Hold the object up close to your body.
- **Έχεις ένα…;**: Point at the partner's bag, eyebrows up.
- **Ναι, έχω.**: Small Greek nod, head tipping down and to the side, and pat the object.
- **Τι έχεις;**: Both palms up, shoulders slightly raised.
