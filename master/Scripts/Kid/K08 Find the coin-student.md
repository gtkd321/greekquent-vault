# Find the coin

**The game:** A pretend βασιλόπιτα cut into pieces, with a coin hidden under one. Count the pieces, then find out who has the coin.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Ένα, δύο, τρία… δέκα!** — One, two, three… ten!
- Grown-up: **Ένα κομμάτι για τον μπαμπά, ένα για τη μαμά…** — One piece for Dad, one for Mom…
- Grown-up: **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;** — Who has the coin?
- You: **Εγώ! Εγώ έχω το φλουρί!** — Me! I have the coin!
- Ss: **Καλή χρονιά!** — Happy New Year!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;** — Who has the coin?
- You (teacher): **Ένα, δύο, τρία… δέκα!** — One, two, three… ten!
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The kid decorates the paper cake with the new year's number and gold coin stickers.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;**: Hold up an imaginary coin and look around the room.
- **Εγώ έχω!**: Jump up, coin held high.
- **ένα κομμάτι**: Slice the air like a knife cutting cake.
- **Καλή χρονιά!**: Raise a glass for a toast.
