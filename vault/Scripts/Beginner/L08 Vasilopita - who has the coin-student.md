## Today's phrases

Make the gesture every time you say the phrase. It helps the phrase stick.

| Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;** | Who has the coin? | Hold up an imaginary coin and look around the room. |
| **Εγώ έχω!** | I do! / I have it! | Jump up, coin held high. |
| **ένα κομμάτι** | a piece | Slice the air like a knife cutting cake. |
| **Καλή χρονιά!** | Happy New Year! | Raise a glass for a toast. |

## Main scene

**Cutting the vasilopita**: The family cuts the New Year's cake. Count the pieces and find out who has the coin.

**New words:** βασιλόπιτα, η γιαγιά

- Teacher: **Καλή χρονιά!** — Happy New Year!
- Everyone: **Καλή χρονιά!** — Happy New Year!
- Teacher: **Έχω μια βασιλόπιτα. Ένα, δύο, τρία… δέκα κομμάτια.** — I have a vasilopita. One, two, three… ten pieces.
- Teacher: **Ένα κομμάτι για τον Χριστό. Ένα για το σπίτι.** — One piece for Christ. One for the house.
- Teacher: **Ένα κομμάτι για τη Μαρία.** — One piece for Maria.
- Maria: **Ευχαριστώ!** — Thank you!
- Teacher: **Ένα για τον Νίκο. Ένα για τη γιαγιά.** — One for Nikos. One for Grandma.
- Teacher: **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;** — Who has the coin?
- Maria: **Δεν έχω.** — I don't have it.
- Nikos: **Δεν έχω!** — I don't!
- Grandma: **Εγώ! Εγώ έχω το φλουρί!** — Me! I have the coin!
- Everyone: **Μπράβο! Καλή χρονιά!** — Well done! Happy New Year!
- A: **Πόσα κομμάτια έχεις;** — How many pieces do you have?
- B: **Έχω δύο.** — I have two.
- A: **Δύο; Θέλω κι εγώ ένα!** — Two? I want one too!

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Number: ένα · δύο · τρία · τέσσερα · πέντε · έξι · επτά · οκτώ · εννιά · δέκα
> - For whom: για τη μαμά · για τον μπαμπά · για τη γιαγιά · για τον παππού
> - Wish: Καλή χρονιά! · Χρόνια πολλά! · Ευτυχισμένο το νέο έτος!
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Είμαι ο/η… από…**
>
> **Twists to try with a partner**
>
> - Countdown: count backwards from ten together, then shout Καλή χρονιά!
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). When you're listening, just listen.

> Cut out your Lesson 8 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
