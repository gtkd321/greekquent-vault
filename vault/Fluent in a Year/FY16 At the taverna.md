## Group meet prompt

**At the taverna**

- **Τι θα πάρετε;** (What will you have?)
- **Θα ήθελα μια χωριάτικη.** (I'd like a Greek salad.)

**Bridge:** Order for the whole group from a real Greek taverna menu.
