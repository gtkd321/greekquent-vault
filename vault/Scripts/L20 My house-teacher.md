## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 19 new words on their printed list: κάτι (41) · μικρός (136) · μένω (201) · η πόρτα (225) · το δωμάτιο (260) · παλιός (287) · καινούργιος (536) · το μπάνιο (603) · το διαμέρισμα (711) · η κουζίνα (754) · κόκκινος (810) · μαύρος (839) · το χρώμα (934) · ο κήπος (1032) · ο σκύλος (1174) · πράσινος (1291) · άσπρος (1428) · κίτρινος (1429) · γκρι (1431)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'I have a small house.'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Έχω ένα μικρό σπίτι.**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Έχω ένα μικρό σπίτι.** | I have a small house. | Make a small roof with your hands. |
> > | **ένα μεγάλο δωμάτιο** | a big room | Spread your arms wide. |
> > | **Η κουζίνα είναι μικρή.** | The kitchen is small. | Stir a pot inside a tiny box drawn with your hands. |
> > | **Βλέπω ένα κόκκινο…** | I see a red… | Point at something red in the room. |
> > | **καινούργιο ή παλιό;** | new or old? | Shiny sparkle hands, then bend over like an old person. |

## 3 · Main scene

**My house**: Two friends describe their homes with sizes and colors. Each adjective matches its noun.

**Familiarity:** 100% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** none

- A: **Πού μένεις;** — Where do you live?
- B: **Μένω σε ένα μικρό σπίτι κοντά στη θάλασσα.** — I live in a small house near the sea.
- A: **Τι ωραία! Είναι καινούργιο ή παλιό;** — How nice! Is it new or old?
- B: **Είναι παλιό, αλλά είναι μεγάλο.** — It's old, but it's big.
- A: **Τι χρώμα είναι;** — What color is it?
- B: **Είναι άσπρο με μια μπλε πόρτα.** — It's white with a blue door.
- A: **Πόσα δωμάτια έχει;** — How many rooms does it have?
- B: **Έχει δύο μικρά δωμάτια και μια μεγάλη κουζίνα.** — It has two small rooms and a big kitchen.
- A: **Μια μεγάλη κουζίνα! Τρως στην κουζίνα;** — A big kitchen! Do you eat in the kitchen?
- B: **Ναι! Η κουζίνα είναι κόκκινη. Εσύ; Έχεις σπίτι ή διαμέρισμα;** — Yes! The kitchen is red. You? Do you have a house or an apartment?
- A: **Έχω ένα καινούργιο διαμέρισμα. Είναι μικρό, αλλά έχει ένα μεγάλο μπαλκόνι.** — I have a new apartment. It's small, but it has a big balcony.
- B: **Βλέπεις τη θάλασσα από το μπαλκόνι;** — Can you see the sea from the balcony?
- A: **Όχι, βλέπω ένα μεγάλο βουνό.** — No, I see a big mountain.
- B: **Και αυτό είναι καλό!** — That's good too!
- T: **ένας μεγάλος σκύλος · μια μεγάλη κουζίνα · ένα μεγάλο σπίτι** — a big dog · a big kitchen · a big house

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Size and age: μεγάλος/-η/-ο · μικρός/-ή/-ό · καινούργιος/-α/-ο · παλιός/-ά/-ό
> - Color: άσπρος · μαύρος · κόκκινος · κίτρινος · πράσινος · μπλε
> - Room: η κουζίνα · το δωμάτιο · το μπάνιο · το μπαλκόνι
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Πού είναι…;**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Draw your partner's house as they describe it, then compare drawings.
> - Agreement race: the teacher calls out a noun, and the class shouts the right form of μεγάλος.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Time shift: as tomorrow, with θα.
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (2 minutes, 90 seconds, then 1 minute). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "I live in a small house near the sea." · B: "It's white with a blue door." · C: "Can you see the sea from the balcony?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Describe one room in your home with two adjectives. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Put sticky notes on 5 things at home, each with a color and an adjective: η μεγάλη πόρτα, το κόκκινο βιβλίο.
> - Play I spy (Βλέπω κάτι…) with someone at home.
>
> **Out in the world:** Walk through your home and stick hand-written Greek labels on five things (η κουζίνα, το παράθυρο, το τραπέζι…). Leave them up all week.
>
> **Watch-list:** καινούργιος is ke-NOOR-yos. Watch for agreement slips like η μεγάλο. κόκκινη vs κόκκινο is the most common error.
