## Today's phrases

Make the gesture every time you say the phrase. It helps the phrase stick.

| Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| **Η οικογένειά μου είναι από…** | My family is from… | Hug-circle with your arms, then point to the map. |
| **Την Κυριακή πάω στην εκκλησία.** | On Sunday I go to church. | Sign of the cross, then walk your fingers. |
| **Τι τρως;** | What do you eat? | Fingers to the mouth, palms up. |
| **Μιλάς ελληνικά;** | Do you speak Greek? | Talking-puppet hand toward the partner. |
| **Θέλω να μάθω να μαγειρεύω.** | I want to learn to cook. | Open a book, then stir a pot. |
| **σαν εσένα** | like you | Point at yourself, then the partner, and put two index fingers side by side. |

## Main scene

**A day out: mid-year review scene**: One long scene that stitches together Lessons 1–15: making plans, the market, a lost phone, and the café.

**New words:** καφέδες

- A: **Γεια σου, Νίκο! Τι κάνεις;** — Hi, Nikos! How are you?
- B: **Καλά! Μπορείς σήμερα;** — Good! Can you get together today?
- A: **Σήμερα δεν μπορώ, έχω δουλειά. Αύριο στις δέκα;** — Today I can't, I have work. Tomorrow at ten?
- B: **Εντάξει. Πάμε στη λαϊκή;** — Okay. Shall we go to the market?
- A: **Ναι! Θέλω ντομάτες και ελιές.** — Yes! I want tomatoes and olives.
- B (next day, at the market): **Πόσο κάνουν οι ελιές;** — How much are the olives?
- Seller: **Πέντε ευρώ το κιλό.** — Five euros a kilo.
- B: **Ένα κιλό, παρακαλώ.** — A kilo, please.
- A: **Ωχ! Πού είναι το κινητό μου;** — Oh no! Where's my phone?
- B: **Δεν ξέρω… Δεν βλέπω το κινητό σου.** — I don't know… I don't see your phone.
- A: **Εδώ είναι! Στην τσάντα μου.** — Here it is! In my bag.
- B: **Πάμε για καφέ;** — Shall we get a coffee?
- A: **Πάμε!** — Let's go!
- Waiter: **Τι θέλετε;** — What would you like?
- A: **Δύο καφέδες, παρακαλώ.** — Two coffees, please.
- B: **Τι ώρα είναι;** — What time is it?
- A: **Είναι δώδεκα.** — It's twelve.
- B: **Ωχ, είναι αργά. Θέλω να πάω στο σπίτι.** — Oh no, it's late. I want to go home.
- A: **Τα λέμε, Νίκο!** — See you, Nikos!

## Practice

> [!tip]- Replays, swaps, and twists
> Replay the scene with a partner, with the page face-down. Peek only when you get stuck. Change one thing each time.
>
> **Swap one thing**
>
> - Plan: σήμερα · αύριο στις δέκα · την Κυριακή
> - Place: στη λαϊκή · στο καφενείο · στη θάλασσα
> - Problem: Πού είναι το κινητό μου; · Δεν έχω ρέστα. · Δεν έχουμε…
> - An old line: work in one review phrase, for example **Μιλάτε αγγλικά;**
>
> **Twists to try with a partner**
>
> - Draw one card from each earlier lesson's scene and blend them into the day out.
>
> **Every time**
>
> - Trade parts with your partner.
> - Tell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Retell round: tell the whole scene in Greek to three different partners, with less time each time (90, 60, then 45 seconds). When you're listening, just listen.

> [!example]- Build it with tiles
> Cut out your Lesson 16 tiles (shaded tiles are verbs). Lay out the sentence mat, then for each sentence:
>
> 1. Build it from the English, then read it aloud.
> 2. Turn it into a question: swap the . for ; and raise your voice at the end.
> 3. Make it negative: put δεν right before the verb.
> 4. Swap one tile for another word from the extra tiles.
> 5. Write your last version by hand in your notebook.
>
> - **A:** How much are the olives?
> - **B:** A kilo, please.
> - **C:** Two coffees, please.
>
> Extra tiles: δεν · ; · . · σήμερα · αύριο στις δέκα · την Κυριακή
