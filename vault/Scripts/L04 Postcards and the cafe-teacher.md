## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 12 new words on their printed list: κάνω (21) · τώρα (37) · άλλος (43) · τίποτα (63) · το σπίτι (75) · βλέπω (89) · καλημέρα (351) · η εκκλησία (621) · ο ήλιος (651) · η θάλασσα (668) · το βουνό (909) · η βάρκα (1200)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'I see the sea.'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα.**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα.** | I see the sea. | Shade your eyes with one hand, then make waves with the other. |
> > | **Τι βλέπεις;** | What do you see? | Point to your eyes, then to the partner, palms up. |
> > | **Τι κάνεις; – Καλά.** | How are you? – Good. | Palm-up hand toward the partner, then thumbs up for "Καλά". |
> > | **Τι θέλετε;** | What would you like? (polite / group) | Waiter pose: pretend to hold a notepad and pen. |
> > | **Ένα νερό, παρακαλώ.** | A water, please. | Hold up one finger, then mime a glass. |
> > | **Τίποτα άλλο;** | Anything else? | Tap the imaginary notepad with the pen, eyebrows up. |
> > | **Όχι, τίποτα.** | No, nothing. | Greek "no": head tilt up, then both hands sweep flat and apart. |
> > | **Μπορείς τώρα;** | Can you now? | Point at the floor twice, quickly ("now"). |
>
> **Anchor verb: βλέπω**  
> T: Βλέπω τη θάλασσα. Βλέπω ένα βουνό. Βλέπω τον ήλιο. Βλέπω μια βάρκα. Βλέπω μια εκκλησία.  
> **The four Τι questions:** Τι έχεις; · Τι θέλεις; · Τι μπορείς; · Τι βλέπεις;  
> **Scenario A: postcard description**  
> S2: Τι βλέπεις;  
> S1: Βλέπω τη θάλασσα και ένα σπίτι.  
> **Scenario B: first fully readable café dialogue**  
> Α: Τι θέλετε;  
> Β: Ένα νερό και ένα κρασί, παρακαλώ.  
> Α: Τίποτα άλλο;  
> Β: Όχι, τίποτα. Ευχαριστώ.  
> **Readable sentences:** Έχω ένα βιβλίο. · Θέλω νερό, παρακαλώ. · Τι βλέπεις; · Μπορείς τώρα; · Τι κάνεις; – Καλά.  
>
> Full teacher transcript: [[Greekquent Year One Curriculum-teacher|Greekquent Year One Curriculum]], Section 3.
>
> **Recall 1 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three of today's phrases. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

## 3 · Main scene

**Postcard and a café order**: Part 1: describe a postcard from Greece. Part 2: order at a café.

**Familiarity:** 96% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** καλημέρα

- A: **Τι βλέπεις;** — What do you see?
- B: **Βλέπω τη θάλασσα. Βλέπω και μια βάρκα.** — I see the sea. I also see a boat.
- A: **Βλέπεις τον ήλιο;** — Do you see the sun?
- B: **Ναι, βλέπω τον ήλιο. Και ένα βουνό.** — Yes, I see the sun. And a mountain.
- A: **Βλέπεις μια εκκλησία;** — Do you see a church?
- B: **Ναι, βλέπω μια εκκλησία και ένα σπίτι.** — Yes, I see a church and a house.
- Waiter: **Καλημέρα! Τι θέλετε;** — Good morning! What would you like?
- A: **Καλημέρα! Ένα νερό και έναν καφέ, παρακαλώ.** — Good morning! A water and a coffee, please.
- Waiter: **(to B) Τι θέλετε;** — What would you like?
- B: **Ένα τσάι, παρακαλώ.** — A tea, please.
- Waiter: **Τίποτα άλλο;** — Anything else?
- A: **Όχι, τίποτα. Ευχαριστώ.** — No, nothing. Thank you.
- Waiter: **Ορίστε.** — Here you are.
- A: **Τι κάνεις, Μαρία;** — How are you, Maria?
- B: **Καλά! Εσύ;** — Good! You?

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Postcard: τη θάλασσα · τον ήλιο · ένα βουνό · μια βάρκα · μια εκκλησία · ένα σπίτι
> - Order: ένα νερό · έναν καφέ · ένα τσάι · ένα κρασί · ένα γλυκό
> - Article check: τη (feminine) · τον (masculine) · το (neuter)
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Μπορώ;**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Mystery postcard: B describes a postcard A can't see, and A has to pick it out of three.
> - The waiter brings the wrong thing on purpose. The customer says Όχι, θέλω… and tries again.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "Do you see the sun?" · B: "Do you see a church?" · C: "A tea, please."
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** papers away. Each student writes two of today's phrases by hand on an index card, then says them with the gesture, plus one true sentence about themselves.
