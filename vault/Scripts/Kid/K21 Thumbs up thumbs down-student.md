# Thumbs up, thumbs down

**The game:** Taste tests, music clips, and pictures: the kid gives a thumbs up or down and says whether he likes it.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Σου αρέσει;** — Do you like it?
- You: **Μου αρέσει!** — I like it!
- You: **Δεν μου αρέσει!** — I don't like it!
- You: **Μου αρέσουν τα αυτοκίνητα.** — I like cars.
- You: **Μου αρέσει να τρέχω.** — I like to run.
- You: **Μου αρέσει η μουσική!** — I like the music!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Σου αρέσει;** — Do you like it?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

A like / don't-like collage: two halves of a page, with pictures cut out or drawn.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Μου αρέσει…**: Hand on heart and a big smile.
- **Μου αρέσουν…**: Hand on heart, then point to several things.
- **Μου αρέσει να μαγειρεύω.**: Hand on heart, then stir a pot.
- **Σου αρέσει η μουσική;**: Point at the partner, then air-guitar or sway.
- **Θέλω να τρέξω.**: Pump your arms as if running.
- **Πάω στο γυμναστήριο.**: Lift an imaginary dumbbell.
