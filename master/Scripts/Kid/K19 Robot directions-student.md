# Robot directions

**The game:** The teacher is a robot, and the kid guides it to a hidden flag using only Greek directions.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Πού είναι η σημαία;** — Where is the flag?
- You: **Ευθεία!** — Straight ahead!
- You: **Δεξιά! Αριστερά!** — Right! Left!
- You: **Σταμάτα!** — Stop!
- Grown-up: **Δεν βλέπω τη σημαία!** — I can't see the flag!
- You: **Είναι κοντά!** — It's close!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Πού είναι η σημαία;** — Where is the flag?
- You (teacher): **Δεν βλέπω τη σημαία!** — I can't see the flag!
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

Make the Greek flag together for the 25th of March: blue and white stripes, with a cross in the corner.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Πού είναι η σημαία;**: Robot arms, head turning stiffly.
- **Ευθεία!**: Both arms point straight ahead.
- **Δεξιά!**: Swing your right arm out.
- **Αριστερά!**: Swing your left arm out.
- **Σταμάτα!**: Hand up flat, and freeze.
- **Είναι κοντά!**: Bring your hands close together.
