> [!tip]- Ask your own
> 1. Open a word or lesson note and tap **Ask** in the left toolbar (Intermediate and up), then **Ask on video instead**.
> 2. Record up to **45 seconds**: say the word, then ask your question.
> 3. Type your first name, and tick the boxes you are happy with:
>    - **Public library:** your video and first name go into this list and the [online question library](https://gtkd321.github.io/greekquent-questions/), which anyone can watch.
>    - **Promo videos:** a few seconds of your clip, with your first name on screen, can appear in Greekquent promo videos.
> 4. Tap **Share to GroupMe** and pick the class group. Everyone sees it right away, and your teacher answers there.
>
> Changed your mind? Tell your teacher and the clip comes out of the library.

> [!question]- Good questions to ask
> - When do I use this word, and when do I use a similar one?
> - How do I say this in the past or the future?
> - Did I pronounce this right? (Say it in your video.)
> - What does this phrase from the lesson really mean?
