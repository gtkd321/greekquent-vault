# Kid variant · Lesson 15: Puppet who only speaks Greek

Play version of [[L15 Do you speak Greek-teacher|Lesson 15: Do you speak Greek]] · [[K15 Puppet who only speaks Greek-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** A sock puppet doesn't speak English, so the kid has to slow it down, ask it to repeat, and teach it words.

**You'll need:** a sock with button eyes (or any puppet)

## How to play

1. Puppet talks too fast. The kid has to say Πιο σιγά!
2. Puppet mumbles. The kid says Ξανά!
3. Then the kid teaches the puppet an English word, and the puppet gets it wrong in a funny way.

## Lines

- T (puppet): **Γεια! Μιλάς αγγλικά;** — Hi! Do you speak English?
- K: **Ναι! Μιλάς ελληνικά;** — Yes! Do you speak Greek?
- K: **Πιο σιγά, παρακαλώ!** — Slower, please!
- K: **Ξανά, παρακαλώ!** — Again, please!
- K: **Δεν καταλαβαίνω!** — I don't understand!
- T (puppet): **Δεν ξέρω αγγλικά…** — I don't know English…

## Make it

Making the puppet: a sock, buttons, yarn hair.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Tourist role-play: the adult is lost in Athens and has to get help from a local who only speaks Greek.
