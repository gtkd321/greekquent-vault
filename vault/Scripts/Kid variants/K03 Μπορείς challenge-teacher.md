# Kid variant · Lesson 3: Μπορείς; challenge

Play version of [[L03 Making plans-teacher|Lesson 3: Making plans]] · [[K03 Μπορείς challenge-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Quick physical challenges. Easy ones get Ναι, μπορώ! and impossible ones get the keystone sentence.

**You'll need:** nothing needed; optional sticker chart

## How to play

1. Teacher demonstrates a move (hop on one foot, touch your toes, spin twice) and asks Μπορείς;
2. Kid tries it and answers.
3. Throw in impossible ones (touch the ceiling, lick your elbow) so he gets to say Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ!
4. Permission rule: before taking a sticker, he has to ask Μπορώ;

## Lines

- T: **Μπορείς;** — Can you?
- K: **Ναι, μπορώ!** — Yes, I can!
- T: **Μπορεί!** — He can!
- K: **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ!** — I want to, but I can't!
- K: **Μπορώ;** — May I?
- T: **Ναι, βέβαια.** — Yes, of course.

## Make it

A sticker chart with a strong-arm picture at the top. One sticker per Μπορώ; he asks correctly.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Balance or stretching challenges work just as well. Also practice the polite Μπορώ; at the door, at the table, and when reaching for something.
