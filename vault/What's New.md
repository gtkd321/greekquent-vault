> - Sunday: **Read it line by line** walks through the week's story one line per card, then all together.

> [!info]- November 17, evening: One card at a time

> - The tool buttons (Look up a word, Send homework) stay underneath, always one tap away.

> [!info]- November 17, later: Genders that agree
> - **The graph follows the noun.** Tap a noun in the word graph and the describing words around it switch to its gender: tap η πόλη and μεγάλος becomes μεγάλη, ο becomes η, ένας becomes μία. Nouns now show their article, so you always see the gender.
> - **Agrees with strip.** Under the word's name you get ready phrases to tap and hear, like μεγάλη πόλη and αρχαία πόλη. Open a describing word like μεγάλος to see it change with each noun: μεγάλο σπίτι, μεγάλη πόλη, μεγάλος αδερφός.
> - **Story links in the graph.** Words that meet in this year's story now link to each other (see "Together in the story" on a word note). That makes many more sparks. A story spark shows the scene where the words met.
> - **Story Lines cards fixed.** A card now shows the form that was said, so το κινητό shows το, not ο. In the text adventure the wrong gender gets "Almost. Right word, but it has to agree with the noun."

> [!info]- November 17: The whole year as one story
> - **Story Lines for all 300 days.** Every day's line on Today is now part of one story that runs the whole year. Tap **Watch it** or **Play it** under the line. Both open right here in the vault, no browser or internet needed. Γιαγιά opens here too.
> - **Sparks in the word graph.** Some words keep turning up together, like ζωή, άνθρωπος and χάνω. When you open a word in the graph, Greekquent finds three that all link to each other and lights them up in gold. Tap **One sentence** to use all three in one sentence, then save it to My pairs. **Another** shows the next spark.
> - **Η and Ο read right.** The voice no longer reads a capital Η or Ο at the start of a sentence as a letter name.

> [!info]- November 16, evening: Watch it, then play it
> - **Story Lines.** Week 1's story is now a short cartoon for each day, with Greek subtitles that light up as the characters speak. [Open it](https://www.perplexity.ai/computer/a/greekquent-story-lines-1bIJ0178Q2KldfTqtqgKZA), or tap **Watch it** under the day's line on Today.

> [!info]- November 16, overnight: A story every day
> - **Today's line.** Each day now has one short story line that uses that day's words (they are in bold). The same people all week: Μαρία, Νίκος, γιαγιά, παππούς, θεία and the νονός. Say it, tap Hear it, then tap **I said it**.
> - **The week in six lines.** Each line adds to the one before, so by Saturday you can tell a little story. On Sunday, Today shows the whole story to read aloud. Every week page has it too, under This week's story.
> - **Comebacks.** Lines you said 1, 3 and 7 days ago come back on Today. You see the English, say it in Greek, then tap to check. Spacing like this helps words stick ([a study of spaced practice with young learners](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/2331186X.2017.1287391)).
> - **Missed a few days?** Today picks up where you left off, one or two lines at a time, or you can jump to today. Nothing is lost.
> - **Kid track.** For younger learners: tap **Kid track** at the bottom of Today or in the More menu. Today then shows the kid lesson lines, one more each day, with the easy words (things you can point at) first ([research on easy-to-picture words](https://academic.oup.com/applij/article/43/2/293/6277142)).
> - **You are the teacher.** Every kid lesson now ends with a swap: the kid asks the questions and catches the grown-up's mistakes.
> - **New game: Γιαγιά.** Find 7 notebooks in the house before Γιαγιά feeds you. Each notebook is a word. [Open it](https://www.perplexity.ai/computer/a/greekquent-giagia-T2tsaLMvQiuN84Ng0un.IA), or tap Play Γιαγιά on Today.
> - **Cleaner examples.** Odd or grown-up example sentences on word cards and in the Anki deck were replaced with everyday ones.
> - **Updates run in the background.** When an update is ready, tap the notice and keep working. A small chip in the corner shows the progress. Your My Work is never touched.
