## Group meet prompt

**Who has the coin**

- **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;** (Who has the coin?)
- **Πόσα κομμάτια θέλεις;** (How many pieces do you want?)

**Bridge:** Count real objects around the room up to ten, then ask Πόσα… έχεις;
