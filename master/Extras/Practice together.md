# Practice together

Practicing next to someone makes it easier to start and to keep going. Everyone works on their own Greek, quietly, at the same time, on a video call or at the same table. Back to [[Today]].

- **At home:** sit next to a friend or family member while they do their own thing. Do today's line, then say it to them.
- **Online:** when your teacher sets a weekly drop-in time, it shows here and on Today's class card with a Join button.
