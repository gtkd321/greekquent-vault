# Traffic-light verb dice

**The game:** Roll a homemade die for the verb, then draw a color for the kind of sentence, using your red/yellow/green colors.

## Say it

- You (green): **Έχω μια μπάλα.** — I have a ball.
- You (red): **Δεν θέλω ψωμί.** — I don't want bread.
- You (red): **Δεν μπορώ τώρα. Αύριο!** — I can't now. Tomorrow!
- You (yellow): **Βλέπεις τη θάλασσα;** — Do you see the sea?
- Grown-up: **Μπράβο!** — Well done!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Θέλεις ψωμί;** — Do you want bread?
- You (teacher): **Βλέπεις τη θάλασσα;** — Do you see the sea?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

Build the die together and let the kid draw the four pictures. The colors match the laminated packet, so the game teaches the system too.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Έχω**: Hug it to your chest.
- **Δεν θέλω**: Push it away with both hands, nose wrinkled.
- **Βλέπεις**: Point at your eye, then at the other person.
- **Αύριο!**: Roll one hand forward, over and over.
- **Μπράβο!**: Clap twice.
