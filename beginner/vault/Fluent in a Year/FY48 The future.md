## Group meet prompt

**The future**

- **Πού θα είσαι σε πέντε χρόνια;** (Where will you be in five years?)
- **Τι θέλεις να αλλάξεις;** (What do you want to change?)

**Bridge:** Each person states one real goal with θα and a date.
