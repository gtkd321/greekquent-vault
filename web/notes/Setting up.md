# Setting up

Two minutes, once. Back to [[Today]].

> [!tip]- 1. Put it on your home screen
> - **iPhone:** open this page in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
> - **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then **Install app**.
> - Or tap **More**, then **Put it on my home screen**.
>
> Open Greekquent from the home screen icon from now on. It works without internet once it has loaded.

> [!tip]- 2. The three tabs (Today, Words, Class)
> They sit along the bottom of the screen (on a computer too).
> - **Today** (house): today's story (Scene, Words, Build, Say it), then review. Doing Fluent in a Year? It is a pace for the same story: one day each day, so the 1,500 words take a year. Pick it when the app first asks how far you want to go, or turn on **Fluent in a Year pace** in Options. The story card then says whether you are on pace or a few days behind.
> - **Words** (list): all the words, most frequent first. Search for any word in English or Greek at the top, or open the Word web.
> - **Class** (two people): this week's lesson, homework and Ask the class. **Settings and help** is at the bottom: Options, the tour, What's new, and saving a copy of your work.
>
> At the top right: **Resources** (verb tables and more) and **More**: Side panel, Look up a word, Word web, Ask the class, Send homework, Options and the rest.

> [!tip]- Less on the page, more in the side panel
> Lessons and word pages show the main things first. The extras (word pairs, words that go together, review prompts, word links) sit in the side panel: tap **More ›** on a word's button row or near the top of a lesson. On Today, tap **More**, then **Side panel**. It slides in from the right. Every other article is under **Extras** at the bottom of the side panel.

> [!tip]- 3. Color your words
> Every word page has a row of buttons just under the meaning: **☆ Star**, **Hear it**, **Word web**, **More ›**, **On paper**, and three colors: **Don't have it** (red), **Kind of** (yellow), **Have it** (green). Tap one after you try to say the word from memory. Tap it again to clear it. Red words come back on the Today page.

> [!tip]- 4. Your own notes
> On Today, tap **More**, then **Side panel**, then **My homework**, and type. Updates never change your work. The lesson pages stay as they are so they can't change by accident.

> [!tip]- 5. Updates and your work
> - Updates arrive by themselves in the background. Nothing to tap.
> - Your colors, φόρα and homework live on this phone (or computer) only. It saves as you go and updates never touch it. On an iPhone, open it from the home screen icon: in a Safari tab, iPhones clear site data after about a week away.
