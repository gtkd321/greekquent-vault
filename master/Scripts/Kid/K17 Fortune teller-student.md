# Fortune teller

**The game:** A folded-paper fortune teller (cootie catcher) predicts weekend plans in the future tense.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What will you do this weekend?
- You: **Θα πάω στο πάρκο!** — I'll go to the park!
- You: **Θα δω μια ταινία.** — I'll watch a movie.
- You: **Θα παίξω με τους φίλους.** — I'll play with my friends.
- Grown-up: **Θα έχεις ώρα;** — Will you have time?

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What will you do this weekend?
- You (teacher): **Θα έχεις ώρα;** — Will you have time?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The fortune teller, colored on the outside with drawings of each plan inside.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;**: Roll a hand forward, then stretch out relaxed.
- **Θα έχω ώρα.**: Roll the hand forward, then tap the wrist and smile.
- **Θα δω μια ταινία.**: Roll the hand forward, then mime eating popcorn.
- **Θα πάμε βόλτα.**: Link arms with a partner and stroll a few steps.
- **Θα πάω…**: Step forward onto the "tomorrow" line on the floor.
