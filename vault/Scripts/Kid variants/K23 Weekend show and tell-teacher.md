# Kid variant · Lesson 23: Weekend show and tell

Play version of [[L23 Last weekend-teacher|Lesson 23: Last weekend]] · [[K23 Weekend show and tell-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** The kid draws what he did last weekend and shows it, using the past-tense chunks.

**You'll need:** paper, markers, a few weekend photos

## How to play

1. Kid draws three things he did last weekend.
2. He presents them one by one, like a show-and-tell.
3. Teacher asks follow-up questions.

## Lines

- T: **Τι έκανες το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What did you do last weekend?
- K: **Πήγα στο πάρκο.** — I went to the park.
- K: **Είδα έναν σκύλο!** — I saw a dog!
- K: **Έφαγα πίτσα.** — I ate pizza.
- K: **Ήπια γάλα.** — I drank milk.
- T: **Τι είπε η μαμά;** — What did Mom say?

## Make it

The weekend drawing, which can go on the fridge or in the vault as a photo.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Show real phone photos from the weekend and tell the story behind each one.
