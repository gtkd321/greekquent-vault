# Setting up

For grown-ups. Two minutes, once. Back to [[Today]].

> [!tip]- 1. Put it on your home screen
> - **iPhone:** open this page in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
> - **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then **Install app**.
> - Or tap **More**, then **Put it on my home screen**.
>
> It works without internet once it has loaded.

> [!tip]- 2. How a week works
> Each week has one game, the same week as the grown-up class. **Today** shows it as a stack of cards. Swipe, or tap the arrows, for the next card.
> 1. **Letters and sounds:** a few Greek letters at a time, until they are all done.
> 2. **Listen first:** two small figures say today's part of the story. Just listen.
> 3. **This week's words:** tap **Hear**, then pick a color.
> 4. **Story puzzle:** put the words in order.
> 5. **Say it together:** one new line a day. Say the earlier lines too. Sunday is the whole game.
> 6. **Hello song:** sing it together.
> 7. **This week's game:** what you need and how to play.
> 8. **Now you are the teacher:** swap. The kid asks the questions, and the grown-up gets one wrong on purpose.
> 9. **Do the moves:** a gesture for each phrase.
> 10. **Dessert word:** one more word to say, act out and draw.
>
> Weeks with no class keep last week's game.

> [!tip]- 3. The tabs (Today, Words, Class)
> They sit along the bottom of the screen.
> - **Today** (house): this week's game.
> - **Words** (list): the words, with a search box at the top for any word in English or Greek.
> - **Class** (two people): this week's lesson, homework and Ask the class. **Settings and help** is at the bottom.
>
> After the last card: **Play Γιαγιά** (a word game with this week's words), **All lessons**, and **Look up a word**.

> [!tip]- 4. Colors
> Red is "not yet", yellow is "kind of", green is "got it". Tap the color after the kid tries the word from memory. Tap it again to clear it.

> [!tip]- 5. Updates and your work
> - Updates arrive by themselves in the background. Nothing to tap.
> - Colors, φόρα and notes live on this phone (or computer) only. It saves as you go and updates never touch it. On an iPhone, open it from the home screen icon: in a Safari tab, iPhones clear site data after about a week away.
