# Kid variant · Lesson 2: Teddy's tea party

Play version of [[L02 Guest at a Greek home-teacher|Lesson 2: Guest at a Greek home]] · [[K02 Teddy's tea party-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** The kid is the host and offers drinks to stuffed animals. The toys answer in silly voices.

**You'll need:** stuffed animals or dolls, play cups (or real ones with water), a small plate of crackers

## How to play

1. Kid pours pretend drinks and offers one to each toy.
2. Teacher gives each toy a voice and has it say yes or no, thank you.
3. Then the kid reports to you what each toy wants.

## Lines

- K: **Θέλεις νερό;** — Do you want water?
- T (as teddy): **Ναι, θέλω λίγο νερό, ευχαριστώ!** — Yes, I'd like a little water, thank you!
- K: **Θέλεις ένα γλυκό;** — Do you want a sweet?
- T (as doll): **Όχι, ευχαριστώ.** — No, thank you.
- T: **Τι θέλει το αρκουδάκι;** — What does the teddy want?
- K: **Το αρκουδάκι θέλει γάλα.** — The teddy wants milk.
- K: **Η κούκλα θέλει τσάι, αλλά…** — The doll wants tea, but…

## Make it

The kid draws a little picture menu (water, milk, tea, a sweet) for the toy café and points to it while serving.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Host a real coffee for each other. Practice refusing politely and offering a second time, the way a Greek host would.
