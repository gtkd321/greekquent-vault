# 09 Games

Links: [[Today]] · [[00 Greekquent Index]] · [[07 Fluent in a Year]]

All five games are part of the vault. They open here in Obsidian, need no internet, and update with the vault.

> [!summary]- Greekquent Buzzer (fastest fingers)
> [Open the Greekquent Buzzer](obsidian://greekquent?open=buzzer) (works offline)
> - Lay the phone flat between two students sitting face to face. The screen is split, so each student reads their own half the right way up.
> - Each round shows an English word and two Greek choices. First correct tap wins the point. A wrong tap locks that student out, so the other can still answer.
> - **Words from:** a lesson (phrases and words), a Fluent in a Year week, or the top 50 to 1,500 words. You can mix in earlier lessons or weeks.
> - **Options:** play to 5, 10, or 15. Reverse mode (Greek prompt, English choices). The phone says the Greek answer aloud. 7-second rounds.
> - Missed words come back a few rounds later. At the end, the app lists them so students can mark them red on their printed list.
> - The end screen shows each player's hit rate. Aim for about 85% right over a few games.
> - Free, with no accounts. On Today, open **More** and tap **Play Buzzer with these** to play today's or review words. Turn off auto-lock and silent mode before class.

> [!summary]- Greekquent Cousins (spot the English cousin)
> [Open Greekquent Cousins](obsidian://greekquent?open=cousins) (works offline)
> - The same face-to-face split screen as the Buzzer, plus **Solo practice** for remote students. Works on iPhone and Android.
> - **Find the cousin:** a Greek word and two English words. Pick the cousin (χρόνος: chronology or chocolate).
> - **Guess the meaning:** a Greek word and its cousin. Pick what the Greek word means (φόβος, cousin phobia: fear or star).
> - **Levels:** Twins (ιδέα, idea), Cousins (καρδιά, cardiac), Hidden cousins (σπίτι, hospital; γεια, hygiene). 157 pairs, all from the 1,500.
> - **Letter rules:** the setup screen lists them (χ → ch, φ → ph, θ → th, ψ → ps, ξ → x, υ → y, κ → c, initial vowel → h), and you can play only the words for one rule. Every answer shows the rule and a one-line bridge, for example "Strategy was first the art of leading an army."
> - 85% hit-rate check and a missed list at the end, like the Buzzer.

> [!summary]- Greekquent Τι λέμε; (hello and goodbye through the day)
> [Open Τι λέμε;](obsidian://greekquent?open=hello) (works offline)
> - One player, 10 quick scenes. Each shows the time of day on a sky and a clock, who you meet, where, and whether you arrive or leave. Pick what a Greek would say.
> - **Level 1, Hello:** Καλημέρα until about 1 pm, Καλησπέρα after lunch.
> - **Level 2, Hello and goodbye:** leaving is different. Καλό απόγευμα, Καλό βράδυ, Καληνύχτα, Καλή μέρα. Καλησπέρα is never a goodbye, and Καληνύχτα is never a hello.
> - **Level 3, Like a local:** σου or σας, Καλή εβδομάδα on Monday, Καλό Σαββατοκύριακο on Friday, Καλό μήνα on the 1st, Καλό μεσημέρι, Καλή συνέχεια.
> - 8 of 10 opens the next level. Every answer gives the reason, and the roots: καλή + εσπέρα (old word for evening), απόγευμα = από + γεύμα (after the meal).
> - Right on the first try earns φόρα for that word. Missed ones can go to Review.
> - In class: put it on the screen and let the room call out the answer before anyone taps.

> [!summary]- Greekquent Γιαγιά (find the notebooks, don't get fed)
> [Open Greekquent Γιαγιά](obsidian://greekquent?open=giagia) (works offline)
> - One player, made for kids and remote students. Works on iPhone and Android (d-pad or swipe) and with arrow keys on a computer.
> - Walk around the house (Κουζίνα, Σαλόνι, Διάδρομος, Υπνοδωμάτιο, Μπάνιο, Μπαλκόνι) and find 7 τετράδια. Each one says a Greek word aloud and gives three English meanings.
> - Right answer: Γιαγιά stops for 2 seconds ("Μπράβο!"). Wrong answer: she gets a little faster, and the notebook stays so the word comes back.
> - She catches you 3 times and it's dinner ("Φάε, παιδί μου!"). Find all 7 and the door opens.
> - **Words from:** any kid lesson (with earlier lessons mixed in), the top 100, 300 or all 1,500, or today's words from the Today page.
> - The end screen lists missed words with Hear buttons and can send them to Review on Today.

> [!summary]- Greekquent Story Lines (watch it, then play it)
> [Open Story Lines](obsidian://greekquent?open=story) (works offline)
> - **The whole year:** 300 days, one story. Μαρία, Νίκος and the family follow the notebooks of a great-aunt to a house in the village. Pick any week and day.
> - **Watch:** each day's story line as a short cartoon. The Greek subtitles light up word by word as the characters speak, and things appear when their word is said. Slower and English buttons; Whole week plays Monday to Saturday.
> - **Text adventure:** the same scene as an 80s-style text game. It only moves on when you type the right Greek word. Greeklish works too (nero = νερό), so no Greek keyboard is needed. HELP gives a clue; after two misses, three word buttons appear.
> - Works on iPhone and Android.
> - On Today, tap **Watch it** or **Play it as a text adventure** under the day's line.

> [!summary]- Run it in class
> 1. Pick the current lesson, play to 5.
> 2. Winner stays, the next student challenges.
> 3. Last round of the night: the Fluent in a Year week with earlier weeks mixed in.
