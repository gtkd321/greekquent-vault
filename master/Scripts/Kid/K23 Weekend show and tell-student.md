# Weekend show and tell

**The game:** The kid draws what he did last weekend and shows it, using the past-tense chunks.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Τι έκανες το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What did you do last weekend?
- You: **Πήγα στο πάρκο.** — I went to the park.
- You: **Είδα έναν σκύλο!** — I saw a dog!
- You: **Έφαγα πίτσα.** — I ate pizza.
- You: **Ήπια γάλα.** — I drank milk.
- Grown-up: **Τι είπε η μαμά;** — What did Mom say?

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι έκανες το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What did you do last weekend?
- You (teacher): **Τι είπε η μαμά;** — What did Mom say?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The weekend drawing, which can go on the fridge or in the vault as a photo.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Τι έκανες**: Point back over your shoulder, palms up.
- **Πήγα**: Walk two fingers across the table.
- **έναν σκύλο**: Pant like a puppy, hands up like paws.
- **Έφαγα πίτσα**: Pick up a big slice and bite.
- **Ήπια γάλα**: Gulp from a pretend glass, then wipe your milk mustache.
- **Τι είπε η μαμά;**: Hand by your ear, listening.
