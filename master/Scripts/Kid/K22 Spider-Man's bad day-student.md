# Spider-Man's bad day

**The game:** A toy acts out yesterday's story in a three-panel comic, giving lots of practice with the anchor verbs in the past.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Χθες ο Spider-Man ήθελε να φάει…** — Yesterday Spider-Man wanted to eat…
- Grown-up: **…αλλά δεν είχε ψωμί!** — …but he didn't have any bread!
- Grown-up: **Ήθελε να παίξει, αλλά δεν μπορούσε.** — He wanted to play, but he couldn't.
- You: **Χθες ήθελα να πάω στο πάρκο, αλλά δεν μπορούσα.** — Yesterday I wanted to go to the park, but I couldn't.
- You: **Έβλεπα τηλεόραση.** — I was watching TV.

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Χθες ο Spider-Man ήθελε να φάει…** — Yesterday Spider-Man wanted to eat…
- You (teacher): **…αλλά δεν είχε ψωμί!** — …but he didn't have any bread!
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The three-panel comic.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Χθες ήθελα να πάω, αλλά δεν μπορούσα.**: Thumb over the shoulder ("yesterday"), reach out, then pull back.
- **Είχα πολλή δουλειά.**: Thumb over the shoulder, then mime a tall stack of papers.
- **Δεν μπορούσα.**: Thumb over the shoulder, then a head shake.
- **Ήμουν στο σπίτι.**: Thumb over the shoulder, then a roof over your head.
- **Πέρσι ήμουν στην Ελλάδα.**: Throw your thumb far back over the shoulder, then point east.
