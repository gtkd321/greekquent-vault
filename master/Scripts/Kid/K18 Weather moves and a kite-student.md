# Weather moves and a kite

**The game:** Each kind of weather gets a body movement. Then make a small kite for Καθαρά Δευτέρα.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Τι καιρό κάνει;** — What's the weather like?
- You: **Κάνει κρύο!** — It's cold!
- You: **Κάνει ζέστη!** — It's hot!
- You: **Βρέχει!** — It's raining!
- You: **Έχει ήλιο!** — It's sunny!
- You: **Βλέπω τον χαρταετό!** — I see the kite!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Τι καιρό κάνει;** — What's the weather like?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The kite, in bright colors, with a tail of tissue paper.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Τι καιρό κάνει;**: Look up at the sky, palm up.
- **Κάνει κρύο!**: Hug yourself and shiver.
- **Κάνει ζέστη!**: Fan your face.
- **Βρέχει!**: Wiggle your fingers down like rain.
- **Έχει ήλιο!**: Arms make a big sun over your head.
- **Βλέπω τον χαρταετό!**: Shade your eyes and tug a kite string.
