# Treasure hunt review

**The game:** Stations around the house, one per earlier game. At each station the kid says a sentence to get the next clue.

## Say it

- You: **Έχω ένα κλειδί!** — I have a key!
- You: **Θέλεις νερό;** — Do you want water?
- You: **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ!** — I want to, but I can't!
- You: **Ο Spider-Man είναι κάτω από το τραπέζι!** — Spider-Man is under the table!
- You: **Πάω στη θάλασσα!** — I'm going to the sea!
- Grown-up: **Μπράβο! Εδώ είναι!** — Well done! Here it is!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Θέλεις νερό;** — Do you want water?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

Clue cards drawn by the teacher (or older kids), each with a picture from that lesson's game.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **ένα κλειδί**: Turn a pretend key: click.
- **Θέλεις νερό;**: Pour from a pretend teapot.
- **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ!**: Reach out, then let your arms flop.
- **κάτω από**: Point down under the table.
- **στη θάλασσα**: Swim with your arms.
- **Εδώ είναι!**: Point down hard at the spot and smile.
