# Kid variant · Lesson 16: Treasure hunt review

Play version of [[L16 Mid-year review-teacher|Lesson 16: Mid-year review]] · [[K16 Treasure hunt review-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Stations around the house, one per earlier game. At each station the kid says a sentence to get the next clue.

**You'll need:** 6–8 clue cards with pictures from earlier lessons, a small prize at the end

## How to play

1. Each station has a picture: the bag, a cup, a strong arm, the telescope, Spider-Man, a pillow island…
2. The kid says a sentence that fits the picture. Then he gets the next clue.
3. The final clue leads to the prize.

## Lines

- K: **Έχω ένα κλειδί!** — I have a key!
- K: **Θέλεις νερό;** — Do you want water?
- K: **Θέλω, αλλά δεν μπορώ!** — I want to, but I can't!
- K: **Ο Spider-Man είναι κάτω από το τραπέζι!** — Spider-Man is under the table!
- K: **Πάω στη θάλασσα!** — I'm going to the sea!
- T: **Μπράβο! Εδώ είναι!** — Well done! Here it is!

## Make it

Clue cards drawn by the teacher (or older kids), each with a picture from that lesson's game.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Use this as a mid-year oral check: the stations become interview questions.
