# Print and download

Everything you print or download is on one page outside the app, so it is always the newest version. **[Open Print and download](https://gtkd321.github.io/frequent/greek/print/)**

- **The 1,500 words:** a printable list with a box to mark black, red, yellow or green.
- **Verb Sheet** and **Sentence Tiles** (print the tiles on card stock).
- **Year One Workbook:** one lesson at a time or the whole year.
- **Textbook:** print edition and screen edition.
- **Flashcards:** the Year One and Fluent in a Year decks for Anki, free.
