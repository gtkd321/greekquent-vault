# Kid variant · Lesson 5: Where is Spider-Man?

Play version of [[L05 Lost items and the shop-teacher|Lesson 5: Lost items and the shop]] · [[K05 Where is Spider-Man-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** Your starter: move a toy around the table and chair, and the kid says where it is. Then he hides it and you have to find it.

**You'll need:** one favorite toy (Spider-Man), a table, a chair, a box

## How to play

1. Teacher puts the toy in a spot and asks where it is. Kid answers with the whole sentence.
2. Go through the spots: on the table, under the table, on the chair, then hold it above the chair with your hand.
3. Add new spots once those are easy: in the box, behind the door, next to the book.
4. Hide-and-seek round: the kid hides the toy, and the teacher looks for it, complaining in Greek that he can't see it.

## Lines

- T: **Πού είναι ο Spider-Man;** — Where is Spider-Man?
- K: **Ο Spider-Man είναι πάνω στο τραπέζι.** — Spider-Man is on the table.
- K: **Ο Spider-Man είναι κάτω από το τραπέζι.** — Spider-Man is under the table.
- K: **Ο Spider-Man είναι πάνω στην καρέκλα.** — Spider-Man is on the chair.
- K: **Ο Spider-Man είναι πάνω από την καρέκλα.** — Spider-Man is above the chair (in the air).
- K: **Είναι μέσα στο κουτί.** — He's in the box.
- K: **Είναι πίσω από την πόρτα.** — He's behind the door.
- K: **Είναι δίπλα στο βιβλίο.** — He's next to the book.
- T: **Δεν βλέπω τον Spider-Man! Πού είναι;** — I can't see Spider-Man! Where is he?
- K: **Δεν ξέρω!** — I don't know!
- T: **Εδώ είναι!** — Here he is!

## Make it

The kid draws a simple map of the room and sticks a small Spider-Man sticker wherever the toy was found.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Hide real keys, phones, or glasses. That's the actual lost-item scene from the lesson, with real stakes.
>
> **Teacher note:** Two small fixes to the starter: write Πού with an accent when it means "where?" (Που without one means "that" or "where" in the middle of a sentence). And "on the chair" is πάνω στην καρέκλα, while πάνω από την καρέκλα means "above the chair", so hold the toy in the air for that one. The contrast is a fun one for kids to see.
