## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 11 new words on their printed list: για (13) · ποιος (52) · τρία (174) · μπράβο (405) · έξι (410) · δέκα (460) · το κομμάτι (461) · η γιαγιά (606) · τέσσερα (631) · η χρονιά (989) · εννιά (1392)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'Who has the coin?'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;** | Who has the coin? | Hold up an imaginary coin and look around the room. |
> > | **Εγώ έχω!** | I do! / I have it! | Jump up, coin held high. |
> > | **ένα κομμάτι** | a piece | Slice the air like a knife cutting cake. |
> > | **Καλή χρονιά!** | Happy New Year! | Raise a glass for a toast. |
>
> **Step 1: numbers 1–10**  
> T: (counts on fingers) ένα, δύο, τρία, τέσσερα, πέντε, έξι, εφτά, οχτώ, εννιά, δέκα. — Ss echo.  
> T: (holds up fingers) Πόσα; — Ss: Τέσσερα!  
> **Step 2: Ποιος έχει…;**  
> T: (hides the key from Lesson 1 in a student's hand) Ποιος έχει το κλειδί;  
> S1: Εγώ! Εγώ έχω!  
> T: Ποιος έχει το κινητό μου; — S2: Εγώ!  
> **Step 3: cutting the vasilopita**  
> T: (cuts) Ένα κομμάτι για τον Χριστό. Ένα κομμάτι για το σπίτι. Ένα κομμάτι για τη Μαρία…  
> T: Πόσα κομμάτια; — Ss: (count together) Ένα, δύο, τρία… δέκα!  
> **Warm-up dialogue: who has the coin?**  
> A: Καλή χρονιά!  
> B: Καλή χρονιά! Θέλεις ένα κομμάτι;  
> A: Ναι, ευχαριστώ.  
> B: Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;  
> A: Δεν έχω. Εσύ;  
> C: Εγώ! Εγώ έχω το φλουρί!  
> All: Μπράβο! Καλή χρονιά!  
> **Exit ticket:** Count from δέκα down to ένα, then ask one Ποιος έχει…; question.  
>
> Full teacher transcript, with English explanations: [[Greekquent Year One Curriculum-teacher|Greekquent Year One Curriculum]], Lesson 8.
>
> **Recall 1 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three of today's phrases. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

## 3 · Main scene

**Cutting the vasilopita**: The family cuts the New Year's cake. Count the pieces and find out who has the coin.

**Familiarity:** 97% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** βασιλόπιτα, η γιαγιά

- T: **Καλή χρονιά!** — Happy New Year!
- Ss: **Καλή χρονιά!** — Happy New Year!
- T: **Έχω μια βασιλόπιτα. Ένα, δύο, τρία… δέκα κομμάτια.** — I have a vasilopita. One, two, three… ten pieces.
- T: **Ένα κομμάτι για τον Χριστό. Ένα για το σπίτι.** — One piece for Christ. One for the house.
- T: **Ένα κομμάτι για τη Μαρία.** — One piece for Maria.
- Maria: **Ευχαριστώ!** — Thank you!
- T: **Ένα για τον Νίκο. Ένα για τη γιαγιά.** — One for Nikos. One for Grandma.
- T: **Ποιος έχει το φλουρί;** — Who has the coin?
- Maria: **Δεν έχω.** — I don't have it.
- Nikos: **Δεν έχω!** — I don't!
- Grandma: **Εγώ! Εγώ έχω το φλουρί!** — Me! I have the coin!
- Ss: **Μπράβο! Καλή χρονιά!** — Well done! Happy New Year!
- A: **Πόσα κομμάτια έχεις;** — How many pieces do you have?
- B: **Έχω δύο.** — I have two.
- A: **Δύο; Θέλω κι εγώ ένα!** — Two? I want one too!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Number: ένα · δύο · τρία · τέσσερα · πέντε · έξι · επτά · οκτώ · εννιά · δέκα
> - For whom: για τη μαμά · για τον μπαμπά · για τη γιαγιά · για τον παππού
> - Wish: Καλή χρονιά! · Χρόνια πολλά! · Ευτυχισμένο το νέο έτος!
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Είμαι ο/η… από…**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Really hide a coin under one of ten cups. Each student counts out loud to choose a cup.
> - Countdown: count backwards from ten together, then shout Καλή χρονιά!
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: ask about someone else (Έχει…; Θέλει…;).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (60, 45, then 30 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "One piece for Maria." · B: "Who has the coin?" · C: "How many pieces do you have?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Count from δέκα down to ένα, then ask one Ποιος έχει…; question. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Count objects at home in Greek (stairs, plates, shoes).
> - Say your phone number digit by digit in Greek.
> - Wish three people Καλή χρονιά! this week.
>
> **Out in the world:** Bake or buy any round cake, hide a coin in it, and cut it the Greek way for your family. Count the pieces out loud in Greek.
>
> **Watch-list:** δύο is DHEE-o (δ as the "th" in "this"). έξι is EK-si. εννιά is e-NYA. Watch for an English r in τρία; a light tap is better.
