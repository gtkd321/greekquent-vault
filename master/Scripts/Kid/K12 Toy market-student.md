# Toy market

**The game:** A play shop with price tags and play money, just like the λαϊκή.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Καλημέρα! Έχετε ντομάτες;** — Good morning! Do you have tomatoes?
- You: **Ναι, έχουμε!** — Yes, we do!
- Grown-up: **Πόσο κάνουν;** — How much are they?
- You: **Δύο ευρώ!** — Two euros!
- Grown-up: **Δύο ντομάτες, παρακαλώ.** — Two tomatoes, please.
- You: **Ορίστε!** — Here you go!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Καλημέρα! Έχετε ντομάτες;** — Good morning! Do you have tomatoes?
- You (teacher): **Πόσο κάνουν;** — How much are they?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

Price tags with drawings of the food. The kid can write the numbers in Greek words once he knows them.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Καλημέρα!**: Big wave and a smile.
- **ντομάτες**: Cup a round tomato in your hand.
- **Πόσο κάνουν;**: Rub your thumb and fingers together.
- **Δύο ευρώ!**: Put two coins on the table, one at a time.
- **παρακαλώ**: Hands together: please.
- **Ορίστε!**: Hand it over with both hands.
