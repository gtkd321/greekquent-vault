# Intermediate · Lesson 26: Capstone: my Greek year

Same theme as [[L26 Capstone and celebration-teacher|Lesson 26: Capstone and celebration]], with a longer scene · [[I26 Capstone my Greek year-student|Student version]] · all lessons in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**Setting:** Adults give a 2-minute talk about their year in Greek, mixing all the tenses, then take questions.

**Review first:** run the spaced review from [[L26 Capstone and celebration-teacher|Lesson 26]], but answer each card in 2 sentences with a reason (γιατί…) or a time (χθες, αύριο, κάθε μέρα).

## Scene

- S: **Φέτος ξεκίνησα μαθήματα ελληνικών τον Οκτώβριο.** — This year I started Greek lessons in October.
- S: **Στην αρχή δεν μπορούσα να διαβάσω ούτε μία λέξη, αλλά τώρα διαβάζω μικρές ιστορίες.** — At first I couldn't read a single word, but now I read short stories.
- S: **Μου άρεσαν πολύ τα μαθήματα για την ταβέρνα και τη λαϊκή, γιατί ήταν σαν πραγματική ζωή.** — I really liked the taverna and market lessons, because they were like real life.
- S: **Το πιο δύσκολο για μένα ήταν το παρελθόν.** — The hardest thing for me was the past tense.
- S: **Το καλοκαίρι θα πάω στην Ελλάδα και θέλω να μιλάω μόνο ελληνικά.** — This summer I'll go to Greece, and I want to speak only Greek.
- S: **Του χρόνου θα συνεχίσω, γιατί θέλω να καταλαβαίνω τη γιαγιά μου χωρίς βοήθεια.** — Next year I'll keep going, because I want to understand my grandmother without help.
- T: **Ποια λέξη σού αρέσει περισσότερο στα ελληνικά;** — What's your favorite word in Greek?
- T: **Τι θα κάνεις πρώτα όταν φτάσεις στην Ελλάδα;** — What will you do first when you get to Greece?

## After the scene

> [!question]- Talk: conversation questions
> Open questions for pairs. Answer in at least two sentences.
>
> - **Τι έμαθες φέτος;** — What did you learn this year?
> - **Τι θέλεις να μάθεις του χρόνου;** — What do you want to learn next year?
> - **Ποιο μάθημα σού άρεσε περισσότερο;** — Which lesson did you like best?

> [!tip]- Push it further and fork it
> - Classmates ask two follow-up questions after each talk.
> - Give the same talk again as if it's a year from now: Πέρσι…, φέτος…
> - Record the talk on your phone and keep it. It's a good benchmark to compare against next year.
>
> The swaps and twists from [[L26 Capstone and celebration-teacher|Lesson 26]] work here too. Change the ending each time you replay the scene.
