# Focus tips

Greekquent is built so the app holds the plan, the time and your place, and you only have to do the Greek. These tips come from research on ADHD, and they help everyone. Back to [[Today]].

> [!tip]- Start small
> The top of Today says one thing to do now and about how long it takes. Do that one thing. After a few days away it asks for just one line, and one line counts.

> [!tip]- Pick your moment
> Choose "after ___" once: after coffee, after the bus, after dinner. Doing it right after something you already do every day makes starting automatic. "Add to my calendar" gives you a daily nudge on Android or iPhone.

> [!tip]- Say it before you see it
> Guess first, out loud, then reveal. That moment of reaching for the word is what makes it stick. The reveal button waits a beat on purpose.

> [!tip]- Move
> Do the gesture every time. Stand up, walk, pace. Walk mode (in Today's side panel, under Focus) plays your Greek hands-free while you move.

> [!tip]- Not yet is progress
> Not yet still earns φόρα, and a day off only fades it a little. Nothing resets to zero, and there are no rankings.

> [!tip]- When things get noisy
> Calm mode stops the moving parts. Focus sound is a soft hiss (both are in Options, at the bottom of Class) that helps some people and bothers others, so try it and keep it only if it helps.

> [!tip]- Practice next to someone
> Working beside another person, even on a video call with both of you quiet, makes it easier to start and keep going. See [[Practice together]].

> [!tip]- Stop while it still feels good
> Five good minutes beat a long session you dread. The steps and dances on the φόρα card reward showing up, not marathons.
