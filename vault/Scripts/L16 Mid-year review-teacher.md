## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 4 new words on their printed list: σαν (57) · της (62) · η ταβέρνα (1414) · μαγειρεύω (1451)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'My family is from…'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Η οικογένειά μου είναι από…**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Η οικογένειά μου είναι από…** | My family is from… | Hug-circle with your arms, then point to the map. |
> > | **Την Κυριακή πάω στην εκκλησία.** | On Sunday I go to church. | Sign of the cross, then walk your fingers. |
> > | **Τι τρως;** | What do you eat? | Fingers to the mouth, palms up. |
> > | **Μιλάς ελληνικά;** | Do you speak Greek? | Talking-puppet hand toward the partner. |
> > | **Θέλω να μάθω να μαγειρεύω.** | I want to learn to cook. | Open a book, then stir a pot. |
> > | **σαν εσένα** | like you | Point at yourself, then the partner, and put two index fingers side by side. |
>
> **Setup**  
> **Station 1: interview questions**  
> T: Πώς σε λένε; Από πού είσαι;  
> T: Έχεις οικογένεια εδώ; Έχεις παιδιά;  
> T: Τι κάνεις το πρωί;  
> T: Πού πας την Κυριακή;  
> T: Τι τρως και τι πίνεις στην ταβέρνα;  
> T: Μπορείς την Τρίτη στις έξι;  
> T: Μιλάς ελληνικά;  
> **Station 2: reading card (read aloud, then answer 3 questions)**  
> Reading: Η Μαρία είναι από την Αμερική, αλλά η οικογένειά της είναι από την Κρήτη.  
> Reading: Την Κυριακή πάει στην εκκλησία με τη γιαγιά της. Μετά πάνε στη λαϊκή.  
> Reading: Η γιαγιά θέλει ντομάτες και ελιές. «Πόσο κάνουν οι ελιές;» λέει. «Πέντε ευρώ το κιλό.»  
> Reading: Το μεσημέρι τρώνε στο σπίτι. Η Μαρία λέει: «Θέλω να μάθω να μαγειρεύω σαν εσένα, γιαγιά!»  
> T: Από πού είναι η οικογένεια της Μαρίας; · Πού πάνε την Κυριακή; · Τι θέλει η γιαγιά;  
> **Rubric (1–3 each)**  
> **Exit ticket:** Each student says one thing they can do now: Μπορώ να ___ στα ελληνικά.  
>
> Full teacher transcript, with English explanations: [[Greekquent Year One Curriculum-teacher|Greekquent Year One Curriculum]], Lesson 16.
>
> **Recall 1 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three of today's phrases. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

## 3 · Main scene

**A day out: mid-year review scene**: One long scene that stitches together Lessons 1–15: making plans, the market, a lost phone, and the café.

**Familiarity:** 99% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** καφέδες

- A: **Γεια σου, Νίκο! Τι κάνεις;** — Hi, Nikos! How are you?
- B: **Καλά! Μπορείς σήμερα;** — Good! Can you get together today?
- A: **Σήμερα δεν μπορώ, έχω δουλειά. Αύριο στις δέκα;** — Today I can't, I have work. Tomorrow at ten?
- B: **Εντάξει. Πάμε στη λαϊκή;** — Okay. Shall we go to the market?
- A: **Ναι! Θέλω ντομάτες και ελιές.** — Yes! I want tomatoes and olives.
- B (next day, at the market): **Πόσο κάνουν οι ελιές;** — How much are the olives?
- Seller: **Πέντε ευρώ το κιλό.** — Five euros a kilo.
- B: **Ένα κιλό, παρακαλώ.** — A kilo, please.
- A: **Ωχ! Πού είναι το κινητό μου;** — Oh no! Where's my phone?
- B: **Δεν ξέρω… Δεν βλέπω το κινητό σου.** — I don't know… I don't see your phone.
- A: **Εδώ είναι! Στην τσάντα μου.** — Here it is! In my bag.
- B: **Πάμε για καφέ;** — Shall we get a coffee?
- A: **Πάμε!** — Let's go!
- Waiter: **Τι θέλετε;** — What would you like?
- A: **Δύο καφέδες, παρακαλώ.** — Two coffees, please.
- B: **Τι ώρα είναι;** — What time is it?
- A: **Είναι δώδεκα.** — It's twelve.
- B: **Ωχ, είναι αργά. Θέλω να πάω στο σπίτι.** — Oh no, it's late. I want to go home.
- A: **Τα λέμε, Νίκο!** — See you, Nikos!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Plan: σήμερα · αύριο στις δέκα · την Κυριακή
> - Place: στη λαϊκή · στο καφενείο · στη θάλασσα
> - Problem: Πού είναι το κινητό μου; · Δεν έχω ρέστα. · Δεν έχουμε…
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Μιλάτε αγγλικά;**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Draw one card from each earlier lesson's scene and blend them into the day out.
> - Oral check: each student does a 3-minute interview with the teacher, using the questions below.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (90, 60, then 45 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "How much are the olives?" · B: "A kilo, please." · C: "Two coffees, please."
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Each student says one thing they can do now: Μπορώ να ___ στα ελληνικά. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Re-read the reading card every day this week.
> - Pick 5 red words from your packet and make them yellow by next week.
>
> **Out in the world:** Record yourself, or say it to a friend: a two-minute talk in Greek about you, your family, and your day. Write the outline by hand first, with keywords only.
>
> **Watch-list:** Use the results to pair stronger and weaker students for Phase C.
