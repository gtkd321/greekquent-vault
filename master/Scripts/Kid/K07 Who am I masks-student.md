# Who am I? masks

**The game:** Paper-plate masks turn the kid into a character, and he introduces himself as that character.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Ποιος είσαι;** — Who are you?
- You: **Είμαι ο Spider-Man!** — I'm Spider-Man!
- Grown-up: **Είσαι από την Αμερική;** — Are you from America?
- You: **Ναι, είμαι από την Αμερική.** — Yes, I'm from America.
- You: **Είμαι η γιαγιά! Είμαι από την Ελλάδα.** — I'm Grandma! I'm from Greece.
- Everyone: **Καλά Χριστούγεννα!** — Merry Christmas!

## You are the teacher

Swap: now you are the teacher. Ask the grown-up the questions.

- You (teacher): **Ποιος είσαι;** — Who are you?
- You (teacher): **Είσαι από την Αμερική;** — Are you from America?
- Grown-up: answers, and sometimes gets it wrong on purpose. Catch it.
- You: **Όχι! Λάθος!** — No! Wrong!
- Grown-up: **Συγγνώμη!** — Sorry! (then says it right)
- You: **Μπράβο! Σωστά!** — Well done! That's right!

## Make it

The mask, plus a little name tag in Greek letters.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Ποιος είσαι;**: Peek around the mask, palms up.
- **Είμαι**: Hand flat on your chest.
- **ο Spider-Man**: Shoot a web from your wrist.
- **η γιαγιά**: Bend over a pretend cane.
- **από την**: Point far away, across the sea.
- **Καλά Χριστούγεννα!**: Arms up like a tree, and twinkle your fingers like lights.
