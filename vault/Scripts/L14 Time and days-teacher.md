## 2 · Warm-up

> [!example]- Warm-up script
> **Word list (1 min):** students black-mark today's 10 new words on their printed list: αργά (185) · μισός (376) · παρά (445) · νωρίς (604) · η Παρασκευή (1011) · η Δευτέρα (1182) · η Τρίτη (1389) · η Τετάρτη (1390) · η Πέμπτη (1391) · το τέταρτο (1399)  
>
> **Guess first (30 sec):** "How do you think you'd say 'What time is it?'?" Take two or three guesses, even wild ones, then teach **Τι ώρα είναι;**. Wrong guesses still help it stick.  
>
> > [!info]- Gestures for today's phrases
> > Students make each gesture themselves every time they say the phrase. Watching you do it is not enough.
> >
> > | Phrase | Meaning | Gesture |
> > |---|---|---|
> > | **Τι ώρα είναι;** | What time is it? | Tap your wrist, eyebrows up. |
> > | **Μπορείς την Τρίτη στις έξι;** | Can you on Tuesday at six? | Count two, then six, on your fingers with the palms facing you, eyebrows up. |
> > | **στις έξι και μισή** | at six thirty | Count six with the palms facing you, then cut the air in half. |
> > | **και τέταρτο** | quarter past | Draw a quarter circle on a pretend clock. |
> > | **Είναι νωρίς. / Είναι αργά.** | It's early. / It's late. | Yawn and stretch for νωρίς; tap the wrist anxiously for αργά. |
>
> **Step 1: the days**  
> T: Δευτέρα, Τρίτη, Τετάρτη, Πέμπτη, Παρασκευή, Σάββατο, Κυριακή. — Ss echo, then chant.  
> **Step 2: the time**  
> T: (moves the clock) Τι ώρα είναι; — Είναι πέντε. — Είναι πέντε και μισή. — Είναι πέντε και τέταρτο. — Είναι έξι παρά τέταρτο.  
> **Step 3: on a day, at a time**  
> Board: τη Δευτέρα · την Τρίτη · την Τετάρτη · την Πέμπτη · την Παρασκευή · το Σάββατο · την Κυριακή  
> Board: στη μία · στις δύο · στις τρεις · στις έξι και μισή  
> T: Την Τρίτη στις έξι. Το Σάββατο στις δέκα.  
> **Step 4: anchor recycling**  
> T: Μπορείς την Τρίτη στις έξι; — S1: Την Τρίτη δεν μπορώ. Την Πέμπτη μπορώ.  
> T: Είναι νωρίς. / Είναι αργά.  
> **Warm-up dialogue: finding a time**  
> A: Μπορείς την Τρίτη στις έξι;  
> B: Την Τρίτη δεν μπορώ, έχω δουλειά. Την Πέμπτη;  
> A: Την Πέμπτη μπορώ. Τι ώρα;  
> B: Στις εφτά και μισή.  
> A: Είναι λίγο αργά. Στις εφτά;  
> B: Εντάξει, στις εφτά. Τα λέμε την Πέμπτη!  
> **Exit ticket:** Say what time it is now, and one day and time you can meet.  
>

>
> **Recall 1 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three of today's phrases. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

## 3 · Main scene

**What time? Which day?**: Two friends check the time and pick a day and time to meet.

**Familiarity:** 100% of the words are known after the warm-up · **New in this scene:** none

- A: **Τι ώρα είναι;** — What time is it?
- B: **Είναι έξι και μισή.** — It's six thirty.
- A: **Ωχ, είναι αργά!** — Oh no, it's late!
- B: **Γιατί;** — Why?
- A: **Έχω δουλειά στις εφτά!** — I have work at seven!
- B: **Α, εντάξει. Μπορείς την Τρίτη στις έξι; Θέλω να πάμε για καφέ.** — Oh, okay. Can you do Tuesday at six? I want to go for coffee.
- A: **Την Τρίτη δεν μπορώ. Την Τετάρτη μπορώ.** — I can't on Tuesday. I can on Wednesday.
- B: **Την Τετάρτη στις έξι και τέταρτο;** — Wednesday at six fifteen?
- A: **Είναι νωρίς για μένα. Στις εφτά;** — That's early for me. At seven?
- B: **Εντάξει, την Τετάρτη στις εφτά.** — Okay, Wednesday at seven.
- A: **Τέλεια, τα λέμε!** — Perfect, see you then!

## 4 · Fork it

> [!tip]- Swaps, twists, and replays
> Papers face-down for every replay. Peek only when stuck, then put the paper back.
>
> **Swap one slot per replay**
>
> - Day: Δευτέρα · Τρίτη · Τετάρτη · Πέμπτη · Παρασκευή · Σάββατο · Κυριακή
> - Time: στις έξι · στις έξι και τέταρτο · στις έξι και μισή · στις επτά παρά τέταρτο
> - Reaction: Είναι νωρίς. · Είναι αργά. · Τέλεια!
> - Mix-in: work one line from today's review into a replay, for example **Θα ήθελα…**
>
> **Twists**
>
> - Paper clocks: set a time, and your partner has to say it.
> - Busy calendars: each student gets a calendar card with some times blocked out, and they have to find a free slot together.
>
> **Every lesson**
>
> - Role swap: A and B trade parts.
> - Person shift: retell it about someone else (έχει, έχουμε, έχουν).
> - Retell round (last replay): each student retells the scene in Greek to a new partner three times, with less time each time (90, 60, then 45 seconds). The listener only listens.
> - Tile round (can replace the slot-swap replay): give the English, and pairs build the sentence from their tiles on the sentence mat. Then flip it to a question with ;, add δεν, swap one tile, and write the last version by hand. A: "What time is it?" · B: "Oh no, it's late!" · C: "Wednesday at six fifteen?"
>
> Three replays: first straight, then with one slot swapped, then the retell round. Change only one thing per round.
>
> **Recall 2 (1 min):** papers face-down. Give the English for three warm-up phrases you didn't use in Recall 1. Students answer in Greek, with the gesture.

> [!note]- Exit ticket, homework, watch-list
> **Recall 3 · Exit ticket:** Say what time it is now, and one day and time you can meet. Papers away. Students write it by hand on an index card, then say it. They keep the cards, and the red ones come back next week.
>
> **Homework (10 min a day)**
> - Each time you check your phone, say the time in Greek.
> - Write your week's schedule in Greek: Τη Δευτέρα στις… .
>
> **Out in the world:** Put a paper weekly planner on the fridge and write the days and one time for each day in Greek. Say the time in Greek each time you check a clock.
>
> **Watch-list:** Παρασκευή is pa-ras-ke-VEE. Πέμπτη is PEM-ptee. Students say τρία for three o'clock; it's τρεις. Note τη Δευτέρα (no ν) vs. την Τρίτη.
