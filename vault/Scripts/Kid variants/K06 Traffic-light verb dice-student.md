# Traffic-light verb dice

**The game:** Roll a homemade die for the verb, then draw a color for the kind of sentence, using your red/yellow/green colors.

## Say it

- You (green): **Έχω καφέ.** — I have coffee.
- You (red): **Δεν θέλω ψωμί.** — I don't want bread.
- You (yellow): **Μπορείς αύριο;** — Can you tomorrow?
- You (yellow): **Βλέπεις τη θάλασσα;** — Do you see the sea?
- Grown-up: **Μπράβο!** — Well done!

## Make it

Build the die together and let the kid draw the four pictures. The colors match the laminated packet, so the game teaches the system too.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Θέλεις έναν καφέ;**: Mime a tiny Greek coffee cup, little finger out.
- **Καλά, εσύ;**: Thumbs up, then a palm-up hand toward the partner.
- **Δεν έχω ώρα σήμερα.**: Tap your wrist, then shake your head.
- **Αύριο μπορείς;**: Roll a hand forward, then point at the partner.
- **Τέλεια!**: Kiss your fingertips.
- **Τα λέμε αύριο.**: Small fingers-together wave while rolling the hand forward.
- **Ξέρεις; – Δεν ξέρω.**: Tap your temple, then shrug.
- **στο καφενείο**: Mime moving a backgammon (τάβλι) piece on a board.
