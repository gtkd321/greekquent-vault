# Kid variant · Lesson 17: Fortune teller

Play version of [[L17 Weekend plans-teacher|Lesson 17: Weekend plans]] · [[K17 Fortune teller-student|Kid page]] · all variants in [[01 Lesson Scripts]]

**The idea:** A folded-paper fortune teller (cootie catcher) predicts weekend plans in the future tense.

**You'll need:** a square of paper, markers

## How to play

1. Fold the fortune teller together. Under each flap, write or draw a plan.
2. Kid picks a number; open and close it; read the fortune.
3. Ask each other about the weekend.

## Lines

- T: **Τι θα κάνεις το Σαββατοκύριακο;** — What will you do this weekend?
- K: **Θα πάω στο πάρκο!** — I'll go to the park!
- K: **Θα δω μια ταινία.** — I'll watch a movie.
- K: **Θα παίξω με τους φίλους.** — I'll play with my friends.
- T: **Θα έχεις ώρα;** — Will you have time?

## Make it

The fortune teller, colored on the outside with drawings of each plan inside.

> [!note]- For adults, and teacher note
> **For adults:** Plan a real weekend together in Greek, with times and places.
