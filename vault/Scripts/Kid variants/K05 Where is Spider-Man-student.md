# Where is Spider-Man?

**The game:** Your starter: move a toy around the table and chair, and the kid says where it is. Then he hides it and you have to find it.

## Say it

- Grown-up: **Πού είναι ο Spider-Man;** — Where is Spider-Man?
- You: **Ο Spider-Man είναι πάνω στο τραπέζι.** — Spider-Man is on the table.
- You: **Ο Spider-Man είναι κάτω από το τραπέζι.** — Spider-Man is under the table.
- You: **Ο Spider-Man είναι πάνω στην καρέκλα.** — Spider-Man is on the chair.
- You: **Ο Spider-Man είναι πάνω από την καρέκλα.** — Spider-Man is above the chair (in the air).
- You: **Είναι μέσα στο κουτί.** — He's in the box.
- You: **Είναι πίσω από την πόρτα.** — He's behind the door.
- You: **Είναι δίπλα στο βιβλίο.** — He's next to the book.
- Grown-up: **Δεν βλέπω τον Spider-Man! Πού είναι;** — I can't see Spider-Man! Where is he?
- You: **Δεν ξέρω!** — I don't know!
- Grown-up: **Εδώ είναι!** — Here he is!

## Make it

The kid draws a simple map of the room and sticks a small Spider-Man sticker wherever the toy was found.

## Do the moves

Do the move every time you say it.

- **Δεν έχω…**: Turn out empty pockets or open empty hands.
- **Πού είναι το κινητό μου;**: Pat your pockets, looking around.
- **Δεν βλέπω…**: Hand over your eyes, head turning.
- **Εδώ είναι!**: Point down hard at the spot and smile.
- **Γιατί δεν μπορείς;**: Both palms up, head tilted to the side.
- **Έχω δουλειά.**: Mime typing fast.
- **Μπορώ να δω;**: Point at your eye, then at the object, eyebrows up.
- **Πόσο κάνει;**: Rub thumb and fingers together.
- **Δύο ευρώ.**: Tap two fingers on the counter, one coin at a time.
- **Δεν ξέρω.**: Shrug with palms up.
